# Retro — YYYY-MM-DD

The retro interrogates the session; `/handoff` routes what it finds. Each section below names
where its contents land, and `/handoff` reads those names here rather than carrying a copy of
the mapping — a copy drifts from what it copied.

## Open questions
Every question this session opened and did not close: forks resolved without asking, things the
owner raised that were stepped past, decisions taken quietly. One line each, and every line
carries a verdict — `answered:` / `deferred:` naming where it is written / `could-not-close:`
with why.
**The retro does not end while a line here has no verdict.** A deferral the owner states is a
resolution. Silence is not. An owner who skips the conversation has deferred them all, which is
also a resolution — write that down as the verdict.
→ handoff: wherever each answer belongs. This section is a gate, not a payload.

## Could not close
The `could-not-close:` lines again, on their own, so that they are a section rather than a
sentence inside one. Write "none" when there are none.
A retro that ends claiming everything is resolved, having dropped one, is the failure this whole
step exists to remove — and a missing section is caught while a missing line inside one is not.
→ handoff: Feedback to the librarian

## Diverged
Where the session went against a written rule — in `CLAUDE.md`, in the code, or in the vault.
What the rule said, what was done instead, and why it looked unnecessary at the time. That last
clause is the point: every recorded divergence in this project began exactly there.
→ handoff: Decisions

## Fell out silently
Scope that left the session without anyone deciding it. Each becomes either a deferral the owner
ratifies, or work that is simply not done — and saying which is the retro's job, not the next
session's.
→ handoff: Deferred, or State

## Went wrong
Failures during execution. For each one: **was it already broken before this session?** Verify
it, never assume — the answer decides who owns the fix, and assuming has gone both ways here.
→ handoff: Lessons, or Feedback to the librarian

## Left fragile
What is left behind that will bite whoever touches it next, and what would make it safe.
→ handoff: Deferred, or Lessons

## Contradicted itself
Where the session's own actions disagreed with each other, or with what it said earlier.
→ handoff: Lessons

## Worth keeping
What worked, with the incident behind it. Without an incident this is self-praise and has
nowhere to go — write "none".
→ handoff: Lessons
