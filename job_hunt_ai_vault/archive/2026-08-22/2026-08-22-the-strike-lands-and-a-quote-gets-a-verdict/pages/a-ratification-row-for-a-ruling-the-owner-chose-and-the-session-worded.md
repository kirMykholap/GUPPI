# The ratification step found a hole in itself on its first run

**The table admits two shapes** — *he said it*, written verbatim in Russian under `owner:`, and
*you understood it*, written in the session's own voice and never signed
([[the-retro-ratifies-every-line-before-it-is-attributed-to-the-owner]]).

**Its first run found a shape that is neither.** Nine rulings of 2026-08-21 were made by **the owner
choosing among options the session had authored**. The decision is unambiguously his and the wording
is unambiguously the session's.

**It is more dangerous than either shape in the table.** An explicit yes makes the line read as
stronger than a quotation, while every word in it was written by the party that wanted the answer
([[a-question-shaped-like-a-proposal-was-executed-as-a-ratified-design]]).

# What it would take

**A row in the table in `.claude/skills/retro/SKILL.md`**, saying that such a ruling is written as
*"the owner chose X from the options put to him"* and **never in quotation marks**.

**Nothing else.** The compilation side already behaves this way where a librarian is told to: the
ruling that replaced the struck hook rule is compiled as a decision and carries no quote block
([[a-hook-refuses-on-a-measured-condition-a-reliable-measurement-and-a-refusal-that-says-how-to-clear-it]]).

# It was put to the owner and not answered

**So the table stands as built, and this page is the record that the gap is known rather than
missed.** A line he did not answer is `could-not-close:`, and this is that rule applied to a question
about the rule itself ([[a-question-asked-is-not-a-question-answered]]).

**What it costs while it stands:** a ruling of this shape is written under whichever of the two rows
the session picks — signed as his words if it picks the first, which is the failure the whole step
exists to prevent ([[a-paraphrase-signed-owner-becomes-a-quote-and-is-reasoned-from-as-his-word]]).

Retired with `superseded_by:` the day it is built or refused

# Retired 2026-08-22 — it was built, and nothing on it lives only here

**The owner ratified the third shape on the day after this page was written**, on his own question at
the retro ([[the-retro-records-a-ruling-chosen-from-a-menu-and-ratifies-decisions-not-utterances]]).

**Checked against the successor before the move**, which is what this store requires of a retirement
rather than the retirement itself ([[a-built-proposal-retires-once-nothing-on-it-lives-only-there]]):

| what lived here | where it lives now |
|---|---|
| the table admits two shapes and a ruling from a menu is neither | the successor's opening section |
| the shape is more dangerous than either, because an explicit yes reads as stronger than a quotation | the successor, under the ground the owner gave for it — the wording was on his screen |
| the row that would be added to `.claude/skills/retro/SKILL.md` | built, in that file |
| the defect that the alternatives are the session's | the successor, plus the divergence its first use produced ([[a-multi-select-whose-options-contradicted-each-other-was-read-as-a-union]]) |
| the note that it was put to him and not answered | superseded — he answered it |

**The measurement that decided it is the successor's, not this page's**: of ten owner utterances in
that session, two were his own words and all eight rulings arrived as menu selections.
