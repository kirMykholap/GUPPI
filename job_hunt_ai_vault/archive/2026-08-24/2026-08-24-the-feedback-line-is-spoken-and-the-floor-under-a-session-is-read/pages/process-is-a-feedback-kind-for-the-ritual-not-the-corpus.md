# Adopted by the owner on 2026-08-17

The feedback channel had three kinds — `miss:`, `lint:`, `measure:`
([[vault-feedback-file]]). **`process:` joins them: a finding about the closing ritual or the
harness itself, rather than about the corpus.**

| kind | what it reports |
|---|---|
| `miss:` | the vault failed to supply something, and where that surfaced |
| `lint:` | a defect in the corpus — a stale claim, a contradiction, a copied list |
| `measure:` | a number, with what it does and does not mean |
| `process:` | the ritual or the harness failed, independently of what the corpus holds |

## It arrived before it was agreed, and that was the right order

Two lines had already been filed under `process:` by a session that had no such kind to file them
under, and the previous ingest transferred them verbatim while raising the vocabulary as an alarm —
a librarian transfers a line under the kind it was written with and does not reclassify it.

**owner:** adopted. **It was not carelessness.** Both lines were about the ritual: one about a queue
only the owner can drain, one about the closing ritual's own off-by-one
([[a-queue-only-one-party-can-drain-is-unbounded]],
[[the-closing-ritual-produces-what-the-journal-cannot-hold]]). None of the other three kinds covers
that, and filing them as `miss:` would have said the corpus lacked something it never claimed to
hold.

## Where the definition lives

**`.superpowers/sdd/vault-feedback.md` carries the writing session's copy**, defined there on
2026-08-17. **`log.md` carries the librarian's**, and the wording matches. Two copies of a
four-line vocabulary in two files with different owners is the smallest instance of the problem the
marked regions exist for ([[marked-regions-and-the-prose-outside-them]]) — a rule page and one
region each would remove it, and neither file is marked up yet.

## Retired 2026-08-24: `process:` is no longer a kind a session writes

**The table above is false from that date.** `process:` and `lint:` became the librarian's own words
in `log.md`, and a session writes `miss:`, `measure:`, `decision:` and `harness:`
([[lint-and-process-stop-being-session-kinds-and-decision-joins]]).

**The ground was a count rather than a judgement.** Of the 254 feedback lines sessions had written,
**58 were decisions with no kind of their own, and 21 of those were filed under `process:`** —
including direct rulings by the owner. The kind this page adopted had become the place a decision
went when the vocabulary had nowhere else to put it.

**What this page got right is untouched and is why the retirement is not a reversal.** A finding
about the ritual is not a finding about the corpus, and filing one as `miss:` would say the vault
lacked something it never claimed to hold. The successor keeps the distinction and moves the pen:
the librarian still writes `process:` lines, in `log.md`, about the ritual.
