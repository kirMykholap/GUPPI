# What the session did, four times, and corrected four times

**Each is a measurement arriving after a claim, and the pattern is what is worth keeping** rather
than any of the individual slips.

| the claim, as made | the measurement, when it arrived |
|---|---|
| *"the probe unblocked the `.claude/rules/` move"* | the runbook names that probe as the blocker and it passed — but the compaction probe's own stated consequence gates the move as well. The claim was **narrower than the truth** ([[what-reaches-a-subagent-a-scoped-rule-does-and-an-html-comment-does-not]]) |
| the tag split was sold to the owner as beating a density counter | **the counter does not exist here.** The neighbouring tags in that folder stand at 54, 60 and 78 against a threshold of ten, and an honest split prints an advisory immediately. He had already agreed, on trust, when the figure arrived; it was corrected to him **before anything was executed** ([[the-schema-tag-splits-into-db-schema-and-record-schema]]) |
| a corpus maximum of *145 characters* quoted against a new 175 | **an instrument difference, not a corpus figure.** 145 counted the title's quotation marks; the comparable reading is 143 ([[the-length-of-a-title-is-unbounded-and-no-check-measures-it]], [[the-evidence-was-not-wrong-the-instrument-was-too-coarse]]) |
| the per-folder schema priced at *"one verdict, one selftest case"* when the owner agreed to it | it is S2's first acceptance criterion, and was **re-priced in two readings before a line of code was written** ([[the-gate-learns-the-legal-key-set-from-a-yaml-per-folder]]) |

# What separates this from a lesson about carelessness

**Every one of them was caught by the party that made it, in the same session, before anything rested
on it.** Two were corrected to the owner after he had already agreed — which is the case that matters,
because agreement is the point at which a wrong figure stops being private.

**The mechanism is not vigilance. It is that a claim gets measured at all.** Each of these four was
made in the ordinary way — a sentence in a proposal, a number in a comparison — and each was measured
later only because the work went on to touch the thing the claim was about. **A claim that nothing
later touches is never measured**, and this project has that on the record as figures travelling
through several pages with nobody counting
([[a-page-that-says-measured-can-carry-a-figure-nobody-measured]],
[[an-unsourced-number-is-dropped-not-chosen]]).

# The rule it argues for, which is cheaper than checking every claim

**A figure quoted to the owner is quoted with its instrument.** Three of the four above are figure
errors and two of the three were instrument errors rather than arithmetic — a different reading of the
same thing ([[a-measurement-agreeing-with-no-expectation-needs-a-different-instrument]]). Naming the
command that produced a number is what makes it correctable by anyone, later, which is the same
principle every acceptance criterion in `sprint.md` already runs on.

**And a correction goes to the owner even after he has agreed.** The tag split is the instance: the
agreement was given on a premise that turned out to be false, the execution had not started, and
telling him cost one turn. **The alternative is that the record of why he agreed is wrong forever**
([[chat-is-not-a-channel-that-survives-the-session]]).

# Two more on 2026-08-21, and both were in briefs written to serve the rule they contradicted

- **The slug count was given as 423 moving by four; it moved by three.** A rule page carries no
  `authority:`, so counting a rule page as a slug-bearer is arithmetic that contradicts the very rule
  the brief was about ([[authority-beside-governs-is-forbidden-on-any-page]]).
- **"About thirty deferrals" came from a grep over titles counting `rejected` and `declined`, which
  mark prohibitions.** The real figure, once the field was filled, is **18**
  ([[a-decision-declares-whether-it-binds]]). A count of a category taken from the words in titles
  measures the vocabulary, not the category
  ([[a-grep-that-prints-one-line-is-a-report-about-the-first-match-and-not-a-count]]).

**Both were corrected before anything was built on them, which is the property this page is about.**
And both have the same tell as the four above: the number was produced by an instrument chosen for
being at hand rather than for answering the question asked.
