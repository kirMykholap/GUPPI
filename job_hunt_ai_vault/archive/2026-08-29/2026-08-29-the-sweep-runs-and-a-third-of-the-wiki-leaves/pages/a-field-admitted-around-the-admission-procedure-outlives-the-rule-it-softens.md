# The incident, found by a retrospective a day later

**`quantity:` was introduced on 2026-08-17, inside the same commit that wrote rule 10**, and it was
filed in that session's journal under *Entities → Appeared* — a line reading *"a frontmatter field,
on nineteen pages"*, beside the directories and hooks that had also appeared.

**Everything a decision leaves behind is missing.** No `owner:` line anywhere touches it. No
`decisions/` page carries an `authority:` for it. There is no `decided:` date. It was compiled, in
good faith, into the two pages describing the rule it softened
([[the-title-check-asks-and-does-not-judge]],
[[a-title-names-what-is-counted-instead-of-counting-it]]) — because a unit filed as an entity is
compiled as an entity ([[an-intent-note-compiled-as-testimony-describes-things-nobody-built]]).

**On that same day the owner refused a different new field**, under a written admission procedure,
on the ground that a field nobody writes and nobody reads becomes a per-page invention
([[a-field-earns-its-place-by-being-read-or-by-being-a-checksum]],
[[the-frontmatter-core-is-fixed-and-updated-joins-it]]). One field went through the procedure and
was refused; the other did not go through it and shipped.

**What it softened had been ruled unconditional twice**, `basis: human`, and the owner had refused
two specific exception classes on the way — counts bound to a date, and design invariants. **Both
are alive in `quantity:` values today.** The field readmitted, page by page and judged by nobody,
exactly the exceptions the rule had refused in general.

# The instrument was installed by its own author, and nobody read it

Rule 10's comment names the alarm: **if the declarations ever much outnumber the retitles, the
boundary is drawn in the wrong place** — countable with `grep -c "^quantity:" wiki/*/*.md`.

**Measured at the retrospective: 24 declarations against 23 retitles, and 5 against 5 since the rule
became checkable.** The instrument was at parity, in the file, for a day. It took the owner's own
suspicion to look ([[an-instrument-that-contains-its-own-answers-can-be-gamed]],
[[a-deferred-check-must-be-run-when-its-condition-arrives]]).

# What to take from it

- **A field is a decision.** It changes what every page may assert, and it is admitted the way a
  decision is admitted or it is not admitted.
- **The section a claim is filed under decides what it becomes.** *Entities → Appeared* means *this
  exists now*; a reader of the compiled corpus cannot recover the fact that nobody chose it
  ([[the-handoff-template-maps-onto-the-categories]]).
- **The dangerous shape is the softener shipped in the same commit as the rule.** It looks like part
  of the rule, it is reviewed as part of the rule, and it is the part of the rule that was never
  agreed.
