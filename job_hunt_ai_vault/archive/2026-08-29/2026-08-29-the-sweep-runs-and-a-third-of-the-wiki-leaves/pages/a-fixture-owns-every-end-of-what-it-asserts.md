# The mechanism the fixture needed

**`plant_idea_pair` plants the decision beside the proposal**, rather than pointing the proposal at
a real decision in the corpus. A real target's `binding:` is a librarian's to change tomorrow, and
the case asserts something about that value — so a case built on it would break for a reason it is
not about ([[the-selftest-works-in-its-own-fixture-and-never-in-the-corpus]],
[[a-rule-over-every-page-makes-every-test-fragile]]).

# The half that had never been met before

**It also registers both slugs in `authority.md`, which no other planter in that file has ever had
to do.** Every existing planter plants a page carrying `governs:`, or a concept where `authority:`
and `governs:` are forbidden together — so none had ever needed an `authority:` slug at all.

**Rule 13 compares the register against the tree in both directions**, so the first version of the
fixture reddened the tree for a reason the case was not about
([[retiring-a-page-removes-its-slug-from-the-authority-register]]).

# What this says about the suite rather than about one fixture

**A rule can be a year old and have no fixture that has ever satisfied it.** The suite is a mutation
test of the gate, and a rule nothing plants a subject for is a rule the suite never exercises in the
positive direction ([[the-selftest-is-a-mutation-test-of-the-gate]]).

**A planter is a claim about what a legal page looks like.** When it plants half of one, the case
tests the half.
