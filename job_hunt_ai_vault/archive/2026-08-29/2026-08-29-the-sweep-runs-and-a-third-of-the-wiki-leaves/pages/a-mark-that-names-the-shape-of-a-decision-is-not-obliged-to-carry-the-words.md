# The incident, and its date is the whole of it

**Eight of fourteen `decision:` marks signed *the owner said it* carried the session's English
paraphrase rather than his words** — recorded on the very day the ratification sweep against exactly
that defect was completed.

**His words were in the transcript the whole time**, and an extract recovered all eight in a minute.

# The diagnosis

**The mark channel learned to name the *shape* of a decision on 2026-08-24** — the owner said it,
the owner selected it, the session understood it — **and was never obliged to carry the *words*.**
The gap between those two obligations is the eight lines.

**Naming the shape is what makes the attribution honest at the ingest.** It does not make the
sentence his, and a paraphrase signed with a shape reads as a quotation to the next reader
([[a-paraphrase-signed-owner-becomes-a-quote-and-is-reasoned-from-as-his-word]]).

# Why the repair belongs in the channel and not in the ingest

**The words exist at the moment the mark is written and are expensive to recover afterwards.** By
the time the librarian reads the line, the transcript is a separate artefact and the mark is the
only thing in front of it.

**It is queued as a harness change rather than fixed here**, in the journal's own `Harness` section.

# Amendment, 2026-08-25 — the words were recoverable and the owner chose not to restore them

**The sentence above stands: an extract recovered all eight in a minute.** What changed is what
follows from it.

**The recovered words are written nowhere.** The journal of that session carries the finding, `log.md`
carries the paraphrases, and the transcript is not an artefact of this repository.

**Restoring them was offered as an option and refused.** The owner chose to strip the signature where
the words are not his and leave the claim standing, over recovering his Russian from the transcripts
([[the-owner-signature-is-removed-where-the-words-are-not-his]]).

**So retroactive repair here is marking a mark false, never restoring it** — and that is a decision of
his rather than a limit of the instrument. A page or a session reading this must not conclude that the
words could not be recovered.
