# The incident

**A plan promised about eleven minutes off the suite from cheapening the injector.** The paired
measurement gives 1.9 s off a 17.2 s gate — three to four minutes across the suite, wrong by a
factor of about three.

**Both figures the projection rested on had been taken on a contended machine**
([[a-measurement-taken-while-other-work-runs-against-the-same-disk-measures-nothing]]).

# The part that makes it a lesson rather than an error

**The session spent that day arguing that a number must be printed rather than maintained**, and put
a maintained number with no instrument behind it into the plan it wrote in the same hour.

**A projection is a number nobody can check**, and it is read by the owner as a promise. The rule
this store already has about registers applies to it exactly: the value that cannot be re-derived is
the value that goes false without saying so
([[a-printed-register-cannot-drift-and-a-maintained-one-always-has]]).

# What to publish instead

**The measurement that exists, its conditions, and what is not known.** *The injector is 43% cheaper
on five back-to-back readings; what that does to the suite is not measured* is a smaller claim and a
true one.
