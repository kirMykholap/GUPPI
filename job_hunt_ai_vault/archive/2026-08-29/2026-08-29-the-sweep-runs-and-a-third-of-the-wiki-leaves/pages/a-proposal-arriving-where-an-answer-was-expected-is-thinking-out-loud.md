# The tell was available at the time, and it was visible

**Three signs, all present before a line of the plan was written:**

- **he had interrupted twice with clarifying questions** — someone deciding between options is not
  still asking what the options are;
- **he had selected none of the offered options**, and the answer he gave was not one of them;
- **he phrased his own idea as a question** — *«а что если …»*.

**None of these needs interpretation.** They are properties of the turn as it arrived.

# Why the mistake is easy and structural

**The tool that asks a question returns his text under the label of an *answer*.** The interface
supplies a ratification the conversation did not, and the reader inherits it without noticing that
it was the shape of the transport rather than a word he said
([[a-question-shaped-like-a-proposal-was-executed-as-a-ratified-design]]).

**It compounds with the next gate.** An unratified design that reaches a plan is approved as part of
two hundred lines, and the moment when saying *no* was cheap has passed silently.

# What it costs when read wrong, both ways

**The design ships without a yes**, and whether it stays is then a question nobody scheduled
([[the-pre-task-ledger-holds-one-session-and-rotates-at-startup]] carries exactly that open item).

**And the question he was actually asking goes unanswered.** His was *where are the rows kept, and
does anything keep them*; it was folded into a design and he had to ask a second time.

**The neighbouring failure is ratification taken after the fact rather than before**
([[the-yaml-pin-was-written-before-the-owner-ratified-it]]): there the owner said yes when finally
asked, which is the ordinary outcome and the reason asking is cheap.

**The repair is one turn**: bring the changed design back on its own for a yes or a no, before it is
written into a plan. It is the session's proposal and it has not been ruled on.

# The other half of the same problem: two opposite readings of his silence, one hour apart — 2026-08-21

**"Unless you object" was taken as consent. «окей, продолжаем» was taken as insufficient.** Both
readings are defensible; **both cannot be the rule**, and the session applied them within an hour of
each other.

> **owner:** «да, это был не конкретный ответ. Будем работать дальше, чтобы понимать что делать в
> таких ситуациях.»

**So the question is open by his own ruling, and it is the mirror of this page's subject.** This page
says a proposal arriving where an answer was expected is not a decision; the same difficulty runs the
other way — an *absence* of objection is not a decision either, and neither is a general assent to
carrying on. **What is not yet decided is which of the two readings is the default**, and until it is,
the safe direction is the one this store already takes with an absent `binding:` value: the stricter
reading, which costs a question ([[a-decision-declares-whether-it-binds]]).
