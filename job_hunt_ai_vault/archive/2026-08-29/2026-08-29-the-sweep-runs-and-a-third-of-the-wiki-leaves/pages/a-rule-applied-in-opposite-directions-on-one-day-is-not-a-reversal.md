# What happened on 2026-08-20

**`/pre-task` left the repository's `CLAUDE.md` for a hook message**
([[the-pre-task-rule-leaves-claude-md-for-a-hook-message]]) and **the parallel-read rule entered
it** ([[dispatch-librarians-in-parallel-only-for-reading]]) — in the same day, in the same ingest,
into the same file.

**Both are decided by the same criterion**
([[core-is-what-bootstraps-the-asking-and-what-is-irreversible-if-missed]]):

- **`/pre-task` leaves** on the owner's ruling that delivery which is reliably ignored is not
  delivery — a change to that criterion, taken knowingly, and not an exemption from it.
- **The parallel-read rule enters** on the criterion's other half, *irreversible if missed*: a lost
  update to a register leaves no trace in a diff, a log or the gate
  ([[parallel-librarians-break-the-one-writer-property]]).

# Why the record has to carry the reason and not the coincidence

**A later reader finds the two adjacent in `git log` and in this store**, and the obvious inference
is that the project reversed itself or that nobody was holding the criterion. Neither is true, and
nothing in the diff distinguishes the case.

**The general form: a rule that is applied in opposite directions on one day is evidence the rule is
working**, provided the applications are written down beside their inputs. Without that, the pair
reads as noise and the criterion loses the authority it just earned — the same reason this store
records the rejected alternative on every decision rather than only the choice.
