# What happened, 2026-08-26

**The session stopped writing `VAULT-FEEDBACK` blocks the moment the retro began.** Through **eight
exchanges** carrying **twenty-one decisions, four measurements and three of its own errors**, not
one block was filed.

**The owner caught it, and says it is the second occurrence.**

# The rule it breaks

**The block is written in the same turn as the thing worth recording**
([[speak-the-feedback-in-the-turn-and-the-hook-files-it]],
[[feedback-is-recorded-in-the-turn-it-is-noticed]]). Everything filed at the retro was reconstructed
afterwards instead.

**Its own extract for the day: 4 blocks filed across 65 replies**, all of them before the retro.

# Why it looked unnecessary at the time, and why that is the wrong shape to trust

**A retro produces a written artefact of its own, so the recording felt already done.** It is not:
`.superpowers/sdd/retro.md` holds what has been resolved, and the feedback channel holds what was
observed in the turn it happened
([[the-retro-writes-its-own-transit-file]]).

**The densest part of a session for rulings is the part the channel went silent on**, which is the
exact inverse of what it is for.

# The harness half, and it is where the repair belongs

**`/retro` carries no instruction to file a block**, and it is the one ritual where every exchange
produces a decision. The skill's steps 3, 4 and 5 all generate ratifiable lines and none of them
says to mark.

**The owner ruled that filing the block is a mandatory procedure at the retro.** The journal records
that ruling in the session's own English rather than in his words, so it is written here in the
session's voice and is not signed as a quotation
([[a-block-attributed-to-the-owner-is-written-in-his-own-russian]]).

**That instruction is not written into the skill yet**, and until it is, the same silence is one
session away ([[knowing-a-rule-is-not-a-mechanism]]).

# The rejected alternative

**Obey the rule and file each block in its turn**, which is what the rule already says. There was no
competing consideration — the failure is not a trade-off recorded here, it is an omission.

# A third occurrence, in the retro that ordered the repair — 2026-08-26

**Nine consecutive replies with no `VAULT-FEEDBACK` block**, which is worse than the eight-exchange
run that produced the owner's ruling. **The owner caught it again**, as he had the time before.

**The ruling that came out of the second occurrence had become item 14 of S3 and was never built**,
and this page's own closing line — *"until it is, the same silence is one session away"* — was correct
to the session.

**Both ordered shapes exist as of that day and neither is a per-turn mechanism**: a short demand at
the head of `.claude/hooks/remind-the-smoke.ps1`, which fires once at the retro's start, and a rendered
rule read once at invocation ([[every-turn-of-a-retro-files-a-feedback-block]]).

**So the count of occurrences is not yet evidence that the repair works.** The failure is a channel
that goes quiet in exchange five of eight, and what would close that is undesigned.
