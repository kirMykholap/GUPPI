# The ratification, 2026-08-23

**The text was read to him verbatim and he signed it in his own words.**

> **owner: да, это мое решение. Подписываюсь**

What he was shown is the paragraph exactly as `CLAUDE.md` carries it and as
[[ask-before-weakening-or-working-around-a-rule]] renders it, both sentences together:

> Ask before you weaken, remove or work around any rule — here, in the code, or in the vault.
> Every recorded divergence in this project began with the rule looking unnecessary.

**He was told what he was being asked before he answered**: that the rule had never been ruled,
that a session had written it into the file rather than he, and that the same shape had already
been struck once in this store. **Both directions were on the table** — ratify it, or strike it as
never-ruled — and he took the first.

**It binds as a `prohibition`.** Weakening, removing or working around a rule is not a session's
call in any of the three places the sentence names, and lifting it is a conversation with him. That
is what the field meant before today and what it means now; what changed is that it is now his.

# The rule predates its own decision by nine days, and it was enforced the whole time

**Written into `CLAUDE.md` on 2026-08-14, decided on 2026-08-23.** `git log -S "Ask before you
weaken" -- CLAUDE.md` returns exactly one commit, `36e9e4c` of 2026-08-14 — the shortening cut —
and the page recording that cut, [[claude-md-is-a-map-and-four-rules]], accounts for the paragraph
nowhere: its table of the rules it chose does not contain it, its inventory of what the file became
does not list it, and the journal of that day does not mention weakening a rule at all.

**So this is a retroactive ratification and not a decision that produced a rule**, and the
distinction is the record rather than a nicety. `decided: 2026-08-23` is the date the ruling was
given, and every other date around this rule is earlier: the paragraph entered `CLAUDE.md` on
2026-08-14, and by 2026-08-17 it had been lifted out into a rule page — `created: 2026-08-17`
there — and was being rendered back into the file every agent reads. **Writing this page as though
the rule followed the ruling would falsify nine days of the store's own history**, days in which
the rule was cited, obeyed, and used to settle collisions between other rules.

**It was not idle in those days.** The pages citing it are read from
`rg -l ask-before-weakening-or-working-around-a-rule` rather than counted here, because a figure
written down is the copy that drifts ([[a-drifting-list-is-repaired-by-deleting-it-not-extending-it]]).
Four of them —
[[rules-becomes-a-type-of-its-own]],
[[the-suite-never-runs-against-the-production-database]],
[[an-ast-boundary-test-is-fixed-at-its-cause-not-in-the-test]] and
[[our-javascript-does-not-choose-which-rows-are-on-screen]] — in the same
`RESOLVED BY THE OWNER` note, where it is the reason a superseded alarm was marked rather than
deleted. It is also one of the rules [[every-rule-in-wiki-rules-owes-a-mechanical-instrument]]
names as having nothing that observes it. **A rule that decides other questions and rests on
nothing is the worst combination of load and support this store can carry**, and that is what was
true until today.

# How the hole was found: a field that had nowhere to point

**Nobody noticed the rule was unsupported by reading it.** Nine days of reading it, citing it and
reasoning from it produced no doubt about where it came from, because a rule rendered into
`CLAUDE.md` reads exactly the same whether or not anything decided it.

**What found it was a mechanical pass.** `decided_by:` was admitted as a field on 2026-08-23
([[decided-by-is-a-field-of-its-own-and-not-a-line-in-sources]]) and narrowed to the two folders a
decision can cause a page in ([[decided-by-is-legal-in-proposals-and-rules-and-required-in-rules]]);
filling it across `wiki/rules/` meant naming, for every rule page in turn, the one decision behind
it. Fourteen of the fifteen had one to name. **This one had nowhere to point**, and the field could
not be filled without inventing a cause — so it was left empty and raised, and the emptiness is what
carried the finding to the owner.

**That is the reusable half, and it generalises past this field.** A required link is an audit that
runs itself: it does not ask whether a claim is well-founded, it asks the page to *name* its
foundation, and a page that cannot is visible in a way that no amount of careful reading makes it.
The same order has now worked three times here — legal and optional, then the back-fill, then
promote — and in each case the back-fill was where the defects surfaced rather than the promotion
([[a-check-that-passed-for-the-wrong-reason-becomes-a-rule]], [[knowing-a-rule-is-not-a-mechanism]]).

**And the librarian's refusal to fill it was the load-bearing move.** Pointing the field at the
nearest plausible page — [[hand-edits-of-the-wiki-are-a-recorded-divergence]] and
[[the-turn-that-explains-a-rule-adds-exceptions-to-it]] were both in `sources:` and both are
*evidence the rule cites*, not rulings that it should exist — would have closed the hole, turned
the field green, and lost the finding permanently. **A field filled from the nearest page is worse
than an empty one**, because the empty one still asks a question.

# Rejected: striking the rule as never-ruled

**The alternative was real and it was offered.** It is what happened to the last unsupported rule
this store found: `advisory-is-the-default-for-a-hook` stood for three days as a quotation stamped
`basis: human`, was read back to him, and he disowned it — «не было такого правила. Это правило
неправильное» — after which it was superseded and archived
([[a-hook-refuses-on-a-measured-condition-a-reliable-measurement-and-a-refusal-that-says-how-to-clear-it]],
[[a-paraphrase-signed-owner-becomes-a-quote-and-is-reasoned-from-as-his-word]]).

**The two cases have the same shape and different answers, and that is the point of asking rather
than deciding.** Both were rules nobody could show a ruling for; the difference between them is not
something a reader could have derived from the tree, from the wording, or from how useful the rule
looked. **Only he could tell them apart**, which is the whole argument for the ratification sweep
being his and not the librarian's ([[the-ratification-sweep-waits-for-a-session-of-its-own]],
[[the-retro-ratifies-every-line-before-it-is-attributed-to-the-owner]]).

**The cost of guessing wrong runs in both directions.** Striking a rule he does hold removes a
constraint from a file every agent reads; keeping one he does not hold is how a session's sentence
becomes law. Neither error is discoverable afterwards from the store alone.

# What this decision does not do

**It does not give the rule an instrument.** Nothing mechanical observes it, and
[[every-rule-in-wiki-rules-owes-a-mechanical-instrument]] still names it as owing one — the
obstacle recorded there is that weakening a rule and repairing one look identical in a diff. A
ratification settles whose rule it is and not whether anything can see it break.

**It does not change a byte of the rule's text.** What was ratified is the paragraph as it stands,
and the rule page's slice is unchanged.

**It does not repair the citation, and the citation is owed.** The document in which he was shown
this text and answered is this session's journal, which does not exist yet: it is written at
`/handoff` and compiled at `/ingest`. **A `sources:` line pointing at a file that is not there reads
as provenance and is not**, so it is deliberately not written today, and the ingest that compiles
this session's journal adds it. Until then the block at the head of this page is the whole of the
evidence, and it is on the page rather than in a source — the same shape as
[[a-refusal-is-conditional-or-absolute-and-nothing-else]], the other prohibition ratified this week.

**What it does discharge** is the one empty `decided_by:` in `wiki/rules/`. The field on
[[ask-before-weakening-or-working-around-a-rule]] now names this page. **Promoting the field from
`optional:` to `required:` in `wiki/rules/_schema.yaml` is not the librarian's** and is not done
here.
