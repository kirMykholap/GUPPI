# Decided 2026-08-17: the word is settled, and the sprint one is renamed

**The end of a session is a *retrospective* — "retro" — and `/handoff` follows it.** The
sprint-level ritual is renamed **sprint retrospective** and is not designed yet.

This settles the open half of [[ingest-runs-after-handoff-at-the-end-of-a-session]], which recorded
the owner's decision to rename the end-of-session skill and said the name had not been chosen.

## Why a name was worth a decision

**The word meant both rituals for three days**, and that ambiguity is not cosmetic: it is what put
two contradictory answers into this vault about when `/ingest` runs. One page read "the
retrospective" as the sprint's and moved the ingest to it; the repository's `CLAUDE.md` read it as
the session's. Two `current` pages, one question, `basis: human` on each — resolved on 2026-08-17 by
the owner retiring the earlier one.

**A word that names two cadences will be read as whichever one the reader already has in mind.**
That is a retrieval failure, not a wording failure, which is why it earns a page rather than a
correction.

## What it obliges

- **The ritual has an order and a step it did not have**: retro, then `/handoff`, then `/ingest` —
  [[the-retro-step]], and the genres are kept apart ([[retro-interrogates-and-handoff-routes]]).
- **`sprint retrospective` is now a term with nothing behind it.** Nothing is designed for it, and
  the next session to reach a sprint boundary meets that gap rather than an ambiguity.
- **What the ritual produces after the journal is written still has no home** —
  [[the-closing-ritual-produces-what-the-journal-cannot-hold]].

## The definition is decided as a `concepts/` page and is still not built — 2026-08-18

**It is unblocked.** The mechanism it waited on landed at `e83ce28` — a page in any folder may carry
`governs:` ([[governs-becomes-a-property-of-any-page]]) — and the owner ruled the same day that **a
definition may render**, not only an imperative or a sourced assertion
([[a-definition-may-render-into-the-repository-claude-md]]).

**Consequence of it still being unbuilt:** the word that already put two contradictory answers into
this corpus has no page rendering into the file that uses it, so the ambiguity is held by whoever
happens to have read this page. **It is the owner's stated first item for the next session.**

**It cannot come from `wiki/rules/`** — a definition is not imperative, and admitting it would empty
that folder's admission test ([[rules-becomes-a-type-of-its-own]]).

## The page exists from 2026-08-18, and the region is not yet in the file

**The definition has its page**: [[a-retrospective-is-a-sessions-end-not-a-sprints-ritual]], in
`concepts/`, carrying `governs: CLAUDE.md` and one `<!-- text -->` slice. The slice is the paragraph
that stood at `CLAUDE.md:58-60`, **moved and not reworded** — cut by line number and diffed against
the page rather than read against it, the way the nine rule migrations were done
([[the-suite-never-runs-against-the-production-database]]).

**The section above is left standing as the state before it.** *"It is the owner's stated first item
for the next session"* was true when it was written, and that session is the one that wrote the page.

**What is still owed is the region.** Marking it in `CLAUDE.md` and running the injector belong to
the session rather than to the librarian, and until that lands the injector reports that the
destination carries no region for the page — the expected state of a half-finished landing
([[a-check-asks-by-running-the-writer-it-checks]]). The typed paragraph is removed only after the
rendered one is verified present, which is the one safe order
([[the-maps-region-replaces-the-prose-it-duplicated]]).
