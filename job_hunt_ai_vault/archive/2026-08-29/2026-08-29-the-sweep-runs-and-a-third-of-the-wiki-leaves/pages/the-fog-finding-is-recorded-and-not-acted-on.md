# Decided 2026-08-20, at the retro of the fourth session

**The owner cannot see the harness in any of the four directions it was offered**, and that is the
finding ([[the-harness-is-no-longer-visible-to-its-owner]]). **What was decided is what to do about
it tonight: nothing.**

**owner:** «Записать, решать потом.»

**Two repairs were offered and both were declined for tonight:**

- **a generated harness map** — one file built by a script from `.claude/settings.json` and the hook
  files, so it cannot go stale, which is this project's own idiom. Its design is
  [[a-generated-harness-map]];
- **a session that builds nothing and only makes the existing visible.** No page carries that as a
  design, because none was written; it is named here so it is not re-invented as new.

**Rejected: building one of them in the session that found the problem.** The finding arrived at the
retro, after the night's approved scope was already committed, and the language question below makes
the first repair a rule change rather than a build.

## What it obliges

**It is not a deferral against a condition and it is not owed to a date.** It waits on his decision,
and the next session re-offers it rather than treating silence as a refusal
([[a-deferred-check-must-be-run-when-its-condition-arrives]] is the *other* shape and does not apply
here).

**It bears on S2's own goal** — *the vault is used, not maintained*. The owner's review is the only
control this project has, and it cannot operate on something he cannot see
([[the-librarian-writes-unattended]]). **Whether that makes it a criterion is his**
([[a-sprint-closes-on-its-goal]]).
