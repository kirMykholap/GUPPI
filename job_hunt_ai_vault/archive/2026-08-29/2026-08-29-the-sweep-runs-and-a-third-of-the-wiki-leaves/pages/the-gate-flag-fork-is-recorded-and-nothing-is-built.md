# The two forms, each with what it costs

**A mode that issues no verdict leaves the prohibition untouched.** It runs a subset, never prints
`VAULT-CHECK: ok`, and always exits 0 — the same standing `--rules` already has, on the same ground:
a mode with no verdict has no verdict to hide
([[the-rules-flag-is-a-mode-rather-than-a-filter-and-stands-outside-the-flag-prohibition]]).

**A flag requires overturning the prohibition by supersession**, which is a conversation with the
owner and not a session's call.
