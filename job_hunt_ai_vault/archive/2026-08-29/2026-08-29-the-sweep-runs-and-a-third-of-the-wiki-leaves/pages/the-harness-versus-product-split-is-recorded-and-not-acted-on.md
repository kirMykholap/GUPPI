# Decided 2026-08-22, with the figure in front of him

**The split was measured by the session that reported it rather than quoted from a journal**, and the
numbers are on the page that already carried the earlier reading
([[an-admission-test-for-mechanisms]]). **The owner read them and chose to record them and move on.**

**That is the same disposition he took on the fog finding**, and for the same reason: the finding is
real, the repairs are all larger than the moment they arrived in
([[the-fog-finding-is-recorded-and-not-acted-on]]).

# What he rejected, and each rejection says something different

- **Making it S2's seventh acceptance criterion, via an admission test for mechanisms.** The criterion
  he took instead is the `authority` index, **decided by a command**; an admission test for mechanisms
  is decided by a judgement, and that is the ground the swap was made on. **No criterion's reading is
  restated here** — that is the owner's ([[a-sprint-closes-on-its-goal]]).
- **Opening the next sprint on the product.** The current sprint is not closed and closing one is his
  ([[product-work-does-not-interleave-into-the-vault-sprint]]).
- **Deferring the question to the retro.** He answered it where it was raised, which is what keeps a
  deferral from becoming a fourth-session carry
  ([[a-conversation-the-owner-scheduled-is-displaced-by-the-work-beside-it]]).

# What it obliges

**It is not a deferral against a condition and it is not owed to a date.** It waits on his decision,
and the next session re-offers it rather than reading silence as a refusal — the same shape the fog
finding was given.

**It does not bear on whether product work may interleave into this sprint.**
[[product-work-does-not-interleave-into-the-vault-sprint]] is untouched by this and answers a
different question: that page says what this sprint admits, and this one says what was decided about
a figure describing where the work has gone.

# One of the two figures behind it stopped describing anything, and the clause went — 2026-08-23

**The owner struck the clause that `concepts` describes the product**, measured at about 19 of 103
pages, choosing that against keeping it and splitting product mechanisms from harness mechanisms
([[the-clause-that-concepts-describes-the-product-is-struck]]).

**That is not this deferral being lifted.** Striking a description that had gone false removes a
sentence; the split this page defers is a mechanism, and the ruling explicitly rejected building
one. **This page is untouched and still waits on his decision.** What changed is that the vault's
own `CLAUDE.md` no longer states the thing the figure contradicted.

**And the proposal it was raised on now has its verdict.** *An admission test for mechanisms* was
raised on exactly this figure and **is not a criterion**; whether it is ever adopted as a rule is
still open, and nothing here refuses it ([[an-admission-test-for-mechanisms]],
[[a-question-asked-is-not-a-question-answered]]).
