# The ruling, 2026-08-23

> **owner:** «да, запиши как proposal»

**Three words, and they are a decision rather than an aside**: the tool was raised in conversation,
nothing was built, and he chose the record over the evaluation.

**Rejected: trying it that day.** The session was landing the style rule and the `decided_by:`
field; a new external tool evaluated beside them would have been judged on whether it fitted the
hour rather than on what it buys.

# What it caused

**[[the-obsidian-cli-as-a-reading-surface-for-this-store]]**, which carries what the CLI would
strengthen — the catalogue and the family lookup — and the two things that disqualify it from the
checking half: it encodes none of this project's contract, and it needs the desktop app running.

# What the deferral costs

**The reading half of this store still has no test**, and it is the half a general tool could help
with.
