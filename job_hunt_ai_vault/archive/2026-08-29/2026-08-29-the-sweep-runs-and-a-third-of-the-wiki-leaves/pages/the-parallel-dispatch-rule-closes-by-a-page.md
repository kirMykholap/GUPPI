# The ruling, 2026-08-25

> **owner:** «2) страницу»

> **owner:** «9) новой страницей как porposal»

**[[dispatch-librarians-in-parallel-only-for-reading]] closes its instrument obligation by a page
carrying the argument**, rather than by looking for a counter that could enforce it. **And the page is
a new one**, rather than the argument resting inside the page that records the obligation
([[every-rule-in-wiki-rules-owes-a-mechanical-instrument]]).

**The argument itself is on that page** ([[a-dispatch-counter-cannot-express-the-parallel-rule]]).

# What was rejected

**A declared dispatch mode**, which would let `lock-the-librarian.ps1` count only the dispatches that
write. **It is a self-declaration** — the party being measured supplies the measurement — and the rule
it would instrument is exactly the one the declaration cannot verify
([[the-quantity-field-is-rejected-and-the-rule-stays-unconditional]],
[[an-instrument-that-contains-its-own-answers-can-be-gamed]]).

# Not done

**The page it closes by is written and the closure is recorded nowhere mechanical.** There is no
register that reads a rule's closure yet
([[the-rule-closure-register-is-deferred-and-its-design-is-complete]]).
