# The incident

**A session recorded no feedback marks for the whole of its retro, in which it took about twenty
decisions** — the densest part of the session for decisions.

**The cause is diagnosable rather than forgetfulness.** Step 5 of `/retro` builds its ratification
list *from the marks*, and the session read that as the retro replacing the mark channel. **It reads
them; it does not replace them.**

# What the gap costs, and it is not the lines

**The lines were recovered from the transcript at the retro.** What cannot be recovered is the
comparison: how many of a retro's open lines came from the marks against how many were found by
reading.

**The reading of 2026-08-24 was fourteen from marks and seven found by reading**, with five more
surfacing inside the retro itself. The previous measured ratio was four from marks against eleven
found by reading. **The marks missed a third here where they missed two thirds then** — and a
session that stops marking mid-way makes the next such comparison meaningless.

# Not repaired
