# The ruling, 2026-08-29

**owner:** «фронтир - это не про спринт или про grill-me, это общая концепция - то, как мы работаем.
Когда мы начинаем сессию в plan mode или когда переходим к retro - мы должны составить фронтир
вопросов и пройтись по нему. По сути мы так всегда и делали, но это не было описано.»

**A frontier is a boundary of open questions, composed at two moments and walked to confirm
everything is closed:** at the start of a session in plan mode, and again at the retro. **It is how
we already worked and it was never written down**, which is his own reading and is the reason the page
exists.

# Where a session's list lives, and it is not the sprint

**owner:** «Мне кажется лучше подходит бриф. Именно он определяет фронтир на сессию. А спринт - это
критерии и обьем работы, это немного другое.»

**Rejected: `sprint.md`.** The sprint holds acceptance criteria and the volume of work; the frontier
is the questions a session opens and closes, which is a different object with a different lifetime.

# What it obliges, and the consequence the session named

**`brief.md` is derived and is never a source**, so the frontier section must be **generated from the
tree** rather than written into the file by hand. A section a librarian types into the brief is gone
at the next ingest and was never anywhere else.

**Nothing generates it yet.** The section is designed and unbuilt, it has no S3 item, and no live page
other than this one describes it. At the next census it reads as an `undesigned` deferral unless a
proposal is written for it.

# Two live pages already use the word, and neither is this

> [!NOTE] RAISED, NOT RESOLVED — different `authority:` slugs, so rule 3 of the gate cannot see either
> disagreement and no `CONTRADICTION` block is legal here.

**[[the-output-side-is-finished-today-and-does-not-close-the-corpuss-growth]]** is a live prohibition
under `archiving-scope-frontier` saying the remaining frontier *"is written down … on `sprint.md` and
on the proposal pages named below"*. **This ruling puts a session's frontier in `brief.md` and says
the sprint is a different thing.** Neither page names the other. The two may be about different
frontiers — one a scope's remainder, one a session's questions — and **saying which is the owner's,
not the librarian's.**

**And [[grill-me-skill]] uses the word for a third thing** under `interview-skill`: the boundary of
questions inside a grilling. That is one word over two mechanisms, the shape that cost this store
three days over *retrospective*
([[a-retrospective-is-a-sessions-end-not-a-sprints-ritual]],
[[a-forked-skill-starts-empty-and-a-subtask-fork-inherits-everything]]).

**Found by [[the-decisions-agent]] on its first dispatch**, which is the class of finding it exists
for.

# Retired 2026-08-30, and the collision this page raised was closed the next day

**Superseded by [[the-frontier-is-one-concept-with-three-carriers]].** The owner ruled that the three
uses of the word are one concept with three carriers: `sprint.md` is the frontier between sessions
and where a deferred pointer lands, `brief.md` is its **view** at session start generated from
`sprint.md`, and `grill-me` extends it.

**What went false is this page's central ruling**, not its reasoning. It put a session's list in
`brief.md` and **rejected `sprint.md` by name**; the successor puts the storage in `sprint.md` and
keeps the brief as the derived view. The reversal is knowing and it is his.

**What this page got right and the successor keeps**: that the brief is derived, so its frontier
section must be generated rather than typed; and that nothing generates it yet
([[the-brief-generates-its-frontier-section-from-sprint-md]]).
