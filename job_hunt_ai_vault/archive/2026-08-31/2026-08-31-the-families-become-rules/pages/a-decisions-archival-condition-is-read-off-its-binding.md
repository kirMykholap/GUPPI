# The ruling, 2026-08-28

*(the owner selected it, from the session's proposal.)*

| `binding:` | when the page may leave `wiki/` |
|---|---|
| `prohibition` | **not while it stands.** A live prohibition is a constraint a plan is checked against |
| `divergence` | **freely.** It teaches and forbids nothing, and its own title says so |
| `deferral` | **on the arrival of its term**, and never on a citation |

**The ground is `/pre-task`.** By its own instruction it does not read `archive/`, so a constraint
moved there stops reaching the reader it exists for
([[a-pre-task-runs-between-the-plan-and-its-approval]],
[[what-an-archived-page-asserts-is-frozen-and-how-it-is-found-is-live]]).

# What each row costs, and the numbers behind them — 2026-08-28

**`wiki/decisions/` holds 329 pages: 265 `prohibition`, 43 `deferral`, 21 `divergence`, and none
without the field.** So the `divergence` clause alone frees 21 pages, and the whole folder's
prohibitions stay where a plan check can find them.

**A deferral's term is a date or an event, not a link.** *On the arrival of its term* is a judgement
and is therefore the reading agent's, not a predicate
([[the-decisions-agent-reads-only-and-runs-at-every-ingest]]).

# What it does not say

**It does not say a prohibition never leaves.** A prohibition that has been superseded or processed
has stopped standing, and leaves by that route instead
([[a-decision-is-processed-when-a-live-page-names-it-in-decided-by]]).

# Three readings that make `deferral` the unusable half of this rule — 2026-08-29

**Nothing anywhere records what a given deferral's term IS.** No field, no convention, no page. **So the
exit cannot be judged mechanically at all**, and the 32 `binding: deferral` pages in `wiki/decisions/`
stand with no way to test any of them. Surfaced when the owner asked why deferrals are kept at all.

**`deferral` covers two different objects with different exits**, and 32 pages wear one mark for both:

- *"this is already designed, do not invent it again"* — which the vault states, and which discharges
  when the thing is built;
- *"we did not get to discussing this and moved it"* — which the owner states, and which discharges
  when the conversation happens.

**Zero of the 32 are named in `decided_by:` by any live proposal or rule.** So no deferral in the folder
has a design behind it that cites it, and none can retire under the model where a decision discharges
against the page it caused. **It does not distinguish "the design does not exist" from "the design
exists and does not cite it"** — only reading them does, which is the census
([[the-decisions-folder-is-censused-before-its-type-system-is-redesigned]]).

## The `processed` predicate archived a deferral whose term had not arrived

**`structure-work-agreed-and-not-executed` left `wiki/` because three live proposals name it in
`decided_by:`** — while this page rules that a deferral leaves on the arrival of its term and **never on
a citation**, and several of its rows, including the `vault-ask` mask-versus-body fork, are undone.

**The two clauses collide, and the collision is visible only on a multi-row deferral.** One row's design
existing is enough to satisfy the citation criterion for the whole page.

**The sweep's own arithmetic, read the same day**: 66 decisions taken — 22 on `binding: divergence`
alone, which is unconditional, and 44 because a live page named them. **Only pages that acquired a
holder were taken mechanically**, and `divergence` is now zero in the folder, so that criterion selects
nothing until a new divergence is written.

# A third exit was opened on 2026-08-30 and this page enumerates only two

> [!NOTE] RAISED, NOT RESOLVED — the `authority:` slugs differ, so rule 3 of the gate cannot see this
> disagreement and no `CONTRADICTION` block is legal for it.

**[[a-refused-proposal-releases-every-decision-in-its-list]]** (`refused-proposal-decision-release`)
says a refused proposal takes **every** decision in its list to `archive/`, **prohibitions included**
and unclosed. This page's *What it does not say* section enumerates **exactly two** exits — superseded,
and processed — and rules that a `prohibition` leaves *"not while it stands"*.

**Both pages are live and both carry `basis: human`, so neither outranks the other.** The older is the
owner selecting from the session's proposal; the newer is his verbatim Russian, given with a ground:
«Если мы отменили proposal - значит все его решения не имеют ценности».

**They may not conflict at all.** The newer ruling is about a decision that is **part of a body of
work** that was refused; this page is about a decision standing on its own. The owner drew that line
himself — a decision inside a proposal is part of that work, a decision pointing at a rule lives by its
own rules. **Whether the two descriptions partition the folder cleanly is his question, not the
librarian's**, and nothing here is chosen.

# This page is suspended for the cleanup — 2026-08-30, and it is not deprecated

**[[the-cleanup-suspends-the-rules-that-would-refuse-it]] lifts this page for the cleanup**, together
with three others, on one ratification: «хорошо, ратифицирую все эти 4 пункта одним решением».

**The clause that is lifted is the `prohibition` row** — *not while it stands*. During the cleanup a
prohibition may leave `wiki/decisions/` for `archive/` once the family rule carrying its clause
exists, which is a third exit this page does not enumerate
([[a-prohibition-family-becomes-one-rule-page-served-by-a-hook]]).

**The ground the lift rests on is that the exit is no longer the loss it was.** This page's whole
argument is that a constraint moved to `archive/` stops reaching the reader it exists for, because
`/pre-task` does not read there. The family mechanism gives it a reader it never had —
the rule is served when a governed file is touched, which `wiki/decisions/` never did for anybody.
**That trade is unfinished**: the delivery does not exist yet
([[the-hooks-authority-branch-is-not-written-and-the-read-matcher-is-downstream]]).

**This page stays `current`, is not `deprecated` and carries no `superseded_by:`.** A repeal is not a
supersession, ruled 2026-08-30, and what is written here is still the record of what was decided and
why.

**The suspension has no stated end.** The repeal's title says *suspends* and its body says the four
*"no longer stand"*, with no restoration condition. So *"returns when"* cannot be written here.

**And the `deferral` row is untouched by all of it.** The thirty-six pages carrying `binding:
deferral` — 32 when this page last counted, 36 on 2026-08-30 — stay in `wiki/decisions/` and are
worked in a session of their own: **owner, 2026-08-30, «остаются. Мы разберем их на следующей сессии.
Ратифифицирую»**. Nothing about their exit changed; nothing anywhere still records what a given
deferral's term is.

## What the suspension does to the unresolved pair above — 2026-08-30

> [!NOTE] STILL RAISED, STILL NOT RESOLVED.

**The ground under the pair moved and the pair did not close.** This page is suspended for the
cleanup and [[a-refused-proposal-releases-every-decision-in-its-list]] is **not**, so during the
cleanup that page is the only live rule on the question and the two do not conflict — **because one of
them does not apply, not because either was chosen.**

as resolved would let a suspension with no stated end silently retire a marked disagreement, so the
