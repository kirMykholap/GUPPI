# What the owner selected, 2026-08-24

> **owner:** «б. Принимаю твою рекомендацию»

**The destination goes in brackets straight after the kind**, as `measure: [slug] text`. Its legal
values are a page slug, a slug marked as a gauge, or the word `none`.

**Chosen against a trailing arrow**, where the anchor would be the last occurrence of a character
that also appears inside the numbers the line is about.

**Chosen against a word between the kind and the colon**, which would break the regex the catcher's
kind parser uses — the one this session had just repaired
([[a-feedback-record-opens-at-column-zero-and-indentation-marks-a-continuation]]).

# The measurement behind the requirement

**Not one of this session's `measure:` lines named a destination**, and it wrote more than forty of
them. The kind says a number was taken; nothing in the line says where it belongs, so every one of
them is routed by a librarian reading it.

# What it obliges

**The routing becomes the writer's**, at the moment the number is taken, when the party who knows
what it is evidence for is still in the room
([[a-measurement-is-testimony-or-a-gauge-and-the-role-decides-where-it-lives]]).

**Nothing checks it yet.** The kind parser reads the kind, and the destination is prose to it until
the section it feeds is built
([[the-measure-section-is-a-marked-region-legal-in-any-folder-and-required-nowhere]]).

# Extended 2026-08-25 — a gauge destination carries a page and a subject

> **owner:** «Расширяем скобку до двух имен, как ты и предлагаешь.»

**A gauge names two things and testimony names one.** The gauge form is a page slug and a subject slug
inside the bracket; testimony keeps the single page slug it always had.

**The reason is that one page can legitimately carry more than one gauge**, so the subject cannot
default to the page — and the subject is what the deferred uniqueness check compares
([[a-gauge-is-checked-for-uniqueness-and-the-check-waits-for-subjects]]).

**The bracket carries the role, which is what lets the two grammars meet.** `[slug]` is testimony and
the gauge form is a gauge, so the librarian never re-decides the kind at the ingest and the measure
region's opening word says the same thing ([[the-measure-section-grammar-is-one-line-per-number]]).

# Where the rule has not reached, read on 2026-08-25

**`log.md` carries 172 `measure:` lines and none of them names a destination**, and the corpus holds
122 pages with `basis: measurement`. The rule was decided on 2026-08-24 and the register written
before it has not been swept.

**And the session that decided it broke it the same week**: about forty destinations were written as a
trailing clause instead of a bracket straight after the kind, by a session that had not read the rule
([[a-gauge-is-written-by-a-script-that-runs-the-command]]).
