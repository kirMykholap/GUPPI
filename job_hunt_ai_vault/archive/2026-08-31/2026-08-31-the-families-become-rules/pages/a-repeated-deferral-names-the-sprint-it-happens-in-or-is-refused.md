# The ruling, 2026-08-25

> **owner:** «3) согласен»

**An item deferred again, having been deferred before, must name the sprint it happens in or be
refused.** There is no further deferral with no destination in time.

**The ground, and it is the whole of the rule:** a deferral with no destination is
indistinguishable from a refusal nobody stated. The page reads as *later*, the register carries it
forward, and nothing ever tests whether *later* arrived
([[a-question-asked-is-not-a-question-answered]]).

# What it does to a refusal

**Refusing is a legitimate outcome and it has a shape already.** A refused proposal moves to
`archive/` with `status: refused` and carries the decision that refused it
([[a-refused-proposal-is-archived-and-takes-status-refused]],
[[the-proposal-status-cannot-tell-built-from-refused]]). So the rule offers two exits and forbids
only the third.

# It bit on the day it was made

**The flag that would skip two of the gate's rules was deferred again in the same session**, and the
owner could not decide it — so the fork is recorded with both of its forms and their cost, and the
sprint it happens in is owed ([[the-gate-flag-fork-is-recorded-and-nothing-is-built]]).

# Not done

**Nothing enumerates the deferrals or counts how often each has been carried.** The rule is a
judgement the next session applies by hand, and no instrument reads it.

# Twenty-four deferrals stand against it, and the destination it asks for was removed — 2026-08-30

> [!NOTE] RAISED, NOT RESOLVED — the `authority:` slugs differ, so rule 3 of the gate cannot see this
> and no `CONTRADICTION` block is legal for it.

**The count of deferrals naming no sprint went sixteen to twenty-four on 2026-08-30**, and the session
that added eight of them recorded doing so. Every deferral the day produced — the sweep, the 53 family
rules, the paired-`authority:` requirement, the `Read` matcher, the awk program's move, the rule about
rules, the fork's dividend — names no sprint
([[everything-on-the-frontier-but-the-cleanup-is-deferred-and-none-of-it-names-a-sprint]]).

**And the same day removed the destination this rule demands.**
[[the-cleanup-is-a-forced-session-outside-any-sprint]] rules that the cleanup — which is what most of
those items wait on — belongs to no sprint. **A rule requiring a deferral to name a sprint cannot be
satisfied by work ruled to have none.**

**Two readings survive and neither is chosen here.** Either *"outside any sprint"* narrows this rule
for the class of forced session, or this rule refuses the deferrals and they should have been refused
rather than carried. **Whether the two partition the work cleanly is the owner's question**, and the
mark stays on both pages until he answers it.
