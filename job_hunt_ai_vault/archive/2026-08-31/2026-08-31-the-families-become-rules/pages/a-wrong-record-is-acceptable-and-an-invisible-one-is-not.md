# Stated 2026-08-20 by the owner, and written down here for the first time

> «Мы можем ошибиться, но ОБЯЗАНЫ иметь возможность это увидеть и исправить. Мы не пытаемся
> построить идеальную систему сразу, мы наращиваем инструменты, чтобы эта система могла
> приближаться к идеалу.»

**It was given as the reason `log.md` may never be rewritten
([[archive-is-rewritten-by-a-path-move-and-log-md-never-is]]) and it is wider than that ruling.**
It is read here as its own claim, on the owner's own framing that it is the principle under all of
it: a wrong record is acceptable; an invisible one is not.

## What it settles when it is applied

**It is the standing argument for building the detector rather than for getting it right the first
time.** Correctness at the moment of writing is not the property being bought; the ability to see
the error afterwards is.

**Rejected, by construction: any repair that removes the evidence of the error.** Correcting a log
entry in place, deleting a page rather than deprecating it
([[the-librarian-writes-unattended]]), or hiding a file from a denominator rather than changing
what the denominator asks ([[hiding-a-file-from-a-denominator-is-weaker-than-counting-it-as-handled]]).

## It reads as the root of rulings already on the record

- **A rewriter must be paired with a detector** ([[a-rewriter-must-be-paired-with-a-detector]]) —
  the rewriter may be wrong, so something must be able to say so.
- **An advisory prints one line and sorts what it names**
  ([[an-advisory-prints-one-line-and-sorts-what-it-names]]) — a signal that cannot be read is an
  invisible record.
- **Nothing is ever deleted; a page is deprecated with its successor named** — the same property,
  applied to the corpus.
- **An intention is never filed as a record of something built**
  ([[proposals-are-a-folder-not-a-status]]) — an error that looks like a fact is the invisible kind.

## What it does not license

It is not an argument for writing carelessly and repairing later. The instruments this project
builds are what make a wrong record visible, and the store has already measured what an instrument
that fails towards *nothing to do* costs ([[an-instrument-that-fails-towards-nothing-to-do]]).
