# Context

**The cleanup of 2026-08-30 was opened over the whole of `wiki/`**, which held 597 live pages that
morning: decisions 320, concepts 142, proposals 50, entities 45, lessons 23, rules 17.

**Two of the six folders were taken out of scope before any criterion was written**, on the owner's
own reading of them rather than on a measurement.

# The question

**Which folders does the cleanup work on?**

# How it works after

**`proposals/` and `lessons/` are out of scope and nothing about them changes.** No archival
criterion is written for either, no sweep walks them, and no verdict column exists for them in the
review table.

**`decisions/` is the folder the cleanup is about**, worked by families
([[a-prohibition-family-becomes-one-rule-page-served-by-a-hook]]). **`concepts/` and `entities/` are
in scope for a taxonomy and not for an exit criterion**, which he declined to name
([[concepts-are-non-functional-requirements-and-entities-are-functional]]).

**This does not say either folder is finished.** `lessons/` had been reduced deliberately in earlier
sessions and `proposals/` had never been read whole — he says so himself. **Out of scope for this
operation is what the ruling means.**

# Recommendation

**Take the scope he gave and record it as a page.** A folder left alone silently reads, later, as a
folder nobody got to; a folder left alone by a ruling reads as a decision. This store already had the
precedent for recording exactly that
([[entities-and-concepts-get-no-archival-criterion-today]]).

**Rejected: writing a criterion for all six folders**, which was the shape the session's first framing
had.

# Ratification

**owner, 2026-08-30:** «proposals сейчас боле менее адекватно. Я не читал их все, но их не так уж
много как будто. lessons так же мы боле менее наладили, можем пока не трогать.»
