# Ruled at the retro of 2026-08-20

**[[a-definition-may-render-into-the-repository-claude-md]] and
[[the-marker-carries-the-records-type]] lose `record-schema`.** Both are about how a record
**reaches** a governed file, not about what a record must **contain**; `harness` is the honest tag
and both already carry it.

`record-schema` in `decisions/` goes from sixteen pages to fourteen.

**The third candidate named on `sprint.md` needed nothing**:
`the-governs-branch-widens-and-the-others-stay-rules-only` already sits in `archive/` with
`superseded_by:`, and a tag on a frozen page classifies nothing.

## The advisory still prints at fourteen, and that is the point

**Fourteen is still above the ceiling of ten**, so the line the trim was raised against is
unchanged. That is what makes this a trim **on the merits** rather than counter-management — the
failure [[an-instrument-that-contains-its-own-answers-can-be-gamed]] names, and the third instance
of it this store has watched for ([[the-dense-tags-are-not-split-and-the-standing-advisories-are-named-here]]).

**Rejected: splitting the tag**, which would buy about two ingests of quiet and cost a judgement
pass over sixteen pages ([[the-tag-density-advisories-move-behind-lint]]).
