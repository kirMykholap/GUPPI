# The ruling, 2026-08-24 — variant (b), selected by the owner

**The librarian opens each proposal's `sources:`, finds the journal, and extracts the deciding
decision.** `status: idea` is set **only where no decision actually exists**.

# What was rejected, and the ground is his

**Marking all 33 pages without the field as `idea` in one sweep.** `decided_by:` landed on
2026-08-23, so **a page may lack it because there was nowhere to record it when it was written, not
because nobody ruled** ([[decided-by-is-a-field-of-its-own-and-not-a-line-in-sources]],
[[decided-by-is-legal-in-proposals-and-rules-and-required-in-rules]]).

**A sweep would write *nobody decided this* onto an unknown number of pages where it is false** —
and an unknown number is the problem: nothing afterwards could say which of the 33 had been
mislabelled ([[an-unsourced-number-is-dropped-not-chosen]]).

# The cost was named before it was accepted

**34 pages read, one ingest's work.** **Only 4 of the 34 mention `decisions/` at all**, so 30 need
the journal opened — which is the whole reason a sweep was attractive.

# The population, measured on the day

**50 pages across `wiki/proposals/` and `wiki/rules/`: 17 filled, 33 short.** `wiki/rules/` is full
at 16 of 16, so **the entire shortfall is in `proposals/`, which carries 1 of 34**
([[the-legal-value-list-lives-in-each-folders-schema]]).

# The ordering it preserves

**Back-fill first, verdict second.** The verdict pairing `status: idea` with `decided_by:` reddens
pages, and a red gate locks out the only writer able to fill the field
([[the-idea-and-decided-by-pair-is-ordered-built-and-the-back-fill-goes-first]],
[[a-lock-on-a-red-tree-hides-the-health-of-what-it-guards]]).

**Neither half is built.** Decided here, and the work is the next librarian's.
