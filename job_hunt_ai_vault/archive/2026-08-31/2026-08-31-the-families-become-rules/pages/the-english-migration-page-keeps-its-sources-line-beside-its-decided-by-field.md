# The ruling, 2026-08-24

> **owner:** «оставить как есть»

**`wiki/proposals/migrating-the-repository-to-english.md` names its cause twice** — once as a line
in `sources:`, the old idiom, and once in `decided_by:`, the field built to replace it. Both stay.

**Rejected: removing the `sources:` entry.** Both sentences are true, and deleting a true line for
tidiness erases provenance.

# Why this page is not a hole in the field that replaced the idiom

**[[decided-by-is-a-field-of-its-own-and-not-a-line-in-sources]] rejects `sources:` as the place a
*cause* is named.** It does not oblige anyone to delete a `sources:` line that is also true: the
page was genuinely compiled from that decision as well as caused by it.

**And this page is the one place a reader can see the two idioms side by side**, which is worth more
than the tidiness it costs.
