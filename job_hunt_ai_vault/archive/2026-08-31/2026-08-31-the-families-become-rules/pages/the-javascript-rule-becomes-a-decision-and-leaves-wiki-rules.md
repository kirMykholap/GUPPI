# The ruling, 2026-08-26

*(the owner selected it, against a second shape in which `governs:` would point at a new file.)*

**[[our-javascript-does-not-choose-which-rows-are-on-screen]] stops being a `wiki/rules/` page.** It
moves to `decisions/`, takes `binding: prohibition`, mints an `authority:` slug, and loses `governs:`
and `render:`.

**The region leaves `CLAUDE.md` in the same change as the `governs:` field**, or rule 11's address
verdict fires on the leftover copy
([[the-address-verdict-catches-the-leftover-copy-not-the-wrong-file]]).

# The ground of the 2026-08-18 decision was re-read, and it holds

**That decision rested on `/pre-task` surfacing a rule nobody knows the name of**, and it gained a
step when `/pre-task` itself left `CLAUDE.md` for a hook message on 2026-08-20 — which the page
recorded as owing a re-reading ([[the-javascript-rule-leaves-claude-md-and-is-not-yet-removed]]).

**The re-reading was done and the ground stands.** `/pre-task` arrives by hook, and the owner has
already called hook delivery **more reliable** than a region a reader skips
([[the-pre-task-rule-leaves-claude-md-for-a-hook-message]],
[[a-pre-task-runs-between-the-plan-and-its-approval]]).

# Rejected

**Rejected: keeping the page in `wiki/rules/` with `governs:` naming some other file.** There is no
such file. The decision of 2026-08-18 established on three measurements that this rule fails the
`paths:` criterion — our JavaScript is inline in a template, nothing of ours sits under `static/`,
and writing a new file matches no read — so a scoped destination would be invented rather than
found.

# What it obliges, and none of it is done

- **The page moves folders**, which moves its address: every inbound `[[wikilink]]`, both registers
  and `log.md` name it ([[a-page-rename-is-sed-and-verify]]).
- **`authority:` is minted and entered in `authority.md`**, and `governs:` and `render:` come off.
- **The region comes out of `CLAUDE.md` in the same change**, and `bash scripts/vault-inject.sh`
  runs before the gate.
- **Region coverage moves** — the denominator counts governed files, and this removes one
  ([[the-criterion-on-governed-files-is-reworded-to-admit-full-generation]]).

**It is work rather than a question now**, and it waits with the rest of the plan's third phase
([[the-plans-remaining-phase-moves-to-s3-and-the-ritual-runs-whole]]).

# The second slug is struck: the rule folds into the page that already answers its question — 2026-08-26

*(the owner selected it, later the same day, against the shape ruled above.)*

**The destination stands and the new page does not.** The rule's text goes into
[[javascript-policy]], which carries `authority: js-scope` and is filed in `authority.md` under *the
front end's stack*, defined as **what our own JavaScript may and may not do**. That is the question
the rule states, so a page of its own would have been a second answer to it — and the obvious
filename for that page collides with `javascript-policy`'s own.

**So three of the four obligations above are struck and one is met differently:**

- **the page does not move folders** — it is `deprecated` with
  `superseded_by: [[javascript-policy]]` and archived, so no address moves and no inbound link is
  rewritten;
- **no `authority:` is minted and none is entered in `authority.md`** — `js-scope` already carries
  the claim, and the register is unchanged by this;
- **`governs:` and `render:` come off**, which is unchanged from the ruling above;
- **the region and region coverage** are the one obligation still live, and what actually happened
  to the region is recorded on [[the-javascript-rule-leaves-claude-md-and-is-not-yet-removed]],
  which is the page that answers whether it is gone.

**Recorded rather than rewritten.** The ruling above is what was decided in the morning and it is
the ground the fold was chosen against; what changed is the shape, not the destination
([[the-date-of-a-correction-note-is-the-date-the-text-became-false]]).
