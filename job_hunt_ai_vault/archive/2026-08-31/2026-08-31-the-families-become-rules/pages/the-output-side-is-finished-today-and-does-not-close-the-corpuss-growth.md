# Why the distinction matters

**Draining a folder and admitting fewer pages are opposite ends of the same pipe**, and only the
draining end was built. The admission end has a lead nobody has measured: **the ingest of
2026-08-26 produced 10 new pages against 24 dated sections appended to existing ones**, and the
share of those 10 that could have been a section is unknown
([[no-new-entity-where-an-existing-one-can-carry-the-claim]],
[[an-admission-test-for-mechanisms]]).
