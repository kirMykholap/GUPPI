# The ruling, 2026-08-26

*(the owner selected it, against building it.)*

**[[a-path-detector-for-prose-references]] is refused.** It is not deferred again.

# The measurement it was refused on is the proposal's own

**Across nine pages the gate was blind to five prose references, and four of those five were correct
as written.** A page recording history is supposed to name a path that no longer exists.

**So the advisory's real output is a judgement queue of one**, and its noise floor is exactly the
history this corpus exists to keep. An instrument whose findings are four fifths correct-as-written
trains its reader past it ([[an-instrument-that-answers-half-a-question-in-the-voice-of-a-whole-one]]).

# What is left uncovered, and it was already accepted

**The prose half of a path move cannot be automated**, which the ruling of 2026-08-19 recorded and
the owner accepted **for now** ([[sed-cannot-tell-an-address-from-a-quotation]],
[[raw-is-emptied-by-a-script-and-the-move-lives-inside-the-ingest]]).

**The eye pass stays the method.** What changes is that the nearest mechanical help is now refused
rather than pending, so nobody re-proposes it as the missing piece.

# Rejected

**Rejected: building it as an advisory.** That is the shape the proposal already narrowed itself to,
and the measurement is against that shape rather than a stronger one.

**Rejected: a fourth deferral** ([[a-repeated-deferral-names-the-sprint-it-happens-in-or-is-refused]]).

# What it obliges, and it is not done

**The proposal takes `status: refused` and names this page in `decided_by:`, and it moves to
`archive/`** ([[a-refused-proposal-is-archived-and-takes-status-refused]]). **The status and the field
are set; the move waits** with the rest of the plan's third phase, and its slug leaves `authority.md`
before it goes ([[retiring-a-page-removes-its-slug-from-the-authority-register]],
[[the-plans-remaining-phase-moves-to-s3-and-the-ritual-runs-whole]]).
