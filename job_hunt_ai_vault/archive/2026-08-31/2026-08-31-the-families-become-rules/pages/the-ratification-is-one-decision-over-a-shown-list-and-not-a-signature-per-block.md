# What he asked for first, and it is the condition the ruling rests on

> **owner:** «показывай полный список и контекст. Я хочу понимать к чему именно относился decision
> перед тем как согласиться или нет»

**He would not rule on a list he could not see the context of.** The sweep was run by showing every
owner-attributed block with its page, its line and what it was about.

# The ruling, 2026-08-24

> **owner:** «я ратифицирую все эти блоки. Утверждения верны по сути.»

**This is ONE ratification of a list, not a signature on each block.** Writing it as a separate
owner utterance per block would reproduce the exact defect the sweep exists to remove — a session's
sentence acquiring his voice ([[a-paraphrase-signed-owner-becomes-a-quote-and-is-reasoned-from-as-his-word]]).

**Rejected: recording each block as separately signed.** It reads stronger and is false: he read a
list and answered once, about the list.

**And the shape of what he ratified is exact.** He ratified that the *claims* are right in substance
— «утверждения верны по сути» — not that the words are his. A block that is a faithful paraphrase
stays a paraphrase.

# One block of the list was rejected

> **owner:** «Мне не нравится пункт 6: он устарел, так как там число и какой статус сейчас - мы не
> знаем.»

**It is the instruction to report a fixed count of unmarked files**, and its successor carries no
number at all ([[the-region-coverage-instruction-carries-no-count-and-reports-the-unmarked-files-as-unchanged]]).

**The outcome is the argument for showing the list whole**: of everything shown, the one block he
rejected is the one with a drifted number sewn into it.

# The duplicates are deferred

> **owner:** «Некоторые из них продублированы - это мы разбрем позже, когда подготовим и
> протестируем механизм»

**One block is duplicated word for word on two pages** — the same sentence on
[[the-maps-region-replaces-the-prose-it-duplicated]] and
[[a-question-asked-is-not-a-question-answered]]. So the list holds fewer distinct claims than it
holds lines, and sorting that out waits for the mechanism.

# What it obliges

**Nothing here marks a page as ratified.** How a confirmed block is written on its page is the open
question this sweep inherited and did not answer
([[a-sweep-may-run-inside-a-session-when-the-list-is-assembled-in-advance-and-shown-whole]]).

# The defect reappeared in the mark channel on the day the sweep closed

**Eight of fourteen `decision:` marks written that same day carried the session's paraphrase rather
than his words** ([[a-mark-that-names-the-shape-of-a-decision-is-not-obliged-to-carry-the-words]]).
**Closing the sweep does not close the channel that fills it**, and the two obligations — name the
shape, carry the words — are still not the same obligation.
