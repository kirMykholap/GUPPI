# The ruling, 2026-08-24

> **owner:** «Мне не нравится пункт 6: он устарел, так как там число и какой статус сейчас - мы не
> знаем.»

**The instruction is: report it as *the unmarked files are unchanged*, never as *coverage
improved*.** It names no quantity, and nothing about it goes stale.

**Rejected: a successor that names today's count.** The predecessor's instruction was written with a
number inside it and the number drifted through four values in two weeks. Writing a new one there
buys the same two weeks.

# Why the number cannot live inside the instruction

**A count of unmarked files is a gauge**: it answers *how much now*, it has one true value at a
time, and it mutates ([[a-measurement-is-testimony-or-a-gauge-and-the-role-decides-where-it-lives]]).
**An instruction is not a place a mutating value can sit** — it is read as a rule and re-quoted by
readers who are not checking it, so the stale copy travels.

**The reading is the command, never a sentence.** `bash scripts/vault-check.sh` prints the advisory
and names every unmarked file by path, so a reader who needs the count has an instrument and does
not need a page ([[a-printed-register-cannot-drift-and-a-maintained-one-always-has]]).

# The reading of 2026-08-24, dated where it stands

**`a region or whole generation covers 8 of 15`**, with seven files unmarked and **all seven named
by the advisory**: `.claude/skills/grill-me/SKILL.md`, `.claude/skills/retro/SKILL.md`,
`.claude/skills/sprint/SKILL.md`, `.claude/skills/vault-lint/SKILL.md`,
`.claude/templates/handoff.md`, `.claude/templates/retro.md` and `job_hunt_ai_vault/CLAUDE.md`.

**This line is dated on purpose and is not part of the instruction above**
([[the-region-coverage-figure-is-dated-where-it-stands]]). It is a reading of one day, and the day
is what makes it true.

# What carries over from the page this replaces, unchanged

- **The denominator moves in both directions and for reasons unrelated to markup** — a governed
  markdown file appearing or leaving moves it, and a rewording of the criterion has moved the
  numerator without any file gaining a region
  ([[the-governed-set-is-swept-and-markdown-by-property]],
  [[the-criterion-on-governed-files-is-reworded-to-admit-full-generation]]).
- **A moved numerator is the same failure as a moved denominator, from the other side.**
- **This is not a licence to patch the figure wherever it appears.** Every copy in the corpus is a
  reading of its own day and stands ([[a-drifting-list-is-repaired-by-deleting-it-not-extending-it]]).
- **No verdict on the sprint's criterion is read off here.** Whether it is met is the owner's
  ([[a-sprint-closes-on-its-goal]]).
