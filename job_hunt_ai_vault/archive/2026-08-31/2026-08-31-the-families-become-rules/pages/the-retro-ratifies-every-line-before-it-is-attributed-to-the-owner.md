# The ruling, 2026-08-21

> **owner: ратификация принимается. Хороший механизм. Тоже вводим.**

**Every line a session is about to attribute to the owner is listed and put to him before the retro
file is written.** He answers each with yes, no, or a correction. Built the same day as step 5 of
`/retro`, which pushed the writing of the retro file and the handoff down by one
([[the-retro-step]]).

**Two shapes, and the difference is the whole of it:** *he said it* is written verbatim, in Russian,
under `**owner:**`; *you understood it* is written in the session's own voice and is **never
signed**.

**A line he did not answer is `could-not-close:`**, never a silent promotion to his word. The
failure direction is the safe one — an unratified line in the session's own voice is merely weaker
than it might have been, while a paraphrase signed `owner:` is a rule nobody voted for
([[a-question-asked-is-not-a-question-answered]]).

**Rejected: relying on the language test alone.** A check separates a quote from a paraphrase by its
alphabet; only the owner separates a faithful paraphrase from a wrong one
([[the-quote-is-cyrillic-rule-stands-as-an-advisory-and-stays-out-of-the-core]]).

# Why it sits in the retro and not in a script

**It is the one ritual where the owner is already in the conversation.** A check cannot ask him and
a librarian never meets him ([[a-librarian-answer-that-stops-at-the-agent-boundary-is-not-an-answer-the-owner-has]]).

**It runs before the retro file, not after.** `/handoff` builds the journal from that file and
`/ingest` builds pages from the journal, so an attribution left unratified here is a page tomorrow.
This is the last point at which a wrong sentence costs a question rather than a supersession
([[the-closing-ritual-produces-what-the-journal-cannot-hold]]).

# What it is answering

**A rule the owner never gave decided three things and was one turn from deciding a fourth**
([[a-hook-refuses-on-a-measured-condition-a-reliable-measurement-and-a-refusal-that-says-how-to-clear-it]],
[[a-paraphrase-signed-owner-becomes-a-quote-and-is-reasoned-from-as-his-word]]).

# The table admits two shapes and its first run found a third

**Nine rulings of that day were made by the owner choosing among options the session had authored** —
his decision, the session's words, and neither row of the table fits it. Put to him at the retro and
not answered, so the table stands as built
([[a-ratification-row-for-a-ruling-the-owner-chose-and-the-session-worded]]).
