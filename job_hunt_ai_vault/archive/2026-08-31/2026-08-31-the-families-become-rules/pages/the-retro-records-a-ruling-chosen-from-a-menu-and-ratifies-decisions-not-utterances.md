# The ruling, 2026-08-22

**Owner selected: the third attribution shape**, on his own question at the retro. The ratification
table had admitted two shapes — *he said it*, verbatim and in Russian under `owner:`, and *you
understood it*, written in the session's voice and never signed
([[the-retro-ratifies-every-line-before-it-is-attributed-to-the-owner]]). **A ruling taken from a
rendered menu is neither**, and it is now written as its own thing.

**The form is a line beginning `owner selected:`, carrying the option's label verbatim** — plus what
it was chosen **against**, where that clause changes the meaning. **It is not a quotation and is
never written in quotation marks**, and it is not a paraphrase either.

**The ground is a property rather than a courtesy.** The defect that produced the struck rule was
never that the words were English or session-written — it was that **nothing ever showed him the
wording** ([[a-paraphrase-signed-owner-becomes-a-quote-and-is-reasoned-from-as-his-word]]). An option
label was on his screen when he took it, so there is no interpretation step between his decision and
the sentence, and nothing in it can drift.

**A page compiled from such a line is `basis: human` and says that the wording is not his.** The
ruling ranks as his; the sentence carrying it ranks as the session's.

## And step 5 stops ratifying what settles nothing

**Only decisions are brought for signature.** The session of this date brought him a
session-opening question — *«что сегодня делаем?»* — for ratification beside seven real rulings, and
**a list padded with nothing is read less carefully**, which is the whole cost of the step going
soft ([[an-alarm-that-fires-when-nothing-is-wrong-is-one-you-learn-not-to-read]]).

# The measurement the shape exists for

**Of ten owner utterances in this session, two were his own words** — *«что сегодня делаем?»* and
*«согласен с рекомендациями»* — **and neither settles anything. All eight rulings arrived as menu
selections.** He raised the gap himself.

**So on this session the two rows of the old table covered no ruling at all.** A shape that fits
none of the decisions actually taken is not a rare case to be handled later; it was the whole of
what happened that day.

# Its own defect, written beside it rather than after it

**The alternatives were the session's.** The label is safe because he read it, and the *set* he read
it from was authored by the party that wanted an answer — which is the objection the retired
proposal already made against this shape and which is not repaired by adopting it
([[a-question-shaped-like-a-proposal-was-executed-as-a-ratified-design]]).

**It was demonstrated on the day it was ratified.** He selected three options at once, because the
set did not contain what he meant — read as a union without asking, which is recorded as a
divergence of its own
([[a-multi-select-whose-options-contradicted-each-other-was-read-as-a-union]]).

# What it does not fix, and what still has to

**An alphabet separates a quotation from a paraphrase; a menu separates a ruling from an
invention.** Neither says whether the option he was shown was the right set of options, and nothing
mechanical can ([[the-quote-is-cyrillic-rule-stands-as-an-advisory-and-stays-out-of-the-core]],
[[a-block-attributed-to-the-owner-is-written-in-his-own-russian]]).

**Rule 14 of the gate does not see these lines and must not.** It reads blockquote lines beginning
`> **owner`, which is the shape of a quotation; an `owner selected:` line is prose and is not one
([[an-advisory-prints-one-line-and-sorts-what-it-names]]).

# Which acts get a menu at all was decided later the same day

**A menu is for a choice; a list he must confirm or correct is enumerated prose in chat**
([[a-list-the-owner-must-confirm-is-prose-in-chat-and-not-a-rendered-menu]]). **This page is
unchanged by that** — it says how a ruling taken from a menu is written down, and the form
`owner selected:` stands wherever a menu was the right instrument.

**What the later ruling adds is a boundary this page did not draw.** The attribution shape was
admitted on the ground that an option label was on his screen when he took it; **that argument says
nothing about how often a menu should be the thing on his screen**, and on the day it was admitted
every one of his rulings came through one.

# The opposite case, measured — 2026-08-26

**Zero menus were asked in a session that took thirty-six decisions.** Every ruling arrived in the
owner's own prose ([[the-session-dialogue-extract]]).

**So the framing defect the `owner selected` shape carries — that the alternatives were the
session's — applies only to the recommendations he was answering, and never to a list he could not
see past.** That distinction is what the counter is for, and this is the first day it reads zero at
scale.

**The retro's own step 2 still names `AskUserQuestion`**, and it was answered in enumerated prose,
which is recorded as a divergence rather than quietly
([[divergence-the-retros-menu-step-was-answered-in-enumerated-prose]],
[[a-list-the-owner-must-confirm-is-prose-in-chat-and-not-a-rendered-menu]]).

**A session whose every ruling came through a menu is a session he did not frame. This is the
mirror, and it is worth the same line.**

# The largest ratification yet, and the count that drifted before it — 2026-08-26

> **owner:** «Твой список подписываю»

**Thirty-four attributed decisions ratified without correction**, each carrying the shape it was
recorded under — *the owner said it*, *the owner selected it*, or *the session understood it* — and
none promoted on the way through.

**The session announced twenty-six ratification lines and then wrote thirty-four.** The count was
stated before the list was finished. **The corrected figure was given in the same message rather than
the first one being left to stand**, which is this store's own repair for a number in prose
([[a-stated-count-drifts-from-the-list-it-heads]],
[[a-drifting-list-is-repaired-by-deleting-it-not-extending-it]]).

**A blanket ratification does not turn an understanding into an utterance.** Thirty-four lines signed
at once are thirty-four decisions of the shapes they already had; the signature ratifies the list, and
the shape of each line is what it was when it was written.
