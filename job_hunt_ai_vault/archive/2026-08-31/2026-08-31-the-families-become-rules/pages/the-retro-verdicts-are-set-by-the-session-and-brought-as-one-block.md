# The ruling, 2026-08-29

**owner:** «проставь вердикты сам и покажи, а я приму или откажу с уточнением»

**The session works the retro list alone, sets a verdict on every line, and brings the whole thing as
one block.** He accepts or refuses, with a correction where he refuses.

**Rejected: working the list with him, line by line.** That is the shape the retro had been run in, and
it spends his turns on lines he would have accepted.

**It is the same shape the ratification sweep already has** — one decision over a list shown whole,
rather than a signature per block
([[the-ratification-is-one-decision-over-a-shown-list-and-not-a-signature-per-block]],
[[a-sweep-may-run-inside-a-session-when-the-list-is-assembled-in-advance-and-shown-whole]]).

# What the list came to, this time

**Sixteen lines: 5 answered, 10 deferred, 1 could not be closed.** **Nine of the ten deferrals name a
destination that does not exist yet** — six new S3 items, two librarian writes and one existing item.

**Today's rulings outran the sprint list**, which is what that ratio means. It does not mean they are
lost: each names where it is picked up.

**The ratification list was twenty-three lines**, of which 19 came from `decision:` marks the feedback
channel filed during the turn, 2 were the session's own understanding and are not attributed to him,
and **4 the marks missed and the session found by reading the session's own course** — keeping the
expensive `--only` case pair, tying log rotation to the sprint close, dropping the `rules/` page for the
case list, and the plan approval itself.

**So in-turn marking caught roughly five of every six rulings.** It does not measure what neither the
marks nor the reading caught ([[speak-the-feedback-in-the-turn-and-the-hook-files-it]]).

# The gap this exposed in the channel

**Approving a plan through `ExitPlanMode` is a ruling over every decision that plan carries** — forty of
them here — **and nothing marks it.** The feedback channel records a ruling made in prose and a ruling
made from a menu, and has no shape for a ruling made by approving a document
([[the-retro-records-a-ruling-chosen-from-a-menu-and-ratifies-decisions-not-utterances]]).
