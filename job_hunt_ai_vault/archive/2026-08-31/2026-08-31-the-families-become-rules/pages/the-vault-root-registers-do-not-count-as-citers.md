# The narrow question, settled 2026-08-29

**`index.md`, `tags.md`, `sprint.md` and `log.md` do not count as live pages naming a slug.** A page
whose only mention is in one of the four is **not** cited, and the archiver's precondition refuses it.

**Rejected: counting them**, on the ground that **`sprint.md` names half the corpus** — an item's line
routinely names the page that answered it, so counting the register would make the precondition
identically true and the guard could never refuse
([[a-citation-is-not-a-test-in-a-corpus-this-densely-linked]]).

**It mattered on the day the sweep first ran**, because the precondition it feeds is what stands
between a page and leaving the store unreachable
([[the-sweep-runs-for-real-over-every-criterion-and-records-what-it-moved]]).

# The wider idea it came out of, which is his and is not built

**owner:** «в идеале эти реестры должны рендерится. То есть иметь свою страницу rule в vault, которая
рендерить их полностью или частично. В таком случае упоминание будет идти на страницу, а не на реестр.»

**Each of the four registers would be rendered from a `wiki/rules/` page of its own**, wholly or in
part, so a mention lands on a page rather than on a register — and the question above would dissolve
rather than be answered ([[the-vault-root-registers-render-from-rule-pages-of-their-own]]).

**Not today's build**, and it is why the narrow ruling exists: it settles what the archiver needs now
without prejudging the design.

# What it obliges while it stands

**A page kept alive only by a register row is not kept alive.** If nothing in `wiki/` points at it, it
may not leave — and a librarian looking for the live citer that releases a page must find one outside
the four ([[a-bare-slug-mention-is-an-inbound-link-that-no-wikilink-counter-sees]]).

**`authority.md` is a fifth register and is not in the list**, because it writes slugs as plain text and
deliberately not as `[[wikilinks]]` — it was never a citer to begin with
([[authority-md-is-a-register-the-one-writer-clause-names]]).
