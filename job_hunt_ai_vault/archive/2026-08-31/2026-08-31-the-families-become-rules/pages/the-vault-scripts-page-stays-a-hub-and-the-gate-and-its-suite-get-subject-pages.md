# The ruling, 2026-08-25

> **owner:** «согласен с тобой. страница остается хабом, а две новые страницу получают предметное
> описание без истории, которая была.»

**[[vault-scripts]] stays where it is and becomes a hub.** The dated sections — what changed when, and
why — stay on it.

**The gate and its selftest suite get a page each, carrying the subject description alone.** No
history, no dated blocks: what the thing is, what it reads, what it refuses.

# It moves no address, and that is measured

**79 pages under `wiki/` link to `[[vault-scripts]]`, 107 occurrences.** The ruling keeps the filename
and the slug exactly as they are, so nothing rewrites an inbound link and the rewriter-and-detector
pair is not needed ([[a-rewriter-must-be-paired-with-a-detector]], [[a-page-rename-is-sed-and-verify]]).

# The register already carries the division the page does not

**`authority.md` gives the gate 15 slugs and the selftest of the vault's own scripts 15 slugs**,
counted off the register. **`vault-scripts.md` says ten and thirteen, and the register's own note on
the second family says thirteen** — three stale copies of two lists, corrected on the hub with a date
rather than patched ([[a-drifting-list-is-repaired-by-deleting-it-not-extending-it]]).

# Not done

**Neither page exists.** No `entities/` page was written at this ingest, because a page describing a
mechanism is written when the mechanism is there to describe and the split is not the mechanism
([[an-ingest-of-a-session-that-built-nothing-writes-decisions-and-proposals]]). **The eighteen-condition
list the owner ordered deleted is therefore still live on the hub.**

# Re-affirmed against the cycle he had just described — 2026-08-26

> **owner:** «оставить то решение, которое было вчера... ТАк будет проще - это практически
> единственный критерий почему так»

**[[vault-scripts]] stays a hub and keeps its slug.** He had just described the lessons cycle, which
would have deprecated this page and archived it
([[the-lessons-cycle-is-the-owners-and-a-lesson-leaves-when-a-page-outside-lessons-cites-it]]); he
ruled against applying it here.

**The criterion is recorded as simplicity, in his own words, rather than propped up with a
measurement found afterwards.** The 79-page link count above is a reason the ruling is cheap, not
the reason he gave.

# The eighteen-condition list moves rather than being deleted — 2026-08-26

**It leaves the live description and enters a dated history section on this same hub**, marked as
replaced by the printed `--rules` register. *(owner selected it.)* **No page is created in order to
be archived** — that was the alternative, and he refused it.

**Done at the ingest of 2026-08-26**, so the *Not done* line above is false from this date about the
list and still true about the two subject pages
([[the-date-of-a-correction-note-is-the-date-the-text-became-false]]).

**One live citation of the number survives the move and is worth naming.**
`wiki/proposals/a-rule-comparing-a-stated-count-against-its-list.md` cites *"18 failing conditions"*
as its only **passing** example. Deleting the list would have removed that example; moving it keeps
the string in the tree, and the proposal's example now points into a history section rather than
into a live description ([[a-rule-comparing-a-stated-count-against-its-list]]).

**The register the list is replaced by is `bash scripts/vault-check.sh --rules`, which printed 16
headings on 2026-08-26** — against the 15 read the day before and the 18 this page's own history
records. A printed register cannot drift; the three numbers are three copies
([[a-printed-register-cannot-drift-and-a-maintained-one-always-has]],
[[a-drifting-list-is-repaired-by-deleting-it-not-extending-it]]).
