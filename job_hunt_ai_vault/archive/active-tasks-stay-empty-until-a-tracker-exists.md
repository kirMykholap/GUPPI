# Active tasks: the boundary is stubbed, not wired

> [!WARNING] DEPRECATED 2026-08-14
> The brief no longer carries this section at all — it was removed rather than kept empty, by the
> owner's decision of 2026-08-14: [[the-brief-has-no-active-tasks-section]]. **The ownership rule
> below is unchanged and lives on in the successor**; only the empty placeholder is gone. Kept here
> because "why did the brief once have an empty section, and why did it stop" is a real question.

**Decided on 2026-08-13.** The brief carries a section for active unblocked tasks, and **it stays
empty** until a tracker — Beads is the intended one — is actually installed. Rejected: dropping the
section, and equally, filling it by hand from the plan.

The reason is the division of ownership the vault rests on: **the tracker owns task status; the
vault owns what we learned from a task.** A hand-maintained task list inside a derived artefact
would be a second copy of something that changes hourly, and it would be the copy people read.

When the tracker arrives, the brief **quotes its output verbatim** and never paraphrases it. A
paraphrase of a task list is a stale task list with a confident tone.

The section is kept rather than omitted for the same reason the handoff template keeps empty
sections ([[the-handoff-template-maps-onto-the-categories]]): a section that is absent is never noticed as missing, whereas one reading "none yet"
asks a question every time it is read. See [[journal-vault-brief-each-artefact-has-one-owner]] for the brief's place
in the loop.
