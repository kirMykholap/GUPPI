# Carried as a deferral at the retro of 2026-08-19

**The question is not "widen the page set". It is what `archive/` is** — frozen testimony like `raw/`
([[raw-holds-testimony-and-artefacts-are-frozen]]), or live retired content that the retrieval rules
still govern. `raw/` has a page saying which it is. **`archive/` does not**, and it has never been
asked.

**Deferred rather than answered**, because answering it decides the reach of several rules at once and
that is a ruling rather than a repair. Recorded here so the next session finds a question instead of a
gap ([[the-no-quantity-rule-reads-the-title-and-the-filename-and-never-the-body]] is the same
treatment applied to a boundary that *was* closed).

# The two cases pull opposite ways, which is why neither rule can be fixed alone

| rule | its reach | what falls outside |
|---|---|---|
| rule 6 — every tag on a page is defined in `tags.md` | `wiki/` only | an archived page carrying a tag the dictionary does not define passes in silence |
| rule 10 — no title or filename carries a measurable quantity | `wiki/` only | archived pages carry counts today, in titles and in slugs |

**An undefined tag in `archive/` is a real defect.** The dictionary and the corpus are supposed to
describe each other, and a page nobody can reach through its tag is exactly what the density rule is
about. **Found live**: the `schema` tag split would have left three archived pages holding a word that
no longer exists, and it was caught only because the librarian went looking rather than because
anything printed ([[the-schema-tag-splits-into-db-schema-and-record-schema]]).

**A counting title in `archive/` is arguably the rule working.**
[[the-wiki-has-five-folders-and-tasks-is-not-one]] is archived **because** its count went stale — the
title going false is what retired the page, and repairing it now would erase the evidence.

**So the same widening is right for one rule and wrong for the other**, and no amount of reasoning
about either rule alone settles it.

# What was measured, so the ruling has a size

**Four archived pages carry a count today**, by rule 10's own predicate:
[[a-region-is-checked-against-its-rules-governs]] (*fifth*), [[the-wiki-has-no-tasks-folder]]
(*four*), and two more in their slugs. Three archived pages were touched by the tag rename for the
undefined-tag case above. **These are the whole of what a ruling either way would move.**

# The consequence of leaving it

**Two rules keep disagreeing about a directory neither of them names**, and each new rule written
against `wiki/` inherits the same unasked question. The cost is not large today; it is that the
answer will eventually be settled by whichever rule someone happens to widen first, from inside a
script — which is the shape [[the-ruleclaim-branch-widens-to-any-page-carrying-authority-and-governs]]
records the other end of.

# Answered 2026-08-19, and this page is retired to `archive/`

The owner ruled the same day, and he took neither of the two options this page framed:
**what an archived page asserts is frozen; how it is found is live**
([[what-an-archived-page-asserts-is-frozen-and-how-it-is-found-is-live]]). Rule 6 widens to
`archive/`, rule 10 does not, and the deferral above is discharged.

**The measurement in this page is why the successor is short.** Four archived pages carry a count and
three were touched by the tag rename — that is the whole of what either widening moves, and it was
taken before the ruling rather than after it.
