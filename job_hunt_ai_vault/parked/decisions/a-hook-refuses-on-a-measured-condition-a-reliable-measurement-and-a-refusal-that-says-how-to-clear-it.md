# The owner struck the rule that stood here before — 2026-08-21

> **owner: не было такого правила. Это правило неправильное.**

Said of [[advisory-is-the-default-for-a-hook]], whose quote block reads *"advisory is the default
for a hook. A hook may refuse an operation only where the action is irreversible and the refusal
cannot be wrong — both conditions at once."* **Those are not his words and the rule is not his.**
They are a previous session's English paraphrase, written into a journal under `**owner:**`,
compiled into a quote stamped `basis: human`, and reasoned from as his word for three days
([[a-paraphrase-signed-owner-becomes-a-quote-and-is-reasoned-from-as-his-word]]).

**The page is struck. It is not yet struck in the tree**, and that is deliberate rather than
pending: retiring it and re-pointing the pages that take their disposition from it is the first
step of an approved plan and belongs to the session that executes the plan, `wiki/` having one
writer ([[the-librarian-writes-unattended]]).

**What takes its licence from the struck page, and must be re-argued rather than inherited:**
[[the-plan-check-hook]], [[the-librarian-lock-hook]], [[session-hooks]] and
[[the-guard-branch-lives-inside-a-hook-that-already-runs]] all cite it for their advisory status,
and the lock takes its licence to refuse from it.

# The test that replaces it

**A hook may refuse when all of these hold:**

- **the condition is measured rather than judged**;
- **that measurement is reliable at the point the hook stands**;
- **the refusal says how to clear it**.

**Where it cannot measure, it allows and says so aloud.**

**The ruling is the owner's and the wording is not his.** He chose it from options this session
authored, so it is compiled as a decision and never as a quotation — which is the distinction the
session that produced it exists to have introduced ([[the-retro-ratifies-every-line-before-it-is-attributed-to-the-owner]]).

**Rejected: the struck page's *irreversible and cannot-be-wrong*,** on the ground that the owner
never set it.

# Applied to the tree, it gives the answers already on disk

**`.claude/hooks/lock-the-librarian.ps1` refuses**, and passes all three: `vault-check.sh` exits a
code rather than an opinion, the exit code is available at the moment of the dispatch, and the
denial carries the gate's own output so the caller knows what to fix ([[the-librarian-lock-hook]]).

**`check-the-plan.ps1` and the write net inside `ask-the-vault.ps1` speak**, and the reason is now
checkable rather than a judgement about reversibility: a ledger row cannot be written in plan mode,
which is the exact moment the ritual is placed, so the measurement is unreliable where those hooks
stand ([[the-plan-check-hook]], [[the-pre-task-ledger]]).

**Same answers, different grounds.** That agreement is the argument for the test rather than
evidence that nothing changed: the struck test decided those three things on a clause nobody ever
gave.

# What it obliges

- **A new hook is advisory unless its page argues all three conditions.** The argument is on a page,
  never in the script's header ([[no-rule-reads-a-harness-file]]).
- **A refusal that cannot say how to clear itself is not licensed**, whatever else it satisfies.
  This clause is new here and has no predecessor in the struck page.
- **The register of which hook refuses is a test, not a sentence** — `scripts/hook-selftest.ps1`
  ([[a-refusal-is-possible-on-pretooluse-alone-and-the-register-is-a-test]]).
- **Only the first condition is mechanical.** The selftest asserts the disposition and not the
  reasoning, which is why the other two stay a page and a conversation.
