# Context

**The session of 2026-08-30 read the previous handoff — 56,821 bytes, about 15,000 tokens — and
almost nothing in it was unique.** The two cost figures were already in the brief and the owner's
strongest verdict was already in `.superpowers/sdd/vault-feedback.md`.

**Nothing in `brief.md` said the handoff need not be read**, and nothing in the vault said so either.
**The absence is the defect, not the session's curiosity**: brief plus feedback is 28 KB against 85 KB
for the same knowledge.

# The question

**What does a session inherit?**

# How it works after

**The brief plus `.superpowers/sdd/vault-feedback.md`, and nothing else.** The handoff of the previous
session is not read.

**owner:** «Все должно работать таким образом, чтобы ты не читал handoff прошлой сесии. Бриф уже
говорит что было сделано и что библиотекарь прокоментировал. Единственное, что может остаться
незамеченным - это vault-feedback.md»

**The feedback file is named as the one leak**, and it is a real one: it has now carried lines past a
`/handoff` drain on three consecutive sessions, because `/ingest` files into it after `/handoff` has
already emptied it.

**This obliges the brief to be sufficient.** Anything a session needs that the brief does not carry is
a miss against the librarian, not a reason to open the journal.

# Recommendation

**State it**, since the corpus said neither yes nor no and the silence read as permission.

# Ratification

**The owner said it**, on 2026-08-30, in his own words, quoted above.
