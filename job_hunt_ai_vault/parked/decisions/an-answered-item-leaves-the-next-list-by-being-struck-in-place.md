# The ruling, 2026-08-26

*(the owner selected it, against leaving an answered item on the list until a separate decision
removes it.)*

**An item that has been answered is struck through where it stands, with one line naming the page the
answer lives on.** It is not deleted and it is not left standing as if it were open.

**The precedent is his own**, from 2026-08-25, when two S3 lines were struck the same way.

# Why the strike rather than a deletion or a silence

**A deleted item takes its history with it** — the next session cannot tell an item that was answered
from one that was never written, which is the same ground on which no page here is ever removed
([[what-an-archived-page-asserts-is-frozen-and-how-it-is-found-is-live]]).

**An answered item left standing costs a reading every session.** Two of ten items on the 2026-08-26
list turned out to be rubbish, and both were found by opening the pages rather than by reading the
list ([[a-recommendation-given-without-checking-its-subject-is-delivered-like-one-that-was-checked]]).

# Rejected

**Rejected: leaving each answered item until a decision of its own removes it.** That is the state
the list was in, and it is why a carried list reached twenty-five items before anyone read it whole.

# The form used on 2026-08-28 is wider than this rule, and nobody ratified the widening

**Twenty-three items were struck that day in a form naming both the page the answer lives on and the
artefact in the tree.** This rule asks for the page.

**The extension is already in twenty-three lines**, and the next session to strike an item will copy
the lines rather than read the rule — which is how a practice quietly outvotes a written rule here,
a class this store has recorded before ([[a-drifting-list-is-repaired-by-deleting-it-not-extending-it]]).

**It is raised, not resolved.** Either the wider form is ratified or the lines are narrowed, and both
are the owner's call rather than the librarian's.

**The strike pass itself is the one that clobbered three items and was repaired**
([[independent-verification-caught-what-a-self-report-would-not-have]]).
