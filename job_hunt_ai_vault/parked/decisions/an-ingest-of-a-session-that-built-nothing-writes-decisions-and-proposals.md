# The question he asked, and it had no answer anywhere in the store

> **owner:** «Мне больше интересно как бибилиотекарь сйечас заведет нашу сессию? План написан, а
> действий нет. Не получится ли так, что вики будет опережать действия?»

**The category definitions imply the answer and no page stated it.** `decisions/` and `proposals/`
are legitimate with no code behind them; `entities/` and `concepts/` describe what exists.

# The ruling, 2026-08-25

> **owner:** «3) Да, и сказать библиотекарю явно: только decisions/ и proposals/, ни одной страницы
> в entities/ или concepts/, описывающей механизм, которого нет. 4) разделить»

**Where a session built nothing, the ingest writes `decisions/` and `proposals/` and no page in
`entities/` or `concepts/` describing a mechanism that does not exist.** A `concepts/` page about a
mechanism that **already exists** and was merely understood that session is legitimate, and is
verified against the tree before it is written.

**And the output splits by who produced it.** What the owner *ruled* becomes a `decisions/` page and
is true whether or not code exists. What the *session designed* becomes a `proposals/` page.

# Why the split is the safe direction

**A decision changed later is a supersession, and a proposal is cheap.** Filing a design as a
decision buys a retirement the first time the build disagrees with it; filing a ruling as a proposal
loses the fact that it was decided.

# The failure it guards against is on the record

**A note of intent compiled as if it were testimony produces pages describing things nobody
built**, and the cost is not a bad page — it is a rule that never existed being obeyed
([[an-intent-note-compiled-as-testimony-describes-things-nobody-built]]). That page is about the
owner's notes; this ruling extends the same discipline to a journal whose session wrote a plan and
no code.

# What it obliges at the ingest

**Every `concepts/` page written from such a journal is checked against the tree first**, and a
mechanism the plan describes is named as unbuilt on the decision or proposal that carries it.
