# The ruling, 2026-08-26

> **owner:** «Вообще нам стоит обсудить механизм архивации для каждой страницы вики. Потому что
> сейчас - это бесконечный рост, так быть не должно.»

**Archiving policy for every page type in the wiki is a named topic for the next session** — not an
item to be designed by whoever gets there first.

# What is already settled, and what the topic is over

**Settled: `lessons/`.** A lesson leaves when a page outside `lessons/` cites it
([[the-lessons-cycle-is-the-owners-and-a-lesson-leaves-when-a-page-outside-lessons-cites-it]]).

**Settled: `proposals/`.** `implemented` and `refused` both leave for `archive/`, each with its own
requirement ([[an-implemented-proposal-moves-to-archive-and-the-successor-carries-its-claim]],
[[a-refused-proposal-is-archived-and-takes-status-refused]]).

**Unsettled: `decisions/`, `concepts/`, `entities/`, `rules/`** — four folders holding the large
majority of the corpus, with no criterion at all for leaving. `decisions/` alone stands at 287
pages on 2026-08-26.

# Why it is a discussion rather than a session's design

**Every criterion available cuts the wrong way somewhere.** A decision that is superseded already
leaves by the supersession procedure; one that is merely old is exactly what `/pre-task` has to keep
reading. **An entity taken out of service is deprecated, not removed** — the store's own clause, in
the vault's `CLAUDE.md`, and nothing is ever deleted here
([[what-an-archived-page-asserts-is-frozen-and-how-it-is-found-is-live]]). So the question is not
*how to move a page* but *what makes a live page stop being live*, and that is a judgement about the
project.

**The growth is the evidence and it is not disputed.** 653 live pages under `wiki/` on 2026-08-26,
against 50 in `archive/`.

# What it obliges

**One line on the sprint, and no design written before the conversation.** A session that arrives at
this topic with a proposal already drafted has answered a question the owner reserved for a
discussion ([[the-owner-decides-how-a-slice-is-executed]]).
