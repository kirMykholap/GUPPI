put back to you"*, and put a question to him that he had already closed.

# What this says about the store rather than about Beads

brief is derived and re-asserts what the corpus holds, so an uncompiled answer is re-raised at every
ingest until somebody notices ([[chat-is-not-a-channel-that-survives-the-session]]).

**It is filed as a miss against the librarian as well as a decision here**, because the brief is the
librarian's output and the false sentence was in it.
