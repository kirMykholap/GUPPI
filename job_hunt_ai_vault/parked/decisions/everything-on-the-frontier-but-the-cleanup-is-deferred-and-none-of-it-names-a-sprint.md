# Context

**The session opened with a frontier and the owner cleared all of it but one item.** The cleanup of
the vault was the day's work; everything else moved.

**Items five to ten of that frontier had been deferred before.** Deferring them again is a repeated
deferral, and the count of deferrals in this store that name no sprint went **sixteen to
twenty-four** on the same day.

# The question

**Which frontier items does the session work, and what happens to the rest?**

# How it works after

**One item is worked and the rest are deferred to the next session.** They stay on `sprint.md`, which
is where a deferred item lands and where the frontier lives between sessions
([[the-frontier-is-one-concept-with-three-carriers]]).

**None of the deferred items names a sprint**, and the same is true of the sweep, the family rules,
the paired-`authority:` requirement and the `Read` matcher — every deferral this day produced.

# Recommendation

**Record the deferral and record what it does to the count**, because the count is the only thing that
makes a repeated deferral visible at all. The alternative — recording the deferral and not the
repetition — is how the number reached twenty-four without anyone deciding it should.

**Rejected: taking more of the frontier**, which he closed himself.

# Ratification

**owner, 2026-08-30: «Все остальные мы переносим.»**

# It stands against a live prohibition, and the destination that rule asks for was removed the same day

> [!NOTE] RAISED, NOT RESOLVED — the `authority:` slugs differ, so rule 3 of the gate cannot see this
> and no `CONTRADICTION` block is legal for it.

**[[a-repeated-deferral-names-the-sprint-it-happens-in-or-is-refused]]** (`repeated-deferral`, live
prohibition, `basis: human`, 2026-08-25) says an item deferred again must name the sprint it happens
in or be refused, and that *"there is no further deferral with no destination in time."*

**Twenty-four items now stand against it, and this session added eight of them.**

**And the destination it asks for was taken away the same day.**
[[the-cleanup-is-a-forced-session-outside-any-sprint]] rules that the cleanup — which is what most of
these items are waiting on — belongs to no sprint. **A rule demanding that a deferral name a sprint
cannot be satisfied by work that is ruled to have none**, so the rule is broken by construction from
2026-08-30 onward unless one of the two is narrowed. **Neither is narrowed here.**

# The deferrals name S3 now — 2026-08-31

**owner selected, 2026-08-31: «согласен».** The twenty-four deferrals that named no sprint name **S3**,
and are worked in the session after the one that ratified it. That closes the construction this page
describes: a deferral had to name a sprint, the work was ruled to have none, and the two could not both
hold.

**What made it closeable is the narrow reading of "outside any sprint"** — the cleanup is not counted
to S3 but is governed by the rules like any work
([[the-cleanup-is-a-forced-session-outside-any-sprint]]). With that, a deferral arising in the cleanup
names the open sprint like any other, and neither rule needed narrowing after all.

**The consequence if the next session does not work them:**
[[a-repeated-deferral-names-the-sprint-it-happens-in-or-is-refused]] stays unsatisfied for a ninth
brief. **That is a count of briefs and not of days**, and it is the only instrument this store has on
whether a deferral is ever worked.
