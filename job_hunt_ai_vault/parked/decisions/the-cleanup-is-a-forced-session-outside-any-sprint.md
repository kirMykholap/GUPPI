# Context

**Work in this project belongs to a sprint**, and the rendered text in the repository's `CLAUDE.md`
says so in one sentence: *"Work outside an open sprint is work nobody agreed to."*

**The session of 2026-08-30 was opened by the owner as an emergency**, going around the rules, to cut
a corpus he had said was too large to read. **S3 is open**, and none of its four acceptance criteria
is about the size of the store.

**The session carried the procedural question from its first reply** — does this work go onto S3, or
does it stand outside — and worked most of the day without an answer.

**Files this touches:** `job_hunt_ai_vault/sprint.md`,
`job_hunt_ai_vault/wiki/rules/the-map-in-claude-md-is-the-vault-its-librarian-and-the-sprint.md`.

# The question

**Does the cleanup enter S3, or does it stand outside every sprint?**

# How it works after

**It stands outside. The sprint does not take part in it**, and nothing the cleanup produces is
weighed against an S3 criterion.

**What that closes**: the session stops attributing its work to a sprint whose goal it does not serve,
and `sprint.md` records the day as work outside the sprint rather than as work on it.

**What it does not close, and this page will not decide it: whether "outside any sprint" means "not
counted to S3" or "governed by no sprint at all".** S3 is open — `sprint.md` reads `Closed: —` — so
the two readings are not the same and they give opposite answers about whether the standing rule was
suspended for a day or merely not applied. **The owner's sentence supports either. Raised in the
brief and chosen by nobody here.**

# Recommendation

**Record it and route the item into `sprint.md` as work outside the sprint.** A day of work with no
recorded standing is the shape that puts a session's output where nothing accounts for it, which is
what the frontier record exists to prevent.

**Rejected: folding the cleanup into S3**, which would have added a fifth criterion nobody wrote to a
sprint already carrying four unclosed ones.

# It disagrees with a live proposal and with a rendered rule

> [!NOTE] RAISED, NOT RESOLVED — the `authority:` slugs differ, so rule 3 of the gate cannot see the
> first of these, and the second is a rule, which carries no slug at all.

**[[a-gate-that-refuses-work-without-an-open-sprint]]** (`sprint-gate-refusal`, live, `decided_by:`
present) exists to make exactly this impossible: *"Nothing stopped the session of 2026-08-17 from
working a whole day outside a sprint, and nothing would stop the next one. The owner named that
failure the same day."* This ruling makes a class of session legitimate that the proposal was written
to refuse. Whether it refutes the proposal or leaves it untouched **depends on which reading of
"outside any sprint" is meant**, which is the question above.

**And the rendered slice of
[[the-map-in-claude-md-is-the-vault-its-librarian-and-the-sprint]] is in the repository's `CLAUDE.md`
right now saying the opposite.** A rule carries no `authority:` so this is not a contradiction by the
letter; it is a delivered sentence that a ruling of the same store has just contradicted, and it is
the session's file rather than the librarian's.

# The reading this page refused to choose is chosen — 2026-08-31

**"What it does not close, and this page will not decide it: whether 'outside any sprint' means 'not
counted to S3' or 'governed by no sprint at all'."** He gave the narrow one.

**owner selected, 2026-08-31: «согласен».** The cleanup is **not counted to S3** and **is governed by
the rules like any other work.** Its output is weighed against no acceptance criterion; every standing
rule applies to it unchanged.

**Rejected: the wide reading.** It would have suspended the rules for a day — and the price was
concrete rather than theoretical: a live rule and a slice rendered into the repository's `CLAUDE.md`
would have been unenforceable for the duration, on the same day the session was writing nineteen rule
pages.

**The `RAISED, NOT RESOLVED` marker above is discharged by this section** and is struck as answered
rather than deleted. The question it named is the one this answers.

**And the deferrals that named no sprint now name S3.** Twenty-four of them, worked next session — the
count and its consequence live on
[[everything-on-the-frontier-but-the-cleanup-is-deferred-and-none-of-it-names-a-sprint]], which is the
page that exists to keep them visible.
