# Context

**Three live pages used the word *frontier* for three things, and two of them recorded that choosing
between them was the owner's.**
[[the-frontier-is-a-general-working-concept-and-a-sessions-list-lives-in-the-brief]] put a session's
list in `brief.md` and **rejected `sprint.md` by name**;
[[the-output-side-is-finished-today-and-does-not-close-the-corpuss-growth]] said the remaining
frontier is written on `sprint.md` and on proposal pages; and [[grill-me-skill]] used it for the
boundary of questions inside an interview. The slugs differ, so rule 3 of the gate could not see the
disagreement and no `CONTRADICTION` block was legal for it.

**That is the shape that cost this store three days over *retrospective***
([[a-retrospective-is-a-sessions-end-not-a-sprints-ritual]]).

# The question

**Are these three frontiers, or one frontier with three carriers — and if one, which file holds it
between sessions?**

# How it works after

**One concept. Three carriers, and they are not interchangeable.**

- **`sprint.md` is the frontier between sessions**, and it is where a deferred pointer lands.
- **`brief.md` is its view at session start**, **generated from `sprint.md`** and never written by
  hand. The brief is derived and is never a source, so a section typed into it is gone at the next
  ingest and was never anywhere else.
- **`grill-me` extends it** with what an interview opens.

**It is set at session start, may grow or shrink during the session by an explicit deferral, and is
walked twice** — before the plan is written and again at the retro, never at the retro alone. **Every
item not discussed is marked deferred**, in the chat, through the feedback channel. `/pre-task` then
runs only over what was discussed.

**The definition renders into `CLAUDE.md` as part of the reply-format rule**, on the owner's
instruction, rather than as a rule page of its own
([[the-reply-is-a-report-answers-and-one-case]]).

**This reverses the predecessor's rejection of `sprint.md`.** That page ruled the sprint a different
object — criteria and volume of work — and put the session's list in `brief.md` alone. The reversal
is knowing: `brief.md` keeps the *view* and loses the *storage*, which is what makes the section
generable at all.

# Recommendation

**One concept with named carriers, rather than three words or one file.** Three words would have to
be minted and taught; one file cannot be both derived and durable.

# Ratification

**The owner said it**, on 2026-08-30, in his own words, and both pages carrying the collision had
already recorded that the choice was his. He also fixed the walking twice separately: «Мы еще должны
учитывать фронтир перед написанием плана в начале сессии, а не только на retro».

# "Before the plan is written" is narrowed to "before the plan is shown" — 2026-08-31

**The clause above says the frontier is walked before the plan is written.** That is satisfiable by a
session that walks it, leaves five items undiscussed, and calls `ExitPlanMode` anyway. **He interrupted
exactly that.**

**owner, 2026-08-31:** «мы еще не закрыли фронтир вопросов. Зачем ты показываешь план? НАм нужно
закончить фронтир»

**So the frontier is *closed* before a plan is shown, and closed means every item has a status** —
discussed, ratified, or explicitly deferred. An item nobody spoke about is not closed by the session
marking it deferred at the end; the deferral is a thing he agrees to. **Rejected: presenting a plan
while any frontier item is undiscussed.**

**The narrowing is about the gate, not the count.** Walking the list twice is unchanged and so are the
three carriers. What changed is that the first walk now has a completion condition and `ExitPlanMode`
is where it is tested.

**The rendered slice is not amended by this ingest.**
[[the-reply-is-a-report-answers-and-one-case]] carries the frontier text into the repository's
`CLAUDE.md`, and its wording still says *"before the plan is written"*. Narrowing a slice changes a
file every session reads, and the journal records this as a decision rather than as a harness change.
**It is raised in the brief as owed text rather than written into the slice here.**
