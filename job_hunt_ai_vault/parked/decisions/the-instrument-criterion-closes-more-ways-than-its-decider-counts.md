# The defect, found at the retro of the second session of 2026-08-23

**Acceptance criterion 1 of S3 is decided by two suites reporting `missed 0` with a case per new
instrument.** That command counts **selftest cases** and nothing else.

**The criterion admits closures the command cannot see.** A rule with no instrument is closed by any
of these:

| how it closes | what the decider sees |
|---|---|
| an instrument is built | a case, counted |
| a page says why no instrument is possible | nothing |
| the owner watches it himself | nothing |

**The third arrived on the same day**, when he made himself the watcher of the style rule
([[the-style-rules-watcher-is-the-owner-and-it-governs-chat-alone]]). The second was already on the
record when the criterion was written.

# The ruling: the command changes, not the criterion

**The owner ruled that the decider is what is fixed.** It must count all three closures, or the
criterion can never read as met however much work is done — every rule closed by a page or by him
subtracts from the numerator and adds to nothing.

**Chosen against rewording the criterion** to mean only what the command already counts. That would
have made the sprint's goal smaller to fit its instrument, which is the inversion this sprint exists
to remove: a claim the vault makes about itself is decided by a command, and the command is the part
that is supposed to follow the claim.

# What it obliges

- **The command is not written here.** It is a harness item and the two suites are the session's to
  change, not the librarian's ([[what-the-gate-reads-as-its-own-input-is-the-sessions-to-write]]).
- **Until it lands, no reading of criterion 1 is taken off the suites' output.** The two counts are
  the state of the tree; whether the criterion is met is the owner's and nothing else's
  ([[a-sprint-closes-on-its-goal]]).
- **The list of rules owing an instrument stays the register of what is open**
  ([[every-rule-in-wiki-rules-owes-a-mechanical-instrument]]), and it now has to record *which* of
  the three closures each rule took, or the new command has nothing to read.
