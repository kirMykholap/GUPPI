# Context

**The retro of 2026-08-30 opened twenty-seven questions** — sixteen from the session's own feedback
marks and eleven found by reading the dialogue extract whole.

**Three ways of working that list were on the table**: the session works it and brings back only what
needs his word; `grill-me` interrogates him through it; or the list is not discussed at all and every
item is deferred.

# The question

**Who works the retro's open list?**

# How it works after

**The session works it, and the items only he can answer are carved out and put to him.** Seven were
carved out this time.

**The gate at the end read twenty-seven questions: nineteen answered, eight deferred with a
destination, none unclosable.** Of the deferred, six went to the next session, one to the sprint list
and one to the librarian's feedback.

**The carve-out is what the ruling is about.** Working the list is cheap; deciding which rows are the
owner's is the judgement, and doing it wrongly in either direction is what the other two options fail
at — `grill-me` asks him everything, and no conversation asks him nothing.

# Recommendation

**The session recommended this and framed all three options.** Its ground was that most of the
twenty-seven were questions about the store's own machinery with an answer readable off the tree, and
that putting those to him spends the one resource the retro is short of.

**Rejected: `grill-me`, and rejected: no conversation at all.**

# Ratification

**owner selected, 2026-08-30: «второй».** He chose from a list the session composed, so the wording is
the session's and none of it is signed as his
([[the-retro-records-a-ruling-chosen-from-a-menu-and-ratifies-decisions-not-utterances]]). The
recommendation he took included the carve-out of seven items and was accepted with it.

# Confirmed unchanged on 2026-08-31, and the confirmation is a line rather than a page

**owner, 2026-08-31: «пройди список и принеси выводы».** The retro list was worked by the session,
which brought conclusions, against `grill-me` and against no conversation at all — the same ruling with
the same two rejected alternatives.

**No second page was written for it.** A ruling restated is a line on the page that holds it; a copy
would be the drifting list this store repairs by deletion.

**One thing that ruling licensed is now decided in its own right.** The session read "the verdicts are
set by the session and brought as one block" off this page and its neighbour, against the prose of
`brief.md` and `sprint.md`, which said the owner writes them. **That inference is now a decision rather
than a reading** ([[the-cleanup-verdict-names-which-delivery-a-family-gets]]).
