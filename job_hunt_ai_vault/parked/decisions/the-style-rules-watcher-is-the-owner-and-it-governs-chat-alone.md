# Two rulings, both his, taken at the retro of the second session of 2026-08-23

> **owner: сторож - я**

> **owner: Правило стиля только для общения - это правильно**

**The first answers who catches a violation.** The style rule rendered into the repository's
`CLAUDE.md` has no mechanical instrument and is not getting one; the owner reads the prose and says
when it is wrong ([[write-plainly-name-the-source-and-say-what-is-not-done]]).

**The second answers what it reaches.** It governs the prose a session writes *for him* — chat, the
plan he approves, the answer to a question. It does **not** govern what goes into `wiki/`. The
librarian works to this store's own rules, which are a different set with a different reader.

# Why the scope question was even open

**The proposal that carried the rule raised it and it was never put to him.** The page asks whether
the rule should also bind the vault's prose and leaves it open in both directions
([[a-style-rule-for-the-prose-a-session-writes]]). Between the ratification and this retro nothing
answered it, so the rule rendered into `CLAUDE.md` with an unanswered question about half its
subject.

**The two bodies of prose are written for different readers and measured differently.** His prose is
read once, by him, and its test is whether he can act on it. A page here is read by an agent that
arrived through a grep, months later, and its tests are the ones this store already has — the title
rule, the no-quantity rule, the dated correction, the frozen source. A rule tuned for the first
would fight the second.

# What each ruling is chosen against

- **Against an instrument.** The session did not offer one because prose style is a judgement no
  linter here reads; what was on offer was *a page saying no instrument is possible*, and he took a
  third thing — himself. That third closure is what the criterion's decider cannot count
  ([[the-instrument-criterion-closes-more-ways-than-its-decider-counts]],
  [[every-rule-in-wiki-rules-owes-a-mechanical-instrument]]).
- **Against extending the rule to `wiki/`.** Rejected in one sentence and it is now answered rather
  than open. Nothing has to be re-read for style, and no page here is in violation of a rule that
  never reached it.

# What it obliges

**A session writing to him applies the rule; a librarian writing a page does not.** And when the
style rule is next amended, the amendment is about chat and the plan, so the question *does this
also change the vault* has a standing answer of no until he says otherwise.

**The rule stays in the list of rules with no instrument**, and it is now one member longer than the
five that list carried ([[every-rule-in-wiki-rules-owes-a-mechanical-instrument]]).
