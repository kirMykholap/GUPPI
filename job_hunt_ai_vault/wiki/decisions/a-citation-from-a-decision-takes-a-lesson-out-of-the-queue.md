# The ruling, 2026-08-26, in his own words

> **owner:** «Если урок упомянут - значит у него есть след в архиве через живую страницу и мы до него
> дотянемся, когда будет нужно. А постоянно живые уроки в lessons - это шум, который мы будем
> постоянно читать. Мы это уже обсуждали прошлый раз.»

**Membership stays wide.** A lesson cited by a `decisions/` page is out of the queue, on the ground
that a mention is a trace: the lesson is reachable through a live page whenever it is wanted, and
that is what the queue exists to produce.

**What he is ruling against is a `lessons/` folder that stays live forever** — pages nobody retires
and everybody re-reads.

# He says this was discussed before, and the corpus does not hold the earlier record

**Reported and not resolved.** The session that heard it searched and found no earlier ruling; this
ingest searched `wiki/` and `archive/` for a prior decision on queue membership and found the
mechanism pages ([[the-lessons-queue-is-the-owners-design-and-is-not-built]],
[[the-lessons-queue-counts-a-cited-lesson-as-handled-and-has-a-floor-it-cannot-reach]],
[[the-lessons-queue-counter-is-fixed-after-the-drafts-are-filed]]) and **no page recording that the
width of membership had been settled**.

**Two readings and nothing decides between them**: a ruling was taken and never written down, or it
was taken somewhere the corpus does not reach. **Either way it is a miss against this store**, and
it is recorded here rather than invented ([[chat-is-not-a-channel-that-survives-the-session]]).

# What it obliges

**The counter already implements it** — the queue's membership test is unchanged, and what the
2026-08-26 edit changed is that the answer is now printed as two numbers
([[the-lessons-queue-counter-prints-cited-and-landed-apart]]).

**Rejected: making a citation from `decisions/` insufficient**, which would keep a lesson in the
queue until a rule or a proposal named it. That is the narrower membership the owner refused: it
raises the queue against lessons that already have a trace.
