# Decided 2026-08-20 by the owner

**Conditional.** The refusal names the condition; the caller satisfies it and retries.

**Absolute.** No condition exists and there is no appeal. **Reserved for a dangerous-operations
hook the owner has not yet shown**, so nothing built to date is of this shape.

**There is no third shape** — in particular, no refusal that declines without saying what would
make it stop declining.

## What it closed

Both open questions on the librarian lock's proposal, in one ruling
([[the-librarian-lock-hook]]):

- **A red check for an unrelated reason DOES block.** The objection was that a global condition
  refusing a local act is a gate people disable. The answer is that the condition is *named and
  clearable*, which is what makes it conditional rather than arbitrary.
- **The denial carrying the check's output is mandatory rather than optional.** A conditional
  refusal that does not print its condition is, to its caller, an absolute one.

## Where it sits beside the default

It does not weaken [[advisory-is-the-default-for-a-hook]] — that rule says *when* a hook may refuse
at all. This one says *what a refusal must look like* once it does, and it is why the lock allows
when it cannot measure: a hook that cannot run the check has no condition to name.

**What is still not enumerated is which hooks refuse and on what test**
([[the-system-naming-which-hooks-block]]). This ruling supplies the vocabulary that system would
be written in.

## What his words add that this page did not carry: an absolute hook is a conversation still owed

**«абсолютные хуки обсуждаются отдельно».** The page said the absolute shape is *reserved for a
dangerous-operations hook the owner has not yet shown*, which reads as a slot waiting for the right
occasion to fill it. **His wording makes it a discussion still owed**: an absolute hook is taken to
him separately, before it exists. **It is not a standing permission** that a sufficiently dangerous
operation unlocks.

**That is the half of the ruling with no mechanism behind it.** Which hooks refuse and on what test
is still unenumerated ([[the-system-naming-which-hooks-block]]), and this clause says the absolute
column of that system is his to fill and nobody else's.
