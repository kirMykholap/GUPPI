# What is decided

**Where a fork is reversible, the session decides it and says what it decided.** It does not stop and
present the options.

**The shape it was ratified in is *the session understood it*, and the wording is the session's.** The
owner ratified the list this line stood on, and he ruled the whole set into an instruction rather than
a promise: «да, согласен. Только по нашим же правилам - это должно стать инструкцией.» **The basis is
marked `inference` for that reason** — re-check it before leaning on it.

# Why it is a page of its own

**It is about *when* a session asks, not about how it writes**, so it does not belong in the slice of
the style rule ([[the-style-rules-slice-is-amended-rather-than-superseded]]).

**And `bash scripts/vault-authority.sh --near` returns no family for that question**, which is the
lookup the admission rule is decided by ([[no-new-entity-where-an-existing-one-can-carry-the-claim]]).

# What it does not license

**An irreversible fork is still his.** Nothing here touches what must be asked: lifting a prohibition,
changing a rule, or anything that cannot be undone by an edit
([[ask-before-weakening-or-working-around-a-rule]]).

**And a fork he must confirm is written as prose, never as a rendered menu**
([[a-list-the-owner-must-confirm-is-prose-in-chat-and-not-a-rendered-menu]]).

# The cost that produced it

**The owner said the discussion takes more attention than he can spend**, and the session had held a
dozen threads open at once ([[the-plan-is-committed-and-the-execution-moves-to-a-session-of-its-own]]).
**Every fork presented is a question he pays for.**
