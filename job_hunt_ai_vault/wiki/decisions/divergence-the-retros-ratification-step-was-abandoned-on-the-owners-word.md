# Context

**The retro ratifies every line before it is attributed to the owner**
([[the-retro-ratifies-every-line-before-it-is-attributed-to-the-owner]]), a live prohibition of
2026-08-21. **It exists against decisions written from memory at the end of a session**: the sweep
that produced it found 53 owner-attributed blocks across 44 pages, 28 of them in English, none his
words.

**The session of 2026-08-30 marked every decision in the turn it was taken.** All 31 attributions are
verbatim Russian, filed as they happened, each already seen by him in the chat with its context.

# The question

**Does the step run when its precondition — decisions written from memory — did not occur?**

# How it works after

**It was started and abandoned mid-run, on the owner's word.**

**owner:** «по этому короткому сообщению я не понимаю о чем вообще идет речь. Нет контекста.»

**Re-presenting the ratifications as a list stripped the context that made the first review
possible**, so the second review was worse than the first rather than a check on it.

**Rejected alternative: finish the step as written.**

**Its whole yield was one finding** — seven of thirty-nine `decision:` lines attributing without
**naming** the shape — **which a grep would have produced for nothing**
([[an-attribution-that-carries-a-quotation-but-names-no-shape]]).

# Recommendation

**Record it as a divergence and put the narrowing to the owner.** The step's precondition is
checkable — whether the decisions were filed in the turn or written at the end — and a step that runs
regardless is a second review of something already reviewed one at a time.

# Ratification

**The owner stopped the step himself**, so this is his ruling wearing a divergence's clothes. **He has
not narrowed the prohibition**, and only he may: it is a live page in his own words.

**Raised, not resolved.** [[the-retro-ratifies-every-line-before-it-is-attributed-to-the-owner]]
stands unchanged and this page does not amend it.
