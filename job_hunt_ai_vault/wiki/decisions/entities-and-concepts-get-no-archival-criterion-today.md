# The ruling, 2026-08-28

*(the owner selected it, against "not ever".)* **Not today** — the two folders keep no archival
criterion, and the question stays open rather than being closed against them.

# What it leaves unswept

**169 pages of 737**, and **no exit criterion at all**: `lessons/` leaves on a citation from outside
itself, `proposals/` on `implemented` or `refused`, `decisions/` on `processed` or
`binding: divergence` — and these two folders have nothing
([[the-lessons-cycle-is-the-owners-and-a-lesson-leaves-when-a-page-outside-lessons-cites-it]],
[[a-refused-proposal-is-archived-and-takes-status-refused]],
[[a-decisions-archival-condition-is-read-off-its-binding]]).

**That is the consequence of the deferral and it is the reason it is written down**: the output
side is finished for four folders and open for two, so the corpus keeps growing in exactly the
places nothing drains ([[the-output-side-is-finished-today-and-does-not-close-the-corpuss-growth]]).

# Why they are hard, named rather than left implicit

**An entity is a noun with properties and a concept is a mechanism with consequences**, and neither
goes false by being acted on. A lesson is drained when something cites it and a proposal is drained
when it is built; there is no equivalent event for *how the finaliser works*.

# The deferral held through the first sweep, and the two folders are now a third of `wiki/` — 2026-08-29

**The sweep ran over `decisions/`, `lessons/`, `proposals/` and `rules/` and left these two untouched**,
exactly as this page says it would
([[the-sweep-runs-for-real-over-every-criterion-and-records-what-it-moved]]).

**They hold 174 pages between them — 43 entities and 131 concepts — against a `wiki/` of 523.** Before
the sweep the same 174 were about a fifth of 762; the share moved because everything else shrank, not
because these grew.

**Item 38 of S3 is where it is picked up**, and it was among the five items the owner dropped from the
day's scope ([[the-day-is-scoped-to-the-optimisation-archiving-and-process-items-of-s3]]).

**What the sweep changed about the question:** the two folders are now the largest thing in the store
that nothing drains, and they are the whole of the remaining cost the archiving topic was opened to
reduce. **The argument above — that neither goes false by being acted on — is untouched by that**, and
is still the reason no criterion has been written.
