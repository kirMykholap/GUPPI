# Context

**The retro of 2026-08-30 put sixteen open questions to the owner and he could not act on them.**
**Ten of the sixteen predate the session**: six are the brief's own carried alarms, some running a
fourth, fifth and sixth edition. Five are places where the plan said one thing and the machinery
permitted only another, each already written into the file it changed together with its ground. Five
are remnants the session named rather than let pass.

**The list read as a sudden pile.** The retro shows carried items and new ones in one undifferentiated
list, with nothing saying which arrived today
([[a-list-that-mixes-carried-items-with-new-ones-reads-as-a-pile]]).

# The question

**Are the sixteen answered tonight or deferred?**

# How it works after

**All sixteen are deferred, each with a destination rather than a resolution.**

**Consequence, named by the session:** six of the brief's alarms enter a further edition unanswered,
including two contradiction pairs and a ruling of his recorded rather than executed.

**The five forks the session resolved without asking are not questions any more** — they are written
into the files they changed with their ground, and each is now **owed a look in a `git diff` rather
than an answer**: the English wording of the five section headings; the `proposals/` heading
`Decisions`; `repealed` scoped to `rules/` where the plan said all six; four folders carrying no
`sections:` block where the plan said declared-and-empty; and the two fields being `optional:` where
the plan said `required:`.

# Recommendation

**The session's ground for pressing was that ten of the sixteen had already waited whole sessions.**
It did not press.

# Ratification

**The owner said it**: «Да, откладываем и идем к handoff». **His ground was that the session was long
and he no longer knew how to act.**

# A live prohibition bites this deferral and it is raised rather than satisfied

**[[a-repeated-deferral-names-the-sprint-it-happens-in-or-is-refused]] applies**, and **nothing in the
journal names a sprint for any of the sixteen.** Ten of them have already waited whole sessions,
which is exactly the population that rule was written for. **The librarian does not refuse a deferral
the owner took; it records that the rule was not satisfied and hands it back.**

# Deferred again, with one item removed from the pile rather than deferred — 2026-08-31

**owner selected, 2026-08-31: «откладываем, согласен».** The three old pairs — the CRLF count, the
`page:` verdict's resolvability, `log.md` rotation — and the `RAISED, NOT RESOLVED` markers are
deferred, which makes them a tenth, seventh and sixth consecutive brief for the three pairs.

**The exception is not a deferral and is the point of this section.** The marker standing on the
suspension of [[a-decisions-archival-condition-is-read-off-its-binding]] is **removed, because its
ground is gone**: the suspension now has a stated end, narrowed permanently rather than left open
([[the-cleanup-suspends-the-rules-that-would-refuse-it]]).

**A second marker went quiet on the same day and was not deferred either, because nobody chose
anything.** The one on [[authority-uniqueness-becomes-a-per-type-question]] names
[[concepts-are-non-functional-requirements-and-entities-are-functional]], which moved into `archive/`
with the sweep. **A counterparty leaving `wiki/` is not a ruling** and the marker stands; it is noted so
that the next reader does not mistake an archive move for an answer.

**The live count is ten markers rather than seven.** Measured at this ingest across `wiki/`, and the
journal's figure of seven is left as the session wrote it — `raw/` is immutable and what a session
believed is data.
