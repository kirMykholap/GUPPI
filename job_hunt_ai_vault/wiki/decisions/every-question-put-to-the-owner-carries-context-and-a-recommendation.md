# The ruling, 2026-08-29

**owner:** «На будущее - стоит завести правило , чтобы ты всегда так задавал вопросы и подавал
материл. С контекстом, обьяснением, описанием как работает сейчас и как строим, и рекомендацией.»

**Four parts, and a question missing any of them is not ready to ask:**

1. **Context** — what the thing is.
2. **How it works today.**
3. **What would be built.**
4. **A recommendation**, with its ground.

**He said it after a list of fourteen bare questions**, which he answered with «я не могу докадываться
о чем речь».

# What it obliges

**The cost is on the asker.** Fourteen questions that each need four paragraphs is a long message, and
that is the trade being made: the alternative was fourteen questions that could not be answered at all.

**It is not a licence to ask more.** One question per message is the standing rule and is untouched
([[write-plainly-name-the-source-and-say-what-is-not-done]]).

**It applies to a fork as much as to a question.** Naming the mechanisms separately before comparing
options is the same demand from the other side — the options cannot be compared until each is
understood ([[a-reversible-fork-is-decided-rather-than-presented]]).

**And the shape stays prose.** A list the owner must confirm or correct is enumerated prose in chat, not
a rendered menu ([[a-list-the-owner-must-confirm-is-prose-in-chat-and-not-a-rendered-menu]]).

# The rule page this asks for is not written

**He asked for a rule** — «стоит завести правило» — and a `wiki/rules/` page renders its text into a
governed file, which is a change to what instructs every agent. **No such page was written by this
ingest**, and the decision stands here alone. Writing it is a harness change with a destination to
choose, and choosing that destination is not the librarian's
([[wiki-rules-holds-the-rules-of-the-repository-claude-md]]).
