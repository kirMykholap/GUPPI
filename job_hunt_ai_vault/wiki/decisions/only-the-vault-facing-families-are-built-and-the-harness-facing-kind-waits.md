# Context

**The two kinds of family need two different pieces of work and the design named only one.**
[[the-prohibition-families-become-rule-pages-served-by-a-hook]] describes 53 families as one build. It
is not one:

- a **vault-facing** family's text is addressed to the vault's own pages, so it becomes a page under
  `render: authority` whose clauses a script assembles from member titles;
- a **harness-facing** family's text belongs in a harness or product file and reaches its reader only
  by being rendered into it, which needs `governs:` and a **written region** — connected prose somebody
  writes, which no script assembles from titles.

**The verdicts split them 18 to 35**, holding 131 prohibitions against 164
([[the-cleanup-verdict-names-which-delivery-a-family-gets]]).

**And the second kind lands on a requirement that is currently lifted.**
[[what-travels-from-a-rule-page-is-a-marked-slice]] is one of the four rules suspended for the cleanup,
and a `render: region` family rule needs exactly the slice that rule asks for. **No page says what a
family rule of the second kind contains.**

# The question

**Are both kinds built this session, or only the first?**

# How it works after

**Only the vault-facing kind is built.** Nineteen pages under `render: authority`, and 129 of their
decisions released to `archive/`.

**The harness-facing kind is discussed next session**, as a discussion and not as a build. What that
discussion must answer before anything is written: what a second-kind family rule's text *is*, which
file its region goes into, and what happens to the marked-slice requirement that is lifted underneath
it — narrowed permanently for the first kind and untouched for this one
([[the-cleanup-suspends-the-rules-that-would-refuse-it]]).

**Nothing else moves in the meantime.** The 35 families keep their 164 prohibitions in
`wiki/decisions/`, which is why the folder stands at 211 rather than falling further — **the cleanup is
half done by page count and that is the ratified state, not a shortfall.**

**A family with no rule keeps its decisions where they are.** The repeal releases a prohibition only
once the family rule carrying its clause exists, so `harness-facing` and `none` release nothing. That
follows from the repeal rather than being new, and it is stated here because the cleanup table's legend
said the decisions leave in both cases — the legend is what was rewritten.

**This deferral names no target and closes on nothing mechanical.** What would close it is a design for
the second kind, and that design does not exist; naming a target it could not have would put a false
handshake in the tree ([[a-decision-declares-its-target-and-the-handshake-closes-it]]).

# Recommendation

**Build the first kind now.** The session put it to him over two alternatives, on the ground that the
second kind is prose work of an unknown size sitting on a suspended requirement.

**Rejected: building both this session.**

# Ratification

**owner selected, 2026-08-31: «Первый род - сейчас. Второй обсуждаем на следующей сессии».**
