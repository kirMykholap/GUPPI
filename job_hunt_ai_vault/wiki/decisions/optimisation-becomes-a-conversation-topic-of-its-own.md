# The ruling, 2026-08-26

*(the owner selected it, against filing the cost of reading the extract under the existing narrow
item about the suite's runtime.)*

**A tenth conversation topic is opened: optimisation.** It has three members on the day it is opened:

- **the selftest suite's runtime**, 34 minutes and rising with the corpus;
- **the cost of reading the session extract whole at `/retro`**, about 26,000 tokens;
- **the cost of dispatching an agent**, 143,840–230,882 tokens per `/pre-task` across seven points.

**The narrow item about the suite's runtime becomes a member of this topic** rather than the place
the other two are filed.

**He named it while refusing a proposal to make the extract reading conditional:** «Но стоит добавить
к вопросу оптимизации - это большая тема»
([[the-session-extract-is-read-whole-at-every-retro]]).

# Why a topic rather than a wider item

**Three costs with one cause between them are a subject.** Filing the extract's cost under the
suite's runtime would make the suite item mean two things, and an item that means two things is
answered about one of them ([[a-conversation-the-owner-scheduled-is-displaced-by-the-work-beside-it]]).

# Rejected

**Rejected: adding the extract's cost to the existing narrow item.** That is the shape he ruled
against.

**Rejected: discussing it now.** It is a topic for a session, not a decision to take at a retro
([[the-open-questions-list-is-built-whether-or-not-it-is-discussed]]).

# A fourth member: the session's own context budget — 2026-08-31

**owner, 2026-08-31:** «200000 контекста уже забито. Это кошмар. Пытаемся экономить»

**Recorded because it changed how the rest of that session was worked** — targeted reads instead of
whole files, and no re-reading of anything already held. It is not a rule and it forbids nothing; it is
the fourth cost with the same cause as the other three, which is what makes this topic a topic.

**It is also the currency two live obligations spend.**
[[the-session-extract-is-read-whole-at-every-retro]] costs 26,000 to 35,000 tokens per retro and
[[a-pre-task-runs-before-anything-non-trivial]] costs a dispatch. **Neither is weakened by this line**
— a topic is where they are discussed, not where they are narrowed.
