# DIVERGENCE, 2026-08-20

**`CLAUDE.md` requires `/pre-task` before anything non-trivial and before touching an unfamiliar
area.** It was not run before the first four items of this session
([[a-pre-task-runs-before-anything-non-trivial]]).

**What was done instead:** targeted reading of the pages the brief named.

**Why it looked unnecessary:** the brief and `sprint.md` already pointed at the work and at the
pages behind it, so the plan felt pre-checked.

**The rejected alternative is the rule itself.**

## The owner raised it as his theory of the day's errors, and it is half right

He proposed that the day's errors came from skipping `/pre-task` and searching the wiki by hand.
**The precise half that holds is about breadth, not about the ritual**
([[breadth-was-what-was-missing-and-pre-task-is-one-way-to-buy-it]]):

- **The `MIGRATION_CHECK_DATABASE_URL` hole supports it.** A targeted read of the pages the brief
  named could not find a page nobody pointed at
  ([[a-hole-was-shipped-in-work-already-reported-as-verified]]).
- **The `Find-Bash` error does not support it at all.** That is a fact about code, settled by one
  `grep -rn 'function Find-Bash'`, and no vault query would have held it
  ([[a-fact-about-code-was-generalised-from-its-neighbour]]).

**A brief is not a `/pre-task`**, and that is the operative distinction: the brief reports what the
last ingest found, and the pages it names are the pages it names.

## It happened again the same day, in the next session

**The rule was diverged from again on 2026-08-20**, in the session that ran after this one, for a
different reason: a repair that surfaced while running an acceptance test did not read as
implementation ([[pre-task-was-skipped-again-and-the-record-is-a-recurrence-rather-than-an-incident]]).

**Two consecutive sessions, three recorded instances, three different causes** — the third being
2026-08-19, where the ritual was literally unavailable
([[the-pre-task-was-skipped-and-the-rule-has-no-by-hand-form]]).

**This page's title names a single session and there are now three pages of this shape.** Whether
the record becomes one class page is raised in the brief and is the owner's, not the librarian's
([[the-librarian-writes-unattended]]).
