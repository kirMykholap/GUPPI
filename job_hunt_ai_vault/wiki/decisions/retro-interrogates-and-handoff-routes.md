# Decided 2026-08-17, and the owner's own words are the test

**Retro interrogates: it finds what the session would not volunteer. Handoff routes: it files what
retro found.**

> *"Handoff literally means lay out the session's data and let go"* — **owner**, 2026-08-17.

That is why the handoff's sections are named after the folders they reach, and the retro's after
the shapes a session fails in: *diverged*, *fell out silently*, *went wrong*, *left fragile*,
*contradicted itself*.

**Rejected: folding the interrogation into the handoff template.** It would produce one document
that asks and classifies at once, and it would give the template sections with no destination —
*went wrong* is not a folder, and forcing it to be one is how an intention gets filed as a record
of something built ([[proposals-are-a-folder-not-a-status]]).

## What it obliges

- **Two forms, and the mapping travels in two hops** — the retro's sections carry `→ handoff:`, the
  handoff's carry `→ ingest:`, each documented at its own source
  ([[the-mapping-is-documented-at-each-hop-and-copied-nowhere]]).
- **A question with no verdict stops the retro**, and it is the handoff that decides where the
  answer is filed, not the retro ([[the-open-questions-list-is-built-whether-or-not-it-is-discussed]]).
- **The evidence for the split is on the step's own page** — three findings that reached the journal
  of 2026-08-17 only because the interrogation ran first ([[the-retro-step]]).

## The two hops share a vocabulary, and one end of it names a token that does not exist — 2026-08-19

**`.claude/templates/handoff.md` routes `- harness:` lines from the drain into the `Harness` section.
`.superpowers/sdd/vault-feedback.md` has no `harness:` kind** — its header names four, and `harness:` is
not one of them ([[the-drain-and-the-template-disagree-about-the-feedback-vocabulary]]).

**So the second hop's mapping names a token the first hop cannot produce**, and the transfer that was
meant to be mechanical is done by hand. It is exactly the failure this decision's *documented at each
hop and copied nowhere* rule is aimed at, arriving through the one thing the two hops **do** share:
the vocabulary itself ([[the-mapping-is-documented-at-each-hop-and-copied-nowhere]]).

**Which document is wrong is the owner's**, and both fixes are one line.
