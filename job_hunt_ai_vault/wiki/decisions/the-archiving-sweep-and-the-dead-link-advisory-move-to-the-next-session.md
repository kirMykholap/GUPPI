# What happened, 2026-08-28

**Of six build items the session scoped, the owner took the first three and then stopped after the
first.** *(he said it, in his own words.)* The three were: the status values into the schemas, the
sweep mode in `scripts/vault-archive.sh`, and the gate advisory on links into deprecated pages. The
session's estimate for the whole set was about four hours, not counting the selftest's own run.

**Only the status values landed.** The sweep and the advisory carry decided, written designs and no
code ([[the-archive-sweep-that-walks-each-folder-and-collects-its-refusals]],
[[the-gate-advisory-naming-a-live-link-into-a-deprecated-page]]).

# The correction he made at ratification, and it changes the scope

**"Take the first three of six" described the frame of that day and was not a decision about how
much work the next session takes.** *(the owner said it, correcting the attribution the session had
written into the ratification list.)*

**The next session takes all of the optimisation, archiving and process items** — not the two left
over from this one ([[the-output-side-is-finished-today-and-does-not-close-the-corpuss-growth]]).

**The session had recorded the frame as a scope decision, which it was not.** That is the class of
error the ratification step exists to catch, and it is the one attribution of twenty-two he changed
([[the-retro-ratifies-every-line-before-it-is-attributed-to-the-owner]],
[[the-retro-records-a-ruling-chosen-from-a-menu-and-ratifies-decisions-not-utterances]]).

# The consequence of not doing it

**Nothing drains any folder yet.** Every archival criterion decided over the past week —
`lessons/` on an outside citation, `decisions/` on `decided_by:` or `divergence`, `proposals/` on
`implemented` or `refused` — is a rule with no program behind it
([[the-archive-mover-is-a-sweep-inside-the-archive-script-at-the-lint-stage]]).

# Deferred again on 2026-08-30, and this time it waits on verdicts rather than on code

**The sweep did not run and the 53 family rules were not written.** They stand on **99 verdicts the
owner has not given** — 53 for the prohibition families and 46 for the concept/entity groups — in
`docs/vault-cleanup-families.md`, whose verdict columns are empty
([[the-prohibition-families-become-rule-pages-served-by-a-hook]]).

**That is a different blocker from the one this page records.** Here the sweep waited on a program;
now the program's criterion waits on a judgement, and no amount of code closes it.

**The consequence, stated plainly: the corpus grew today rather than shrank**, and the next sweep runs
over a larger `wiki/`. It went 597 to 598 during the session and gains roughly thirty more at the
ingest, which is the ratified outcome rather than an accident
([[this-session-is-compiled-without-compressing-what-it-wrote]]).

**The sweep's own reach has narrowed as well.** The sweep of 2026-08-29 took 239 pages, `wiki/` 762 →
523; the corpus was back to 597 two days later. **A sweep by the same criteria today selects about
nine pages of 597** — `lessons/` none, `rules/` none, `decisions/` about five, `proposals/` four,
`entities/` and `concepts/` not walked at all. **That does not mean the criteria are broken; it means
they have taken everything they can reach**, which is the whole argument for a criterion that reaches
`decisions/`.

**It is deferred again with no sprint named**
([[everything-on-the-frontier-but-the-cleanup-is-deferred-and-none-of-it-names-a-sprint]]).
