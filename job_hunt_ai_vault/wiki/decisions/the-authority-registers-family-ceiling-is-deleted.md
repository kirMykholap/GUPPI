# The ruling, 2026-08-26

*(the owner selected it, against keeping the line and against rebalancing the families to satisfy
it.)*

**The sentence *"About ten to a family"* comes out of `authority.md`.** It has not been applied once
since it was written on 2026-08-21, and by 2026-08-26 the register held **666 slugs in 15 families**
— an average of forty-four, with three families carrying a dated note saying they stand over the
ceiling and nothing following from it.

**A rule nobody applies is worse than no rule**, because a reader takes it for a live constraint and
the three notes read as debt rather than as a rule that was never in force.

# Rejected

**Rejected: keeping the line.** It would go on being cited and go on not deciding anything.

**Rejected: rebalancing the families to satisfy it.** That is a re-partition of 666 slugs, and the
ground it would rest on — ten is what a reader opens for one question — is about *pages read for one
question*, not about how a register is grouped. The reading ceiling stands where it belongs, in the
reading discipline ([[the-folder-is-the-first-narrowing]]).

# What it obliges, and it is not done

**The line and the three over-the-ceiling notes leave `authority.md`.** It is a register the
librarian writes ([[authority-md-is-a-register-the-one-writer-clause-names]]), and the
deletion belongs to the plan's third phase, which the owner moved to the next session
([[the-plans-remaining-phase-moves-to-s3-and-the-ritual-runs-whole]]).

**In the meantime the sentence is marked false in place rather than removed**, because nothing in
this store is deleted before its successor exists to be pointed at.

# It is done, and the heading above is a reading of the day it was written — 2026-08-26

**The paragraph, its strike block and the ceiling clause of all three `note:` lines are out of
`authority.md`.** What was kept from each note is what it said about the family rather than about the
ceiling: which slugs landed together and why, and — in two of the three — that rebalancing is a
judgement about the corpus. **The rebalancing-is-`/vault-lint`-work clause on
[[authority-becomes-the-index-and-is-required-outside-rules]] is untouched**, which is where that
ruling lives.

**The register keeps no trace of the deleted line and does not need one.** A pointer to this page
inside `authority.md` would be a second copy of a claim that already has a page, and the register is
a lookup rather than a history ([[a-drifting-list-is-repaired-by-deleting-it-not-extending-it]]).

**Two `sprint.md` items went stale in the same edit and are struck in place** — *the `authority.md`
families standing over the ceiling*, and the line reading *item 6 above is eleven slugs in one
family* ([[an-answered-item-leaves-the-next-list-by-being-struck-in-place]]).
