# The ruling, 2026-08-29

**owner:** «значит продолжаем разговор по оптимизации, архивации и процессов. Наша задача -
подготовить детальный план действий и имплементировать сегодня решения»

**The subject of the day was his**, and so was the instruction that it end in built code rather than
in a design.

**He narrowed it a second time once the plan was sized:** S3 items **32, 33, 37, 40, 41, 42 and 43**,
selected from the seven-item recommendation the session put to him. **Dropped: 34, 35, 36, 38 and
39.**

**He parked item 41 later in the same session**
([[the-strike-form-item-is-parked-and-the-count-leaves-the-governing-pages-prose]]).

# What it obliges

**Work outside those items was outside the day.** The rulings that arrived anyway — the librarian's
dispatch tool, the `/ingest` rewiring, the log's rotation, the registers rendering from rule pages —
were **decided and not built**, each with its own page saying so.

**Six of the seven taken were built, proved and left green.**

**Nothing was committed.** 239 renames-with-modification and eleven modified files stood uncommitted
at the journal's writing, and the tree still read that way when this was compiled.

# Where the narrowing shows

**Dropping 34, 35 and 36 is why the reading-agent rewiring is a design here and not a mechanism**
([[the-ingest-rewired-so-the-librarian-dispatches-and-returns-once]]).

**Dropping 38 is why `entities/` and `concepts/` were not swept** — 174 pages, and now a third of
`wiki/` ([[entities-and-concepts-get-no-archival-criterion-today]]).
