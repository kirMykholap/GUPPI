# Decided 2026-08-18: the rendered text replaces the typed text

**Two claims left the repository's `CLAUDE.md` by hand and arrive rendered instead.** Both were
verified present in the rendered region *before* the old text was dropped, which is the only order
in which this is safe: the file has lost an assertion to a cut twice, and both times it was found by
a human reading the removed text beside the page meant to carry it
([[claude-md-can-lose-an-assertion-but-cannot-strand-a-page]]).

**Rejected: leaving the region beside the prose.** That would have left the file saying *"Nothing is
duplicated here"* twice, in two places, one typed and one rendered — the exact shape the injector
exists to end ([[a-mechanisms-first-use-outside-its-home-is-where-it-drifts]]).

**The placement is ratified.** What was not is the waiting.

## This is a divergence, and the owner's ruling on it is the part to keep

The repository's `CLAUDE.md` says *"Ask before you weaken, remove or work around any rule"*
([[ask-before-weakening-or-working-around-a-rule]]). The hook fired. **The question had been put to
the owner and was still unanswered when the session proceeded**, on reasoning stated out loud.

> **owner: a question once asked is closed by the owner and by nobody else.**

**Stating the reasoning is not the same as waiting**, and the general form of that is
[[a-question-asked-is-not-a-question-answered]]. What separates a legal notification from an owed
question is on [[narrowing-a-scope-notifies-and-widening-it-asks]].

## Why it looked unnecessary at the time

Because the alternative was visibly worse, and the session could see that it was worse. **That is
the shape every recorded divergence in this project has opened with**
([[hand-edits-of-the-wiki-are-a-recorded-divergence]]) — the rule looks unnecessary precisely in the
cases where the answer seems obvious, which are the cases where an answer from the owner costs the
least to wait for.
