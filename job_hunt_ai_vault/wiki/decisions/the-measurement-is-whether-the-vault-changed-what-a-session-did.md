# Decided 2026-08-16: two numbers per session, and no scale

**Per session: how many times the vault was asked, and how many of those answers changed what the
session did.**

**Chosen over coverage, and over readiness scales**, for two reasons the owner gave together: it
needs no scale that does not exist, and it is the number that decides whether this store is a tool
or ballast. Every alternative on the table required inventing a threshold, and an invented
threshold is what this project has already been burned by
([[an-unsourced-number-is-dropped-not-chosen]], [[the-growth-signal-is-the-brief]]).

## The first reading, 2026-08-16

**Five lookups, five of which changed what the session did.**

| the lookup | what it changed |
|---|---|
| [[the-growth-signal-is-the-brief]] and [[an-unsourced-number-is-dropped-not-chosen]] | stopped a threshold being invented |
| `tags.md`'s record that `collection` was split | killed the `batches` merge ([[batches-stays-a-tag-of-its-own]]) |
| [[the-librarian-writes-unattended]] | stopped a direct write to `tags.md` |
| the brief's hook about fragile selftests | shaped how the new rule was added |

**Four of the five were about the vault's own machinery**, and the one about the subject came from
the owner rather than from the corpus. That is the shape of the reading, and it is worth carrying
forward with the number: a store that is only ever useful about itself is measuring its own
maintenance.

## What it does not say

A lookup that changed nothing is not a failure — the answer *"no decision has been recorded about
this"* is one of the two things a brief exists to say. The number is a ratio kept over sessions,
not a per-lookup verdict, and it has one session in it.
