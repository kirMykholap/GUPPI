# The ruling, 2026-08-25

> **owner:** «Я вижу, что сессия получается очень большая. МОжет мы напишем план и перенесем
> выполнение на отдлеьную сессию? Боюсь, что что-то может сломаться или потеряться, но продолжать в
> этой сессии выглядит очень дорого»

**The session ends at the plan plus the closing ritual.** Every item was worked to a decision and
nothing was built.

> **owner:** «копируем и комитим»

**The plan is copied into `docs/superpowers/plans/` and committed**, restoring a pattern last used on
2026-08-13 ([[the-plan-the-owner-reads-to-approve-it-is-written-in-russian]]).

# What the tree says about that pattern

**`docs/superpowers/plans/` held eight plans and the newest was dated 2026-08-13.** Twelve days of
plans lived nowhere in the repository — the plan the owner reads is written in Russian and lives
outside it, at `C:\Users\kir\.claude\plans\`, by the language rule that says so
([[english-in-the-repository-russian-with-the-owner]]). **The committed copy is in English**, which
is what makes it a repository artefact.

# What he said about the cost, and it is the reason the cut was made

> **owner:** «вопросов много. Мне не хвататет внимания и понимания, чтобы эффективно работать с
> тобой. Слишком много усилий и внимания требует обсуждение. Мне тяжело понимать некоторые вещи..»

**A dozen items taken to the discussion stage in one session with nothing built is a shape he named
as too expensive.** One item carried through discussion, decision, build and verification costs less
attention per unit of progress and shows him something working. **That finding belongs to the
sprint** and is not a rule this page states.

**The instruction half of the same complaint is the style rule's amendment**
([[the-style-rules-slice-is-amended-rather-than-superseded]]).

# Not done

**Everything the plan describes.** No script was written, no rule page edited, no `wiki/` page
touched by the session. **The next session is the one that executes it**, and the vault's own record
of that session is this ingest, written under the rule that it writes only what was decided and what
was designed ([[an-ingest-of-a-session-that-built-nothing-writes-decisions-and-proposals]]).

# The opposite was chosen on 2026-08-29, and this page already argued for it — 2026-08-29

> [!NOTE] RAISED, NOT RESOLVED — the disagreement is with a ruling under a different `authority:`
> **owner:** «Я предлагаю строить его сразу, а не раскладывать на пункты S3» — the agent architecture
> was built in the session that designed it, which is what the sentence above forbids.

**The page disagrees with itself and that is why no new page was written.** Its own closing argument is
that one item carried through discussion, decision, build and verification costs less attention than
the same item split across two sessions — which leans the other way.

**What the day measured is on the owner's side of it.** Five commits and three built items came out of
a session that had spent most of its length arguing a design, and he stopped the arguing himself:
*«Я уже перестал понимать что происходит. Сессия большая, а результат ноль.»* **The plan was then cut
to what could be built without a librarian write**, which is S3 items 34, 35 and 36.

**And the thing built was designed the same day and ratified the same day.** Four of its seven parts
already had S3 items; only the fifth actor — `vault-ask` as `context: fork` — is unbuilt
([[who-reads-the-vault-and-who-writes-it]]).

**Which of the two readings is the rule is his, not the librarian's.** Both stand.
