# The ruling, 2026-08-26

*(the owner selected it, against the then-current arrangement of dropping both.)*

**Phase 1 of the ritual enumerates `binding: prohibition` and `binding: deferral`, and discards
`binding: divergence` alone.** A deferral is context a plan needs — *this is already designed, do
not invent it again* — and it was being thrown away with the one value that teaches nothing
([[a-decision-declares-whether-it-binds]]).

**The prose paragraph instructing the agent to reach past the filter by judgement is deleted in the
same change.** A mechanism replaces the instruction rather than standing beside it: an agent told to
apply a filter and then told to override it when it feels wrong is being asked to do the filter's
job twice.

**The counts the ruling was taken against**, measured 2026-08-26 over `wiki/decisions/`: **240
`prohibition`, 41 `deferral`, 17 `divergence`.** Keeping `deferral` widens the enumeration by about
a sixth; dropping `divergence` narrows it by rather less.

# The evidence arrived from inside the mechanism the ruling is about

**The `/pre-task` that checked the plan carrying this ruling reached past its own `binding:` filter
four times, and three of the four pages changed a plan item.** Every one of the four was
`binding: deferral`, and each was the **subject** of a plan item rather than context for it.

That is independent evidence, produced by the filter under discussion, inside the run that was
checking the decision about it ([[a-plan-can-be-factually-wrong-about-the-tree-and-the-pre-task-is-what-reads-the-tree]]).

# What it obliges, and it is built

**`.claude/agents/pre-task.md` was edited by the session's own hand the same day.** The edits sit
outside the rule region at lines 28–102, so no address verdict is touched and the injector is not
involved ([[the-pre-task-agent]], [[the-injector-walks-pages-to-files-and-the-check-walks-files-to-pages]]).

# Rejected

**Rejected: the arrangement as it stood** — dropping `prohibition`'s two companions together, on the
ground that phase 1 is meant to return what *stops* a plan. That reading is right about `divergence`
and wrong about `deferral`, and the run above shows the cost.

**Rejected: leaving the override paragraph in place beside the wider filter.** With the filter
corrected the paragraph has nothing left to correct, and a standing licence to ignore a filter is
how a filter stops being measurable.
