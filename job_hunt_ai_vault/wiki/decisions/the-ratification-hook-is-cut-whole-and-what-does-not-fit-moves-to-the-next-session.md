# The ruling, 2026-08-23, in the second session of that day

> **owner: окей, согласен с твоим разделением. Переносим на следующую сессию все, что не влазит
> сегодня**

**The last phase of the approved plan — the ratification hook — was cut entire.** Not started, not
partly landed, not left in flight.

# Why whole rather than half

**Half of that phase is worse than none of it.** The hook has two ends: a registration in the hook
configuration and a rule page naming the file it renders into. Landing one leaves either a hook
nothing invokes or a rule whose destination does not exist — and a rule naming a missing destination
is a red gate, not a partial feature ([[a-region-is-a-paired-comment-named-for-a-rule]],
[[a-governed-file-with-no-region-is-an-advisory]]).

**The session put the split to him and he agreed with the split before he agreed with the cut.**
That order is what the first clause of his sentence records: the boundary was the thing being
ratified, and the deferral followed from it.

# The general half is the one worth keeping

**Whatever does not fit in a session moves to the next one, rather than being compressed into it.**
That is the rule his second clause states, and it is the standing answer to a session that is
running out of room: cut a phase at its boundary and carry it, do not shrink every phase to fit.

**Where the carried work goes is already decided**: onto `sprint.md`, not into a brief, because a
brief dies with the session that reads it
([[the-deferrals-of-this-session-go-onto-the-sprint]]).

# What it obliges

- **The ratification hook is unstarted work of S3** and is recorded on `sprint.md` with the rest.
- **Nothing about the ratification sweep it would have served is changed.** That sweep is deferred
  on its own ruling and for its own reasons, now for a third consecutive session
  ([[the-ratification-sweep-waits-for-a-session-of-its-own]]).
