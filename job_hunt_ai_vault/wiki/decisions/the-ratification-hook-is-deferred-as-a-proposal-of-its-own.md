# Context

**The reply-format rule says no case opens until the previous one is ratified or explicitly deferred**
([[the-reply-is-a-report-answers-and-one-case]]). The obvious instrument is a hook that watches for an
unratified decision and refuses to let the next case open.

# The question

**Is that hook built now, or deferred?**

# How it works after

**Deferred, as a proposal of its own.** The rendered rule plus the printed frontier carry the same
discipline at no cost, and the owner will say if it slips.

**The ground the owner accepted**: such a hook can only read **what the session itself writes** into
the ledger, which is the class that already produces false green smoke rows here
([[the-smoke-gate-hooks]]). An instrument that reads its author's own report of a fact is not an
instrument.

**A different hook for the same rule is admissible and is not this one.** A `MessageDisplay` hook
checking that the reply **opens with the frontier line** checks **form**, which is visible whole and
cannot be faked without being performed
([[the-reply-format-rule-gets-a-hook-that-checks-the-form-and-not-the-fact]]).

# Recommendation

**Defer**, on the fact-versus-form distinction, and let the rendered rule do the work meanwhile.

# Ratification

**The owner said it**, on 2026-08-30: «Хук откладывается как отдельный proposal. Если я замечу, что ты
пропускаешь ратификацию - укажу и мы вернемся к этому proposal».

# A second ratification hook has been deferred for seven days

**[[the-ratification-hook-is-cut-whole-and-what-does-not-fit-moves-to-the-next-session]]** deferred a
**different** ratification hook on 2026-08-23 and **it has not been built since**. Two open deferrals
of two hooks named for ratification is worth the owner's eye, and it is raised rather than merged: the
two hooks watch different things and the librarian does not decide whether they are one item.
