# Surfaced at the retro of 2026-08-19 and ratified as a deferral

**The `record-schema` tag sweep and the three trim candidates at `/vault-lint` fell out of the session
silently.** The brief named them in its *next* list, the session did other work, and **nothing raised
them** — not the session, not the ritual, not the gate.

**owner: they are deferred, and they go to `sprint.md` as `next`.**

**Rejected: leaving them in the brief's *next* list.** That is where they already were, and a second
brief was about to carry them the same way.

# Why the destination is the point rather than the deferral

**A brief is derived and is regenerated at every ingest** ([[journal-vault-brief-each-artefact-has-one-owner]]).
An item that lives only there is re-asserted every session by a document nobody is accountable to, and it
falls out the first time a session's attention goes elsewhere — **with no artefact recording that it
did.** The sprint is a destination; a brief is a summary of destinations
([[the-growth-signal-is-the-brief]]).

**This is the second time an item has had to be moved off a brief for exactly this reason.** The first
was the filename renames, moved onto `sprint.md` on 2026-08-18 with the note *"it has arrived in two
consecutive briefs and a brief is not a destination"*.

# What it obliges

**The journal carries them explicitly rather than a brief carrying them silently**, and `sprint.md`'s
*next* line is where the ordering is read from.

**They are not a criterion and are not being read as one.** The `record-schema` advisory is a tag-density
line, and splitting a dense tag is the owner's
([[the-dense-tags-are-not-split-and-the-standing-advisories-are-named-here]]); the trim candidates are
`/vault-lint` findings whose boundary the per-type conversation settles
([[the-wide-half-of-the-per-type-schema-is-a-conversation-not-a-task]]).

# Untouched for a further day — reported 2026-08-20

**Neither item moved in the session of 2026-08-19.** They are the owner's first item for the next
session, and the journal names them as untouched **for a further day** — which is exactly the shape
the ratification recorded on this page was written to stop.

**The ratification did its job even so**, and the distinction is worth keeping: they fell out of a
session again, but **they did not fall out of the record**. Before this page existed, the brief's
*next* list was the only place they lived, and a second brief was about to carry them the same way
([[the-growth-signal-is-the-brief]]). Now `sprint.md` carries them and a session that skips them
skips something written down.

**No verdict is read off here.** Whether a deferral standing for a further day is acceptable is the
owner's ([[a-sprint-closes-on-its-goal]]).

## Actioned at the retro of 2026-08-20, and the ratification is what got them there

**`record-schema` is dropped from the two live trim candidates**, sixteen pages to fourteen in
`decisions/`, and the third candidate needed nothing because it is already retired
([[record-schema-is-dropped-from-the-trim-candidates]]).

**The sweep itself resolved into the channel split rather than into a tag split.** The problem the
`record-schema` advisory represented was that twenty statistics buried the one line about the run in
front of you; moving them behind `--lint` addresses that without re-tagging anything
([[the-tag-density-advisories-move-behind-lint]]).

**The advisory still prints at fourteen**, which is what keeps this a trim on the merits.

**What this page claimed and what it bought are now both testable.** The items fell out of two
sessions in a row and did not fall out of the record either time — because `sprint.md` carried
them, and a session that skipped them skipped something written down.
