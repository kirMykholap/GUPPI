# The ruling, 2026-08-24

> **owner:** «я посмотрю повторится это или нет. Если повторится - будем решать что делать»

**One occurrence is not a pattern, and the fix would change a skill.** He chose to watch.

**Rejected: editing `/retro` on the strength of one session.** The skill is read by every session
that ends, and a change made against a single incident is a change nobody can evaluate afterwards.

# What is being watched, precisely

**A session that records no marks during its retro zeroes an ongoing measurement**: how many of the
retro's open lines came from the marks against how many were found by reading the transcript. The
reading of 2026-08-24 was fourteen from marks against seven found by reading, where the previous
reading was four against eleven ([[the-retro-reads-the-marks-and-does-not-replace-them]]).

**So the cost of a repeat is not the lost lines — it is the lost comparison.**

# A third reading, and what the marks missed was not a random tail — 2026-08-30

**Sixteen from the marks against eleven found by reading the extract**, out of twenty-seven open
questions. The series now reads four against eleven, fourteen against seven, sixteen against eleven.

**The extract was read whole**, which is what makes this reading comparable to the other two —
1,605 lines, 267,988 bytes, sixteen owner turns, thirty-nine session replies, fourteen feedback
blocks, zero menus.

**What the marks missed is the part that changes what this measurement means.** Among the eleven were
**a taxonomy the owner had ratified in his own words and that exists in no file**
([[concepts-are-non-functional-requirements-and-entities-are-functional]]) and **an agent-type choice
that changes what the search can see at all** — `agent: Explore` loads no `CLAUDE.md`
([[the-vault-ask-skill]]).

**So the tail the channel drops is not random.** Both of those are decisions the session took or heard
without recognising them as decisions, which is exactly the class a mark cannot catch: the channel
records what a session **noticed**, and what it did not notice is what reading finds.

**The channel also cannot mark a retraction.** One line filed as the owner's decision — *a page
citing a decision permits archiving it* — was withdrawn by him two turns later, and the mark stands
with no sign of it. The session separated them by hand at step 5, and nothing mechanical would have.
