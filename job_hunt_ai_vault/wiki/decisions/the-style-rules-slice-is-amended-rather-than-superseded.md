# The ruling, 2026-08-25

> **owner:** «да, согласен. Только по нашим же правилам - это должно стать инструкцией. Как ты это
> планируешь изменить и куда внести как инструкцию?»

> **owner:** «Главное - добавить туда "Простой язык по умолчанию. Не по запросу". Он пока что хорошо
> себя показывал всегда»

> **owner:** «это поправка»

**The changes to how a session writes become an instruction rather than a promise**, and they land in
the slice of the rule that already governs the repository's `CLAUDE.md`
([[write-plainly-name-the-source-and-say-what-is-not-done]]).

**It is an amendment and not a supersession.** The rule's claim is unchanged — write so the owner can
read it — so no successor is written, the page does not move to `archive/`, and the old text stays on
the page beside the new, dated ([[a-rule-ships-one-piece-of-text-or-it-is-more-than-one-rule]]).

# What the slice now says, and what moved

**The plain-language line absorbs the line that used to say KISS**, and it carries *by default, never
on request*. **The answer-first line gains that the reasoning below it must be skippable.** **One
question per message is added.**

**The line about page slugs in chat was dropped by the session**, a narrowing of its own proposal,
announced rather than done quietly ([[narrowing-a-scope-notifies-and-widening-it-asks]]):

> **owner:** «не стоит так переживать за slug в чате - это меньшая из проблем.»

**And the counter that would have measured slugs in chat is not built.**

# The cause is measured, not guessed

**He asked for a plain-language explanation three times in one session** — on the measure section, on
the page the sweep's instrument cannot reach, and on the two routed gauges — **and each plain version
landed immediately.**

**Two of the things he had to ask for were already in the slice**: give the answer in the first
sentence, and KISS. **So the failure was compliance, not coverage** — which is the argument for
strengthening the wording rather than adding a rule
([[a-drifting-list-is-repaired-by-deleting-it-not-extending-it]]).

# What travels and what does not

**One of the changes is not about prose at all.** Deciding a reversible fork instead of presenting it
is about *when* a session asks, so it becomes a page of its own rather than a line in a rule about
writing ([[a-reversible-fork-is-decided-rather-than-presented]]).
