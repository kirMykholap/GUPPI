# What was deferred, 2026-08-29

**Nobody measured what the sweep took away from `/pre-task`, and nobody is going to before its next
run.** The sweep of 2026-08-29 moved **65 decisions** out of the set that ritual enumerates, four of
them rulings from 2026-08-28 ([[the-sweep-runs-for-real-over-every-criterion-and-records-what-it-moved]]).

**Rejected: measuring it before the sweep ran**, which is what the session recommended when it argued
for holding the `decided_by:` criterion back. The owner ran the sweep over every criterion instead.

# Why it is a real loss and not a bookkeeping one

**The claims survive; the field does not.** A decision archived because a live page names it in
`decided_by:` has had its content carried forward onto that page, so nothing is lost to a reader who
opens it. But **`binding:` exists only on a decision page**, and `binding:` is what phase 1 filters on
to answer *what stops this plan* ([[a-decision-declares-whether-it-binds]],
[[the-pre-task-keeps-deferral-in-phase-one-and-drops-divergence-alone]]).

**So the enumeration that answers the ritual's own question is smaller by 65 pages and nothing says by
how much it is worse.** A page in `archive/` is not swept, not checked and not read by `/pre-task`.

**The first reading is its next run**, and the two counts that run returns — how many pages the
shortlist named and how many were opened — are the only series that can show it
([[the-pre-task-agent]]).

# What would close it

**A comparison of one plan checked twice**, before and after, which is unavailable: the corpus has
already moved. **What is available is the trend across runs**, and the run of 2026-08-29 gives the
last reading over the full corpus — 123 shortlisted, 49 opened in full and 8 in part, fourteen
corrections against a forty-item plan.

# The first readings, and the framing was wrong before it was checked — 2026-08-29

**26 of the 239 archived pages carry `binding: prohibition` with no `superseded_by:` and no
`processed`**, so they are outside every `/pre-task` enumeration.

**The shortlist density per item did not fall.** 71 shortlisted and 38 full plus 7 partial over 11
items, against 123/49/8 over 40 items the day before — about 6.5 per item both times
([[the-pre-task-agent]]).

**It does not mean nothing was lost.** On the first run the single most load-bearing page against the
plan, `the-harness-is-compiled-not-maintained`, was unreachable from the catalogue and was found by
following a wikilink. **On the second, three binding decisions were in `archive/`, two of which rewrote
plan items outright** — all three reached by following wikilinks out of live proposals. **Second and
third consecutive runs of that shape.**

## Checked rather than repeated, the claim changed

**The session asserted twice that archiving had put constraints out of reach and framed it as the
defect.** Reading the live pages instead: **the decisions-agent cadence ruling IS carried by its live
proposal, at lines 16 and 49-50 — nothing was lost there.** One claim intact, one genuinely lost.

**So the right question is not whether `/pre-task` reads `archive/`** — the owner is right that it
should not — **but whether the citing page carries the claim.** For `the-lessons-collector-is-a-reading-dispatch`
it did not: four live proposals name it in `decided_by:` and every one of them is a draft it produced
([[a-decision-is-processed-when-a-live-page-names-it-in-decided-by]], [[the-lessons-agent]]).

**More precisely than the first framing:** the decision *pages* left and their claims stayed on live
pages `/pre-task` keeps whole. **What is lost is the decision's own prose** — the reasoning and the
rejected alternative — **and whether that matters is exactly what the census would measure**
([[the-decisions-folder-is-censused-before-its-type-system-is-redesigned]]).

# The next reading in the series — 2026-08-31

**148 pages on the shortlist, 33 opened in full.** The previous reading was 123 shortlisted, 49 opened
in full and 8 in part, so the shortlist grew and the reading shrank.

**The pair was not reported in the form the template asks for** and is written here because nothing else
records it: the two counts come out of the agent's own answer, never out of
`.superpowers/sdd/pre-task-ledger.tsv`, and neither can be recovered afterwards.

**Read it against the sweep of the same day.** 129 decisions left `wiki/` after this run, and
`bash scripts/vault-catalogue.sh` does not print `archive/` — so the next reading of this series is
taken over a different corpus, and a fall in the shortlist will mean the folder shrank rather than that
phase 1 got sharper. **The series is only comparable across a stable enumeration**, and it stopped being
one on 2026-08-31.
