# The incident

**The retro of 2026-08-30 put sixteen open questions to the owner and he said he did not know how to
act.** He deferred all sixteen: «Да, откладываем и идем к handoff».

**Ten of the sixteen predate the session.** Six are the brief's own carried alarms, some running a
fourth, fifth and sixth edition. Five more are places where the plan said one thing and the machinery
permitted only another — **each already written into the file it changed, with its ground**, so they
were not questions at all but changes owed a look in a `git diff`.

**Only about a third of the list was new that evening.**

# The defect is presentation, not the count

**The retro shows carried items and new ones in one undifferentiated list, with nothing saying which
arrived today.** Sixteen items of one apparent kind is a pile; six new questions plus six known alarms
plus five diffs to review is three short lists, each with a different action.

**The same list in a different shape had worked hours earlier that day.** The owner said so
unprompted about the frontier: «Ты удачно указал короткое описание и мне понятнго о чем именно пойдет
речь». **A numbered list with a one-line description per item was legible; the same material as a flat
enumeration was not.** That comparison is inside one session, which is what makes it evidence rather
than a preference.

# What it costs

**A pile is deferred whole.** Nothing in it is judged on its own merits, including the items that were
new and cheap. Six of the brief's alarms therefore enter a further edition unanswered
([[every-open-question-of-the-retro-is-deferred-with-a-destination]]), and an advisory that keeps
being carried is one nobody pays
([[an-advisory-carrying-a-debt-count-does-not-get-paid-down]]).

# The general shape

**When a list is put to a person for a decision, its items must be separable by what he would do about
them.** *New question*, *carried alarm* and *already-done, please review* are three actions. Merging
them into one list does not save his time; it costs the whole list.

**The same evening produced the reverse failure in the reply channel**: the ratification step put its
list to him with no statement of what the step was or what he was being asked to check
([[divergence-the-retros-ratification-step-was-abandoned-on-the-owners-word]]). **A list without its
kinds and a list without its purpose fail the same way** — he cannot tell what action any row wants.

**The brief now carries less than the session knew** because of it, and the inheritance rule makes that
expensive: a session reads the brief and the feedback file and nothing else
([[a-session-does-not-read-the-previous-sessions-handoff]]).

# A second mechanism produced the same failure the same day, and the sorting was the whole of the work

**`/pre-task` returned twenty live prohibitions as one pile and the owner read the day as blocked
again**: «А теперь мы опять вернулись в состояние "нам мешает куча правил"… У нас был практически
готовый план, а теперь я не знаю что мы делаем. Опять.»

**Every line of that report was true.** Nothing in it was a false finding, and it was still unusable.

**The same content re-sorted restored the plan in one exchange** — three mechanical breakages on one
side, and on the other a list of rules he was already in the act of lifting. **The sorting, not the
finding, was the work**, and it should have been in the first message rather than the second
([[divergence-the-pre-task-was-dispatched-on-a-session-the-owner-had-declared-an-override]]).

**Why this is worth more than a second instance of the same claim.** The page's original evidence was
one channel — a list a session composed for him. This is a **different mechanism**, an agent's report,
produced by no one's presentation choice, failing the same way. **That is what turns a preference about
how to write a list into a property of the reader**: an undifferentiated report is unusable even when
every line in it is true, whoever assembled it.
