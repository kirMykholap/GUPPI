# What happened, 2026-08-29

**A plan of forty decisions went to `/pre-task` and came back with fourteen corrections.** None of the
fourteen named the obligation that a retired page's slug leaves `authority.md` before the page moves —
**because the obligation was in none of the forty items.**

**The sweep then moved 239 pages, left 239 stale register rows, and reddened the gate on every one of
them.** `lock-the-librarian.ps1` read the red gate as a refusal to dispatch, which locked out the only
party permitted to write the register ([[the-librarian-lock-hook]],
[[a-lock-on-a-red-tree-hides-the-health-of-what-it-guards]]).

**The deadlock was real and was broken by repealing the rule, not by satisfying it**
([[the-authority-register-step-is-repealed-and-an-archived-page-keeps-its-slug]]).

# Why the check could not have caught it

**`/pre-task` answers per item of the plan.** That is its contract and it is what makes it cheap: it
reads the corpus for each thing the plan proposes to do. **An obligation the plan does not contain is
invisible to it**, and nothing in the ritual notices the difference between *checked and clean* and
*never asked* ([[the-pre-task-agent]]).

**The brief already carried the rule**, under *what must not be broken*. It was read at session start
and it was not carried into the plan, so it was never put to the checker.

# The rule

**When a plan touches the vault's own machinery, every line of the brief's *what must not be broken*
list becomes an item of the plan** — even the lines the plan has no intention of going near. The point
is not that the plan proposes to break them; it is that `/pre-task` can only speak about what it is
given.

**The cost is a handful of items that come back clean.** The cost of not doing it, once, was 239 stale
rows and a deadlock between two guards ([[a-plan-can-be-factually-wrong-about-the-tree-and-the-pre-task-is-what-reads-the-tree]]).

# What the run was worth anyway

**The shortlist named 123 pages and the agent opened 49 in full plus 8 in part** — the widest and
deepest reading either count has recorded here. **Fourteen corrections against forty items**, the
largest a collision between two items of the same day that neither item's own page could see.

**So the ritual paid for itself and still missed this**, which is the part worth remembering: a good
answer to the wrong question reads exactly like a good answer.

# It was obeyed in part on 2026-08-30, and the part that was dropped is the measurable one

**Twelve of the fifteen lines became items, and the session wrote them in its own words rather than
in the brief's.** The rule asks for **each** line to become an item, and paraphrasing is what makes
"twelve of fifteen" a judgement rather than a count — nothing can compare a reworded item against
the line it came from.

**`/pre-task` then returned six findings that hit the phases directly**, which is the same yield the
readings above record: **the enumeration does not replace the check**, and a plan that enumerates
twelve of fifteen constraints is still checked against all of them by something else.

**On this session the check was harmful in another way and that is recorded separately** — the owner
had declared the session an override, and the report could not tell a rule that breaks the machine
from a rule he was in the act of lifting
([[divergence-the-pre-task-was-dispatched-on-a-session-the-owner-had-declared-an-override]]).
