# Raised and ruled on 2026-08-21, and not built

**The rule: a block attributed to the owner is written in his own Russian.** Where a session
understood rather than heard, it writes in its own voice and does not sign.

**The owner ruled on its shape before anything was built** — it stands, its verdict is an advisory
rather than red, and it does not enter the core of the repository's `CLAUDE.md`
([[the-quote-is-cyrillic-rule-stands-as-an-advisory-and-stays-out-of-the-core]]). **What is unbuilt
is the mechanism, not the decision.**

**He set it first in the order of the next session's work, ahead of the pre-task chain.** That
ordering is his ([[a-pre-task-agent-and-a-post-tool-use-hook-that-writes-its-row]]).

# What it would take

- **A `wiki/rules/` page carrying the text**, governing `.claude/skills/handoff/SKILL.md` and
  `.claude/skills/ingest/SKILL.md` — the two places an attributed line is written down. It is **the
  second customer for the `governs:` list** that landed the same day, and the first rule page here to
  name more than one destination ([[governs-takes-a-bare-path-or-an-inline-list-and-the-block-form-is-red]],
  [[a-region-is-a-paired-comment-named-for-a-rule]]).
- **A numbered advisory verdict in `scripts/vault-check.sh`**, and cases in both directions — an
  advisory can only be tested by reading the output, never by the exit code
  ([[a-clean-run-cannot-test-an-advisory-exemption]],
  [[a-new-verdict-is-proved-in-the-real-tree-because-the-check-cannot-see-a-sandbox]]).

# What it would print on the corpus as it stands

**25 of the 53 owner-attributed blocks carry no Cyrillic**, across 44 pages, measured 2026-08-21.
Every one of them would print on the first run, which is the standing objection to an always-firing
advisory and is a question for whoever builds it, not for this page
([[an-alarm-that-fires-when-nothing-is-wrong-is-one-you-learn-not-to-read]],
[[a-paraphrase-signed-owner-becomes-a-quote-and-is-reasoned-from-as-his-word]]).

# What it cannot do, by the owner's own counter-example

**A genuine English quotation from the owner exists as a case**, which is why the verdict is
advisory and why the rule is not universal. **An alphabet separates a quotation from a paraphrase;
it says nothing about whether the paraphrase is faithful**, and only the owner can
([[the-retro-ratifies-every-line-before-it-is-attributed-to-the-owner]]).

# What is open

**Whether a paraphrase is demoted to `basis: inference`.** Offered at the retro and neither taken
nor refused, so a surviving paraphrase still outranks a page on `inference` and loses to nothing
([[a-question-asked-is-not-a-question-answered]]).

Retired with `superseded_by:` the day it is built or refused

## The reader moves one hop earlier, into the catcher — and that hop now exists — 2026-08-26

**The lessons collector returned this as an append.** Its point: a verdict over `wiki/` arrives after
a page has been written, and the cheapest place to ask whether an attribution carries its words is
**where the line is filed**, not where the page is checked.

**That hop was built the same day.** `.claude/hooks/catch-the-feedback.ps1` notes a `decision:`
record whose shape declaration is *the owner said it* and which carries no guillemet quotation —
advisory, filed as written, never a refusal ([[the-feedback-catcher-hook]]).

**The predicate there is the absent quotation, not the alphabet**, and the two populations are
different: over `log.md` the quotation test is a strict superset of the alphabet one; over `wiki/`
it is not, and this page's own measurement about the older block spelling is why
([[the-alphabet-test-behind-the-owner-block-advisory-is-overturned]]).

**So the append is now a description of something that exists**, and what still lives only on this
page is unchanged: whether a surviving paraphrase is demoted to `basis: inference`.
