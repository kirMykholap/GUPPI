# What it would be

**Phase 1 of `/pre-task` stops discarding `wiki/lessons/` by folder name and discards by what a page
is.** A lesson recording an incident stays out; a lesson stating a standing procedural rule comes in.

**The mechanism is deliberately not designed here.** What is proposed is that the exclusion stop being
unconditional.

**This is the compilation of `docs/the-pre-task-enumeration-reaches-a-lesson-that-states-a-standing-rule.md`,
written by [[the-lessons-agent]].** It proposes an edit to `.claude/agents/pre-task.md` **and it asks
the owner to revisit a ruling of his own**, which a session may not do by itself — so what follows is
the case for asking rather than the change.

# Why it is raised: the exclusion has now cost a finding four times

**The ruling is [[the-pre-task-enumeration-covers-every-folder-but-lessons]]**, `binding: deferral`,
2026-08-21, his. Its ground in its own words: the lessons are a work list, so *a plan check reading
them would be reading intentions as constraints.*

**That page already records three runs on which the exclusion cost a finding** — 2026-08-25 twice and
2026-08-28 once. **This journal is the fourth**: the requirement that a plan touching the vault's own
machinery enumerate the brief's *what must not be broken* list as items lives at
[[a-plan-touching-the-vaults-own-machinery-enumerates-what-must-not-be-broken-as-items]], and the run
reached it **only because the caller named its slug.**

**So a rule stated in `lessons/` is invisible to the ritual that is supposed to apply it**, and the
one page saying how a plan over vault machinery must be shaped sits in the folder the plan checker is
told not to open.

## The arithmetic the ruling rested on has collapsed

**`lessons/` held 121 pages when the exclusion was ruled and holds 18 now**, after the sweep of
2026-08-29. The cost the exclusion bought — a cheap enumeration — is no longer worth buying: the
folder is the smallest in `wiki/` bar `rules/`.

**The ruling's stated ground has already gone false once and was dated in place rather than
re-taken.** It says the lessons are *to become a queue drained at ingest*; the drain moved to a
`/vault-lint` dispatch on 2026-08-24 and is not an ingest step. The exclusion survived that correction
untouched — correct process, and also how a ruling outlives its reason.

## The queue's own criterion marked both recurring lessons as handled

**The two lessons this journal recurred on are both cited twice from outside `lessons/`**, so
`scripts/lessons-queue.py` reads both as `CITED`, which is the loop's end state. **Both recurred
anyway, within days.** That is two fresh instances of a defect already recorded and since archived —
*the queue counts a cited lesson as handled*. **A citation is a trace that somebody read the page; it
is not evidence that the failure stopped** ([[a-citation-is-not-a-test-in-a-corpus-this-densely-linked]]).

**Eleven of the eighteen live lessons are cited by nothing outside the folder at all**, so the folder
is mostly unread, while the two pages that *are* read are the two that failed again.

# What it would take

**One line of `.claude/agents/pre-task.md` and a way to tell the two kinds apart.** Line 116 reads
*"Drop `## lessons` and `## archive`"*. The `archive/` half is not touched by this.

1. **A field on the lesson page.** Cheapest to read, and the shape this vault has already refused: a
   page declaring itself changes what an instrument sees and nothing checks the declaration
   ([[the-quantity-field-is-rejected-and-the-rule-stays-unconditional]]).
2. **The folder splits.** A lesson stating a standing rule is arguably a `rule` — except a rule must
   name a `governs:` destination, and *enumerate the constraint list as items* is addressed to a plan,
   which is not a file in this repository.
3. **Enumerate `lessons/` whole and let the shortlist narrow.** Eighteen rows added to a pass that
   already prints hundreds. **The cheapest today, and it would not have been in August.**

**Route 3 is what the ruling's own discharge section endorses for the other four folders** — *the
reading is narrowed by the shortlist rather than by the scope*.

# What is open

- **This reverses a ruling of the owner's and only he can.** The session's part is four datapoints and
  the changed arithmetic; this page does not assume the answer.
- **Whether route 3 reintroduces the harm the ruling named.** Eleven of eighteen live lessons are
  incident records and would be exactly the noise he was avoiding. Nobody has measured how a shortlist
  behaves when a sixth of its input is a work list.
- **What happens when `lessons/` grows back.** The folder was drained by a sweep, not by a policy; a
  ruling taken on today's size goes false without saying so
  ([[a-drifting-list-is-repaired-by-deleting-it-not-extending-it]]).
- **Whether the `archive/` half is the same question.** Two consecutive runs had their load-bearing
  constraints in `archive/`, all recovered by following wikilinks out of live pages. He has ruled that
  `/pre-task` should not read `archive/`; the right question is whether the citing page carries the
  claim, and that is a different proposal, named here so it is not folded into this one
  ([[what-the-pre-task-lost-to-the-decided-by-criterion-is-unmeasured]]).

# What it would oblige

- **A reading on the next run.** The two counts `/pre-task` returns — shortlisted and opened — are the
  only series showing whether a wider scope cost anything, and they are recorded per run on
  [[the-pre-task-agent]].
- **The ruling page is superseded if the exclusion is lifted and dated in place if it is only
  narrowed.** Which of the two depends on his answer, and this page does not pick.
- **`.claude/agents/pre-task.md` carries a rendered region**, so an edit inside it is a `wiki/rules/`
  write. The line at issue sits **outside** the region — recorded as lines 28–102 and re-read as 28–102
  on 2026-08-29 — so this is a session edit, and **the boundary is re-measured against the file before
  anyone touches it** rather than taken from here.

# What it would retire

**[[a-plan-touching-the-vaults-own-machinery-enumerates-what-must-not-be-broken-as-items]] — only if
the exclusion is lifted.** That page exists because the ritual could not reach the constraint, so the
plan had to carry it; a ritual that reaches it makes the page's remedy the wrong one.

**If the exclusion stands, this proposal retires nothing** and its whole product is the fourth
datapoint on the ruling page. That outcome is legitimate and is named so the page is not read as
assuming its own acceptance.
