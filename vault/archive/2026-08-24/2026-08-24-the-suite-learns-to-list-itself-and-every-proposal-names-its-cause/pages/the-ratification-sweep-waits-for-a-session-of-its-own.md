# The deferral, 2026-08-22

**The sweep happens next session**, as its own work: the session brings the owner each
owner-attributed block **with its text**, and acts only on what he disowns.

**Rejected: doing it today, at the end of a session that had already spent itself.** Blocks read
consecutively are read carelessly by the tenth, and carelessness here costs a rule rather than a
minute — which is the exact failure the sweep exists to undo
([[a-paraphrase-signed-owner-becomes-a-quote-and-is-reasoned-from-as-his-word]]).

**The list is the advisory, read from the run rather than from a page.**
`bash scripts/vault-check.sh --lint` names the blocks carrying no Cyrillic, one `path:line` per line
([[the-quote-is-cyrillic-rule-stands-as-an-advisory-and-stays-out-of-the-core]]). **A figure written
here would be the copy that drifts**
([[a-drifting-list-is-repaired-by-deleting-it-not-extending-it]]).

# [[a-refusal-is-conditional-or-absolute-and-nothing-else]] goes to the top of it

**It is `basis: human`, `binding: prohibition`, and the ruling it states is absent from the source it
cites** — re-checked at two ingests, and marked on the page itself rather than acted on.

**Striking a second prohibition on the session's reading would repeat the morning's failure
exactly.** The rule that was struck this week was struck because *the owner read the words and
disowned them*, and he has not read this one's text
([[the-retro-ratifies-every-line-before-it-is-attributed-to-the-owner]]).

**What waiting costs, stated rather than assumed: a prohibition nobody gave stays enforceable for a
third session.** That is the price of the safe direction, and it is paid deliberately.

# Why the whole sweep is his and not the librarian's

**Only he can separate a faithful paraphrase from a wrong one.** An alphabet separates a quotation
from a paraphrase and a menu separates a ruling from an invention; neither reaches the question the
sweep asks ([[the-retro-records-a-ruling-chosen-from-a-menu-and-ratifies-decisions-not-utterances]],
[[a-block-attributed-to-the-owner-is-written-in-his-own-russian]]).

**And the advisory is behind `--lint` for the duration**, so a bare gate run does not print the same
list until the sweep lands ([[the-tag-density-advisories-move-behind-lint]],
[[an-alarm-that-fires-when-nothing-is-wrong-is-one-you-learn-not-to-read]]).

# The first confirm landed, and this page had contemplated no confirm branch — 2026-08-22

**[[a-refusal-is-conditional-or-absolute-and-nothing-else]] was read to the owner on this date and he
confirmed it in his own words.** It is the page this one sent to the top of the list, and it is the
first block in the sweep to be **confirmed** rather than disowned.

**This page tells the session to act *only on what he disowns* and says nothing about what to do
when he does not.** That instruction is exactly right about the risk it was written for — a session
striking a rule on its own reading — and it leaves the other branch undefined: a page that was
flagged, shown and upheld is now different from a page that was never flagged, and nothing here says
where that difference is written.

**What was done, and it is a precedent rather than a ruling.** The block was marked
`> [!NOTE] RESOLVED BY THE OWNER, 2026-08-22` and left standing beside the `> [!CAUTION]` it
resolves; his words were written at the foot of the page in Russian; `decided:` was not moved,
because a confirmation is not a second decision. **Deleting the caution was refused**: a resolved
contradiction is still a thing that happened ([[the-index-is-a-map-not-a-catalogue]],
[[a-deprecated-rule-does-not-render]]).

**The procedure for a confirm is written down in a skill and in precedent, and in no `decisions/`
page.** `.claude/skills/vault-lint/SKILL.md` carries the marking shapes, and the tree carries pages
that used them; **the sweep's own decision page — this one — does not.** That is named here and not
repaired, because what the sweep does with a confirmed block is a judgement about the store's
procedure and belongs to the owner ([[a-question-asked-is-not-a-question-answered]],
[[knowing-a-rule-is-not-a-mechanism]]).

**The rest of the sweep is untouched.** One block of the list has been read to him; the advisory
behind `--lint` still names the others, and it is still read from the run rather than from a page
([[the-quote-is-cyrillic-rule-stands-as-an-advisory-and-stays-out-of-the-core]]).

# DIVERGENCE: the sweep was entered inside a build session — 2026-08-22

**This page rules the sweep a session of its own and rejects doing it at the end of a spent one. It
was entered at the end of a spent build session anyway.** The rejected alternative is the one this
page states: obey it, and bring the block on another day.

**What was done differently from what the page forbids**, recorded because it is why the departure
was cheap rather than why it was allowed:

- **the departure was announced before it was taken**, not reported afterwards
  ([[narrowing-a-scope-notifies-and-widening-it-asks]]);
- **one block was brought and not the list** — [[a-refusal-is-conditional-or-absolute-and-nothing-else]],
  the one this page sent to the top;
- **it produced the ratification**, ending three sessions of an unratified `prohibition`.

**It is a divergence anyway, and the outcome is not the test.** The reason this page gives for a
session of its own is that a question of this size decided at the end of a spent session is decided
badly — an argument about the conditions, which a good outcome does not retire
([[a-question-asked-is-not-a-question-answered]], [[the-maps-region-replaces-the-prose-it-duplicated]]).

**The rest of the sweep is unmoved**, and its blocks are still read from `bash scripts/vault-check.sh --lint`
rather than from a page.

# Deferred again on 2026-08-23, in one sentence, and it is now the second consecutive deferral

> **owner: отложить проверку честности еще раз**

**«Еще раз» is the whole of the content: he knows it is being deferred a second time and deferred
it anyway.** The mechanism proving `basis: human` goes to S4 with the tasks folder, by the same
day's narrowing of S3 to the plan phases he approved.

**Nothing about the reasoning above changes and nothing here re-argues it.** The sweep is still its
own work, the list is still read from `bash scripts/vault-check.sh --lint` rather than written
down, and the unratified block still goes first.

**What a second deferral does change is the standing of the corpus's `basis: human` pages while it
waits.** Each of them ranks above a page on `inference` or `code` in any conflict, and none of them
has been checked against him; that is the exact condition the sweep exists to end, and it is now
three sessions old ([[a-paraphrase-signed-owner-becomes-a-quote-and-is-reasoned-from-as-his-word]]).
**It is recorded and not argued** — deferring it is his, twice
([[a-vault-page-is-a-question-to-the-owner-not-a-settled-fact]]).

# Deferred a third consecutive time — 2026-08-23, and the first time with the list in front of him

> **owner: ужас конечно... Давай не сейчас**

**The list was shown before the deferral, which is new.** The two earlier deferrals were taken
against a described sweep; this one was taken against the 27 blocks across 22 pages that
`bash scripts/vault-check.sh --lint` prints, read to him.

**The consequence is unchanged and is now a fourth session old.** `basis: human` pages outrank
everything in a conflict, and none of these 27 blocks has been checked against him.

**Two of the 27 sit on pages the work of that session rests on**: the clause letting a session write
the schemas ([[what-the-gate-reads-as-its-own-input-is-the-sessions-to-write]]) and the ruling that
the legal values live in each folder's schema
([[the-legal-value-list-lives-in-each-folders-schema]]). Both were relied on all day, and both are
attributed to him by a block nobody has read back to him.

**The hook that would have fed this sweep was cut in the same session**, whole rather than in half
([[the-ratification-hook-is-cut-whole-and-what-does-not-fit-moves-to-the-next-session]]).

# Superseded 2026-08-24: the sweep ran, and the condition replaced the calendar

**The sweep of the owner-attributed blocks was completed on 2026-08-24**, inside a build session,
with the list assembled in advance and shown whole. **The owner changed this rule rather than
merely finding it broken**: a sweep may run inside a session when the list is assembled in advance
and shown whole
([[a-sweep-may-run-inside-a-session-when-the-list-is-assembled-in-advance-and-shown-whole]]).

**The break is recorded separately from the change**
([[divergence-the-ratification-sweep-ran-inside-a-build-session]]), because the outcome is not what
makes a departure legitimate — which is this page's own argument and the reason it is worth reading
after it stops being true.
