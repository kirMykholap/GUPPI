# The incident, 2026-08-22

**A `/pre-task` phase-2 read was dispatched, and what reached the librarian was its price.**
233,165 tokens, 49 tool uses, 10m40s, 65 pages opened over a 43-page shortlist — and not one of the
findings the run returned ([[the-pre-task-splits-into-a-cheap-enumeration-and-a-narrow-dispatch]]).

**The librarian refused to increment the run counter on that ground.**
[[a-pre-task-paid-for-itself-in-findings-nobody-would-have-made]] is the corpus's only evidence that
the ritual is worth its price, and it counts runs by what they returned. A run entered there as a
cost with no findings beside it **reads as a run that found nothing** — which is the opposite of what
happened, and it would have been written into the one page arguing for the ritual.

**The findings existed.** The run returned two, and both are load-bearing: that
`scripts/vault-catalogue.sh` prints no `binding:` column, so phase 1 as specified cannot be performed
from the catalogue alone; and that the `prohibition`-only filter drops the pages most load-bearing
for a plan whose subject is a ratified deferral. They reached the corpus a day later, through this
journal, and only because the retro asked for them.

# Why a cost travels and a finding does not

**The cost is produced by the harness and the finding is produced by the agent.** A subagent cannot
see its own usage, so the number is read off the caller's report and is *lying there* for whoever
writes the next brief ([[a-subagent-cannot-report-its-own-cost]]). The findings are in the answer,
which the caller has already acted on — and once acted on, they feel recorded.

**A cost is also the shape of a fact.** It is a row, it has units, it looks like something a
knowledge store wants. A finding is prose that has to be re-read and re-stated, and it is the half
that expires: the answer scrolls away with the session
([[deriving-what-the-vault-already-holds-costs-a-round]]).

# What it obliges

**A dispatch reported to the librarian is reported run-and-findings, or not reported.** A price with
no findings is not a partial record — it is a record that asserts something false about the run,
because the page it lands on counts value and not spend.

**The two are separable in the store and must be separated in the brief**: the price belongs on
[[agent-round-trip-cost]], which is about classes of dispatch, and the findings belong on the page
that argues the dispatch pays for itself. **A brief that carries one and calls it the run has chosen
which page gets to be wrong.**

**And the caller is the only party who can carry both.** The agent returns findings and cannot see
its cost; the harness reports a cost and cannot read the findings. Neither half reaches a page unless
the session writes it down ([[a-subagent-cannot-report-its-own-cost]]).
