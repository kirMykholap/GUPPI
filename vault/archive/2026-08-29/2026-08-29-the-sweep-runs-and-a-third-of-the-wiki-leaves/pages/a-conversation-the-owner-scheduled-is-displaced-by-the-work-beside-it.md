# The pattern, and it is one pattern rather than a list of misses

**Three things the owner deferred to *this* session, in his own words, each now moving into a fourth
consecutive session:**

- the seventh S2 criterion — whether this work earns one;
- a machine-readable format for `tags.md` and `authority.md`
  ([[a-machine-readable-format-for-the-registers]]);
- the fog finding ([[the-harness-is-no-longer-visible-to-its-owner]],
  [[the-fog-finding-is-recorded-and-not-acted-on]]).

**They were recorded correctly.** Each is on `sprint.md`'s next list, which is where a deferral was
ruled to live precisely so that it could not fall out of a regenerated brief
([[the-deferrals-of-this-session-go-onto-the-sprint]],
[[the-record-schema-sweep-and-the-trim-candidates-go-onto-the-sprint]]). **Being recorded is not
the failing half.**

# What the shape is

**The session opens on harness work, the harness work fills it, and the conversations sit beside it
unnoticed for its whole length.** A conversation has no artefact that fires — no hook, no gate, no
check — while a mechanism has a plan, a test and a diff pulling attention through it.

**So the displacement is structural rather than a lapse of attention.** The queue of things to
*build* is enforced by the tooling that builds them; the queue of things to *discuss* is enforced by
somebody remembering, which is the class of protection this project has already ruled insufficient
([[knowing-a-rule-is-not-a-mechanism]]).

# Why the count is the finding

**One occurrence is a busy session. Four consecutive ones is a property of how the sessions are
run.** The same reading was taken about a skipped ritual: a divergence recorded in consecutive
sessions is a recurrence rather than an incident
([[pre-task-was-skipped-again-and-the-record-is-a-recurrence-rather-than-an-incident]]).

**And the direction it fails in is the quiet one.** The work that got done is visible in the diff
and in the journal; the conversation that did not happen leaves nothing behind except a line on a
list that looks the same as it did yesterday
([[an-instrument-that-fails-towards-nothing-to-do]]).

# What is not decided here

**No remedy is proposed.** Whether these get a moment of their own — the way the pre-task ritual got
one — is the owner's, and offering him a mechanism is exactly the move this lesson says the sessions
already over-select ([[an-admission-test-for-mechanisms]], [[a-ritual-that-runs-unannounced-cannot-be-told-from-improvisation]]).

**It is reported in the brief instead**, which is the one artefact he reads at the start of a
session ([[the-growth-signal-is-the-brief]]).
