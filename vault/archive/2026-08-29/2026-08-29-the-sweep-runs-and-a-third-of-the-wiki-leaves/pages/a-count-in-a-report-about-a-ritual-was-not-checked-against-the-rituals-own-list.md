# The incident

**A session reported its retro as having answered one more line than it had.** The owner had
answered wider than one of the questions and left its specific fork open, so the line was addressed
and not closed.

**The list was in front of the session while it wrote the wrong count.**

# Why an off-by-one here is not trivial

**A retro's whole product is the list of lines and their state.** A count that runs one ahead says
a question was closed, and the next session reads a closed question as settled rather than as owed
([[a-question-asked-is-not-a-question-answered]]).

# The shape it shares with the day's other counting failure

**Both were numbers stated about a list the writer could see** — a suite counted by adding the
functions in view rather than reading the run ([[a-subtotal-read-as-a-total]]), and a retro counted
by impression rather than by walking its own lines. **Neither needed an instrument; both needed the
list re-read.**
