# The incident, 2026-08-28

**The dead-link finding was measured over `wiki/decisions/` and reported as a whole-corpus result:
76 live pages over 24 dead targets.** Over all of `wiki/` it is **210 pages over 42 targets, 424
link occurrences** — wrong by about a factor of three in the direction that understates the defect.

**Nothing in the reported figure named its population.** A reader had no way to tell a folder from
the corpus, and the decision it was about — whether to build the advisory at all — is priced on the
size ([[the-gate-advisory-on-a-live-link-into-a-deprecated-page-is-ordered-built]]).

# The same defect, in the other direction, in a brief

**A brief of 2026-08-26 quoted «120 pages still do» about the `*(Compiled from …)*` signature and
named no population.** None of four ways of counting produces 120. **The true readings, taken
2026-08-27:** 96 files across the whole vault carry the literal, 88 of them under `wiki/`, 118
occurrences. An earlier count of 100 / 89 / 128 came from a grep for `Compiled from` without the
opening bracket, which also caught prose *about* the signature
([[a-figure-measured-before-the-tree-moved-is-not-a-measurement-of-this-tree]]).

# The rule this argues for

**A number is published with the set it was counted over, or it is not published.** The population
is not context — it is half the measurement, and without it a slice and a census are the same
sentence ([[an-unsourced-number-is-dropped-not-chosen]],
[[a-projection-is-published-with-its-instrument-or-not-at-all]]).

**And a wider measurement replaces a narrower one by being stated beside it**, not by correcting the
earlier line: the earlier figure was true of its own slice
([[the-region-coverage-figure-is-dated-where-it-stands]]).
