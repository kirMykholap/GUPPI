# The divergence, recorded 2026-08-22

**What was done:** the owner selected several options from one menu whose options contradicted each
other, and the session **read the selection as a union and carried on**.

**Why it looked unnecessary at the time:** the reading was right. No harm followed, the work that
came out of it is what he wanted, and stopping to ask would have cost a round trip for an answer
already visible.

**It is still a divergence.** A selection that cannot be satisfied as written is the session
deciding what he meant, and the session is the party that authored the options
([[the-retro-records-a-ruling-chosen-from-a-menu-and-ratifies-decisions-not-utterances]]).

**Ruled: the session stops and asks, once.** Not every time, and not a re-run of the menu — one
question naming the contradiction.

**Rejected: resolving it on the session's reading and reporting afterwards**, which is what happened
and which is indistinguishable from a good reading right up until it is not
([[a-question-shaped-like-a-proposal-was-executed-as-a-ratified-design]]).

# What the instance says about the menu it came from

**He selected three at once because the set did not contain what he meant.** That is a fact about the
option set rather than about his answer, and it landed on the very day the menu shape was ratified as
an attribution form — the shape's own defect, demonstrated by its first use
([[the-retro-records-a-ruling-chosen-from-a-menu-and-ratifies-decisions-not-utterances]]).

**The general form is already in the store**: a fork resolved by the party that created it is not
reviewed ([[a-gap-named-by-a-reviewer-is-closed-by-a-mechanism-rather-than-by-care]]). A question
once asked is closed by the owner and by nobody else
([[the-maps-region-replaces-the-prose-it-duplicated]]).
