# The chain, and it runs every session

1. **The owner speaks Russian**, in chat.
2. **A session paraphrases him into English** and writes the paraphrase into its journal under
   `**owner:**` — the template's own marker for *his testimony, outranking the line beside it*.
3. **The librarian compiles it into a quote block**, stamped `basis: human`, which is the strongest
   ground this store has ([[a-vault-page-is-a-question-to-the-owner-not-a-settled-fact]]).
4. **Later sessions reason from it as his word**, because that is precisely what `basis: human` is
   for.
5. **Nothing ever shows him the paraphrase.**

**Every step is correct by its own rules. The chain is the defect.**

# What it cost, measured on the page that carried it

**`wiki/decisions/advisory-is-the-default-for-a-hook.md` stated a rule the owner says he never
gave** — «не было такого правила. Это правило неправильное», 2026-08-21. Its words are the English
paraphrase a session wrote into the journal of 2026-08-19.

**It stood for three days and decided three things**: the guard branch's advisory choice,
`check-the-plan.ps1`, and the write net. **It was one turn from deciding a fourth** — the two
condition test was about to block a step of an approved plan, and the obstacle was fabricated.

**It surfaced only because a plan was being checked against it.** Nothing else in this project reads
a decision page closely enough to notice that its quotation is not a quotation
([[a-pre-task-paid-for-itself-in-findings-nobody-would-have-made]]).

# How wide it is

**53 owner-attributed quote blocks across 44 `wiki/` pages, and 25 of them carry no Cyrillic** —
measured 2026-08-21 by classifying `grep -rhE '^> [*][*]owner' wiki --include=*.md` by codepage
range. **None of the 25 is in his words.**

**English does not prove he did not say it**, and the list is not a verdict. It is printed in full in
the journal this page was compiled from, and the instruction that came with it is: **act only on
what the owner disowns.** It is not copied here — a list copied out of its source drifts from it
([[a-copied-list-drifts-and-a-sweep-cannot]]).

**A second page was sampled at random and it does not hold up either.**
[[a-refusal-is-conditional-or-absolute-and-nothing-else]] is `basis: human` and
`binding: prohibition`, and the ruling it states is **absent from the source it cites** on two search
patterns. **Flagged and deliberately not struck**: striking a second prohibition on one session's
reading is the owner's call.

**The first reading of that measurement was reported with its figures swapped**, by the session
diagnosing the attribution defect, and it reached chat, the feedback file, a skill and a plan before
being caught ([[knowing-a-rule-is-not-a-mechanism]]).

# The remedies, and which of them exists

**The ratification step is built** and is the only one that can separate a *faithful* paraphrase
from a wrong one, because only the owner can
([[the-retro-ratifies-every-line-before-it-is-attributed-to-the-owner]]).

**The alphabet verdict is ruled and unbuilt**, and it is an advisory by his ruling because a genuine
English quotation from him would trip a red one
([[the-quote-is-cyrillic-rule-stands-as-an-advisory-and-stays-out-of-the-core]],
[[a-quote-is-cyrillic-rule-with-an-advisory-verdict]]).

**Demoting a paraphrase to `basis: inference` was offered and neither taken nor refused**, so the
conflict ranking still reads a paraphrase as his word wherever one survives
([[a-question-asked-is-not-a-question-answered]]).

# What a journal can do about it without any mechanism at all

**The journal this page comes from wrote every attributed line in his verbatim Russian, and wrote
everything it had merely understood in its own voice, unsigned.** That convention costs nothing and
is the whole of the repair at the source; the mechanisms above exist for the sessions that forget
it ([[an-intent-note-compiled-as-testimony-describes-things-nobody-built]]).

## The instrument is bytes, and naming it is the point of this section

**A `grep` with a Cyrillic character class reported this corpus's split reversed.** It agreed with
itself across two separate runs, which is how a wrong figure survived a correction and reached three
files — and it is why the table further up this page carries a disagreement rather than a repair.

**What now reads the corpus is UTF-8 lead bytes.** Cyrillic is U+0400–U+04FF, whose UTF-8 lead bytes
are exactly 208 to 211; the class is built from those codes under `LC_ALL=C`, so no locale can
reinterpret it, and it was proved on planted input before it was wired into rule 14 of
`scripts/vault-check.sh`. **This reading is that instrument's**, and it agrees with the re-measurement
in the table above rather than with the journal's correction.

**So: any future sweep of this kind names the reader it was taken with.** A figure whose instrument
is unnamed cannot be re-derived, and this one has now been wrong once in a way that two runs of the
same wrong tool could not detect ([[the-evidence-was-not-wrong-the-instrument-was-too-coarse]],
[[an-instrument-that-fails-towards-nothing-to-do]], [[a-figure-measured-before-the-tree-moved-is-not-a-measurement-of-this-tree]]).

## What is now built against the chain this page describes

**The alphabet verdict is no longer *ruled and unbuilt*.** Rule 14 of `bash scripts/vault-check.sh`
prints one line per block, behind `--lint`, and the rule page that reaches the session writing the
line is [[a-block-attributed-to-the-owner-is-written-in-his-own-russian]]
([[the-quote-is-cyrillic-rule-stands-as-an-advisory-and-stays-out-of-the-core]]).

**Neither of those catches a wrong paraphrase**, which is unchanged and is this page's whole point:
the ratification step is the only instrument that can, because only the owner can
([[the-retro-ratifies-every-line-before-it-is-attributed-to-the-owner]]).

# The page sampled at random held up, and the owner said so in his own words — 2026-08-22

**The section above says of [[a-refusal-is-conditional-or-absolute-and-nothing-else]] that *"a second
page was sampled at random and it does not hold up either"*. It held up.** He was shown that page's
text on this date and confirmed the ruling, unprompted and in his own Russian
([[the-ratification-sweep-waits-for-a-session-of-its-own]]).

**The sentence is dated rather than rewritten**, because it was a true report of what a search of the
cited source returned ([[the-date-of-a-correction-note-is-the-date-the-text-became-false]]).

**What did not hold up is the citation, and the two were never the same claim.** The ruling is his;
the journal named in that page's `sources:` still does not contain it. **This page's chain is about
the words being wrong, and the sample turned out to be a different defect wearing the same
symptom** — a real ruling filed against a source that cannot support it. **Both are found by the
same instrument and neither is found by any other**: only the owner reading the text.

**So the sample is one for one and not two for two, and it is a smaller number than it looks.** Two
pages have now been read to him: one he disowned and one he confirmed. **That is not a rate and is
not converted into one** ([[an-unsourced-number-is-dropped-not-chosen]]); what it does say is that
the flag on a page is not a prediction of the answer, which is the argument for showing him the text
rather than acting on the flag.
