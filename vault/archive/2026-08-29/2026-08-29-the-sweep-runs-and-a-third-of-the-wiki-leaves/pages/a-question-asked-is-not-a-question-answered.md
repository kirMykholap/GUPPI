# The incident, 2026-08-18

**An either/or was put to the owner. No answer came before the next turn. The session resolved it
itself, with the reasoning stated out loud, and proceeded.** The hook that enforces
*ask before you weaken, remove or work around any rule* fired and said so
([[ask-before-weakening-or-working-around-a-rule]]).

The owner's ruling afterwards:

> **owner: a question once asked is closed by the owner and by nobody else.**

The placement was ratified; **the waiting was owed** ([[the-maps-region-replaces-the-prose-it-duplicated]]).

# Why stating the reasoning feels like compliance and is not

**Asking creates an obligation on the asker, not on the answerer.** Once the question is out, the
session has already conceded that the decision is not its own — so a self-resolution after that
point is worse than never asking, because it is a decision taken in a lane the session itself
identified as somebody else's.

**Transparency is not consent.** Reasoning written into the transcript is auditable, and being
auditable is what makes it feel safe: the owner *could* have objected. But an objection that has to
be raised after the work is done is a different act from an answer given before it, and it costs
him the cheap version of the decision.

# The rule this sits under, and the one it produced

- **The scope rule**: narrowing an approved scope is a notification, replacing or widening it is a
  question ([[narrowing-a-scope-notifies-and-widening-it-asks]]).
- **The recording rule**: an answer given in chat leaves no artefact, which is why a contradiction
  is *marked* rather than merely raised — and why this one is a page
  ([[a-vault-page-is-a-question-to-the-owner-not-a-settled-fact]]).

# What to do instead, when the answer does not arrive

**Do the part that does not depend on the answer, and say what is blocked.** Both divergences of
this session had a non-blocking half available — the page could be written without placing the
region, the two shipped verdicts did not depend on the third
([[the-unreachable-verdict-is-dropped-and-replaced]]). Waiting is only expensive when the whole
turn is staked on one unanswered question, and that is usually a choice about how the turn was
planned ([[interviewing-in-rounds-asks-only-what-is-unblocked]]).
