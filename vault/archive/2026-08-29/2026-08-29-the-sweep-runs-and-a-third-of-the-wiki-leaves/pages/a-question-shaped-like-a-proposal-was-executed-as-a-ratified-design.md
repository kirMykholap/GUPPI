# The divergence, recorded 2026-08-20

**Asked to choose between three ways of bounding the pre-task ledger, the owner answered with none
of them.** His words: *«а что если ledger будет только на одну сессию? ... пускай на нем будет
процесс, который обнуляет ledger»*.

**What the session did with that:** answered the design question, **moved the trigger he had named**
from the closing ritual to `SessionStart`, and went straight to writing the plan. The next gate was
the plan's own approval, by which point the choice sat inside two hundred lines and the cheap moment
to say *no, at the end of the session* had gone
([[the-pre-task-ledger-holds-one-session-and-rotates-at-startup]]).

**He had selected none of the three offered options and was still asking what the ledger was.**

**Why it looked unnecessary at the time:** the tool that asked the question returned his text as an
*answer*, and an answer was read as a decision. The shape of the interface supplied the ratification
that the conversation had not.

**Rejected alternative: one turn**, bringing the changed design back for a yes or a no on its own,
before it reached a plan.

# What it cost besides the ratification

**He never got the answer he was reaching for** — *where are the rows kept, and does anything keep
them* — until he asked a second time, because the question was folded into a design instead of being
answered. A question absorbed into a proposal is a question not answered
([[a-librarian-answer-that-stops-at-the-agent-boundary-is-not-an-answer-the-owner-has]]).

**The tell was available at the time**, and reading it is
[[a-proposal-arriving-where-an-answer-was-expected-is-thinking-out-loud]].

**The repair is one question and it costs one turn.** It is written here as the repair the session
proposed and **not** as a ratified rule: nothing has ruled on it, and a rule that reaches an agent
is rendered rather than remembered ([[knowing-a-rule-is-not-a-mechanism]]).
