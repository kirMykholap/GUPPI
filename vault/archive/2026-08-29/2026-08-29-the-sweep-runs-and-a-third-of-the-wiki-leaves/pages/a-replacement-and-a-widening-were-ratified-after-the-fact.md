# What the rule says, and what happened

**Doing less than what was approved is announced and proceeds; doing something else, or more, waits
for an answer** ([[narrowing-a-scope-notifies-and-widening-it-asks]]). Both departures below were of
the second kind, and both were notified **after** they had happened.

| the departure | its kind |
|---|---|
| the `ingested:` mechanism — the plan named one thing and a different one was built | a **replacement**, and the **fourth instance of this exact shape** on the record here ([[the-ingested-line-stays-a-filename-and-the-hook-resolves-it-wherever-the-journal-lives]]) |
| cases added to `scripts/hook-selftest.ps1` that the plan did not name | a **widening** |

> **owner: ратифицирую оба.**

**They are recorded as divergences anyway.** Ratification does not undo the fact that the question
was never asked, and the artefact of a question asked late is not the artefact of a question asked
([[a-question-asked-is-not-a-question-answered]]).

# A third departure, minor and authorised in advance

**A probe hook was registered in `.claude/settings.json`** to measure whether a `Skill` matcher
fires. The owner had asked for that measurement, it was announced, and the file was restored with no
diff ([[the-ritual-records-itself-rather-than-being-detected]]).

**It is recorded because a temporary harness registration is still a harness change** — the
distinction that matters is authorised versus not, never permanent versus temporary
([[hand-edits-of-the-wiki-are-a-recorded-divergence]]).

# What this teaches and what it forbids

**Nothing.** A divergence records how a rule was already broken; the rule it was broken against is
unchanged and is where a reader goes ([[a-decision-declares-whether-it-binds]]).

**The reading worth carrying is the recurrence.** This is the fourth replacement of an approved step
recorded here, and a divergence in consecutive sessions is a recurrence rather than an incident
([[pre-task-was-skipped-again-and-the-record-is-a-recurrence-rather-than-an-incident]],
[[an-approved-scope-was-widened-by-a-step-that-was-forced-rather-than-chosen]],
[[an-approved-scope-was-widened-by-a-helper-extraction-that-was-chosen]]).

# It happened again on 2026-08-22, and that makes it a pattern rather than a day

**The `/pre-task` agent was given a clause widening what the ritual does, written on the session's
own finding and never put to the owner first.** The clause tells the agent to reach past phase 1's
`binding: prohibition` filter and read a deferral when the plan's subject is one
([[the-pre-task-agent]], [[the-pre-task-enumeration-covers-every-folder-but-lessons]]).

**owner selected: the clause reaching past the `binding:` filter stays in the agent.** Ratified after
the fact, as both departures above were.

**It is a widening by this page's own table** — the ritual does more than what was agreed, on a
finding the session made while performing it. The rule it departs from is unchanged:
*doing less than what was approved is announced and proceeds; doing something else, or more, waits
for an answer* ([[narrowing-a-scope-notifies-and-widening-it-asks]]).

**What the recurrence adds.** The first record could be read as one session's haste. Two, in
consecutive sessions, in the same direction — the widening is written, then shown — say the ratifying
step is being reached for as a repair rather than as a permission
([[pre-task-was-skipped-again-and-the-record-is-a-recurrence-rather-than-an-incident]]).
