# The finding, 2026-08-20, and it is the session's largest

**owner:** «Я вообще не понимаю что происходит в этой сессии. Все работает непонятно как.»

**Asked where the fog is, he was offered four candidates and selected all of them:**

- **the mechanism** — what the hooks, skills and scripts actually do;
- **the session's course** — what is happening while it happens;
- **the vault's register** — what the store now holds and what it says;
- **what changed on disk** — the tree after a night's work.

**A question offered as a narrowing that comes back whole is data.** It says the problem is not one
missing view but that no view is arriving, and it is the reason this is filed as a finding rather
than as a feature request.

# Why it bears on the sprint's goal rather than on comfort

**S2's goal is *the vault is used, not maintained*.** The owner's review is the only control this
project has — every ritual here ends in `git diff` and `log.md` read by him, and the librarian
writes unattended precisely because that review exists
([[the-librarian-writes-unattended]], [[a-sprint-closes-on-its-goal]]).

**A control cannot operate on something its operator cannot see.** That is the whole of the
argument, and it is why the finding is not softened by the fact that everything currently works.

# Part of the fog is the language, and that part is a rule change

**Everything durable in this repository is written in English by rule**; the only Russian he gets is
chat, and nothing keeps chat ([[migrating-the-repository-to-english]]). So the artefacts that would
make the harness visible are, by construction, in the language he does not read them fastest in.

**Any map that is ever built has a language question in front of it**, and that question is a change
to a standing rule and therefore his ([[ask-before-weakening-or-working-around-a-rule]]).

# What was done about it: recorded, not repaired

**Two repairs were offered and both were declined for tonight**
([[the-fog-finding-is-recorded-and-not-acted-on]]); the design of the first is
[[a-generated-harness-map]]. **owner:** «Записать, решать потом.»

# He reopened it himself on 2026-08-23, and it was never put back to him — `could-not-close:`

**His first message of that day was the same complaint from the inside**: he cannot read what this
project writes, and he asked for a style rule first, ahead of everything else
([[a-style-rule-for-the-prose-a-session-writes]]). **The session then spent the day on
schemas, refusals and an archive ruling, and the question of whether he now understands how the
thing being built works was never asked back.**

**The style rule was ratified twice and written nowhere**, so the one repair he named for the fog
did not land either, and its five lines survive only in a chat log.

**That is the pattern this page already carries and the third session it has held for**: the
finding is recorded, the repairs are all larger than the moment they arrive in, and the
conversation is displaced by the work beside it
([[the-fog-finding-is-recorded-and-not-acted-on]],
[[a-conversation-the-owner-scheduled-is-displaced-by-the-work-beside-it]]).

**The one countervailing measurement from that day** is that sixteen of twenty rulings arrived as
options this session composed, and on one of them he refused the frame and supplied a third
([[a-list-the-owner-must-confirm-is-prose-in-chat-and-not-a-rendered-menu]]). **A frame he can
break is a frame he can see through**, once out of twenty times anybody looked.
