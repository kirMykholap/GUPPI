# The ruling, 2026-08-21

**The owner designed it and the session did not build it.** At the end of an ingest the librarian
reads the lessons and produces two records from each — the fact into `concepts/` or `decisions/`,
the action into `proposals/` — and a lesson is archived only once nothing on it lives only there.

**Rejected: building it in the session that heard it.** That session was already changing what a
page must carry, and a second change to what the ingest does would have landed untested beside it.

# What it caused

**[[lessons-become-a-queue-drained-into-proposals]]** carries the design, the clause about when a
lesson may be archived, and the route the proposals take to the owner.

# What the deferral costs, and it is measurable

**`lessons/` grows monotonically and nothing asks what should be done about any of it.** It held
121 pages when the design was written. The folder is a store where the design makes it a queue.

**The first run of the collector was deferred again on 2026-08-24**, by the owner, in the same
sentence that deferred the instruments for the rules of `wiki/rules/`
([[the-instrument-work-and-the-first-lessons-collector-run-move-to-the-next-session]]).
