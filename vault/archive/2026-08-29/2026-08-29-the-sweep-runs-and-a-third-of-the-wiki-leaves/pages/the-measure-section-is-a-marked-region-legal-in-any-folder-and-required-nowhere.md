# What the owner selected, 2026-08-24

> **owner:** «Раздел... Принимается»

**A marked section — `<!-- measure -->` and its closing partner — legal in any folder, required
nowhere, with no frontmatter flag beside it.**

**Chosen against a frontmatter field**, which cannot hold the prose a measurement needs — a number,
its conditions, its date and its subject — and which is what the gate reads for verdicts, so a field
of prose would be a field no verdict can use.

**Chosen against a flag-plus-section pair.** A flag in frontmatter saying *this page carries a
measurement* restates what a sweep of the bodies can already see, and two records of one fact drift.

# It is a second customer of an existing mechanic, which is the argument for it

**The text slice on a rule page is already a machine-read region inside a body**, with
`scripts/vault-inject.sh` reading it and rules 11 and 12 of the gate checking both ends
([[wiki-rules-holds-the-rules-of-the-repository-claude-md]],
[[a-check-asks-by-running-the-writer-it-checks]]). Nothing new has to be invented to find a region
or to verify it.

# What is deferred, and it is most of the work

**The grammar of the section and its build are a session of their own**, on the owner's ruling. The
grammar has to be checked against the numbers already written across the corpus, which is reading
rather than design. **Nothing is built**, and the design that exists is on
[[the-measure-section-carries-a-numbers-role-and-its-grammar-is-not-designed]].

**Consequence, stated rather than discovered: every `measure:` line written between now and then
names a destination and has nowhere structured to land**
([[a-measure-line-names-its-destination-in-brackets-after-the-kind]]).
