# The ruling, 2026-08-21

**Stages one and two of the funnel were built that day and the delivery was not.**
`bash scripts/vault-catalogue.sh` prints `authority` and `title` in one pass, and `authority.md`
groups the slugs into families — and no skill, agent, hook or setting names either of them.

**Rejected: wiring it in the same session.** The session had just made `authority:` required across
the corpus and filled it; editing the agents and skills that read the vault on top of that would
have put two untested changes into one gate run.

# What it caused

**[[the-reading-funnel-is-delivered-as-a-procedure]]**, which names the stages and what delivery
would take.

# What the deferral costs

**An instrument exists and nobody is told to use it** — the same shape as the mechanisms found
built and wired to nothing in that session ([[knowing-a-rule-is-not-a-mechanism]]).
