# The sentence the predicate was derived from is his — 2026-08-28

> **owner:** «decison не может сам породить сущность - он может породить предложение по порождению
> этой сущности, а сам proposal уже следит за критериями попрождения сущности.»

**From it the session derived the mechanism: a decision is processed when some live page names it in
`decided_by:`.** The derivation is the session's; the sentence under it is his.

**And `decided_by:` is the mechanism, with nothing else admitted.** *(the owner selected it.)*
`sources:` means what a page was compiled **from**, not what **produced** it, and one field cannot
carry both meanings and still be readable by a script
([[decided-by-is-a-field-of-its-own-and-not-a-line-in-sources]]).

# No further mechanical predicate is coming

*(the owner selected it, 2026-08-28, after both mechanical predicates the session proposed failed
on measurement.)* **Everything beyond `decided_by:` and `binding: divergence` is decided by the
reading agent, on meaning** ([[the-decisions-agent-reads-only-and-runs-at-every-ingest]]).

**What the two failures were.** The loose predicate — *mentioned anywhere by a live proposal or
rule* — selects 181 of 329, but 120 of its 165 matches rest on prose in a page body and nothing
else. And *something cites it* selects almost the whole folder
([[a-citation-is-not-a-test-in-a-corpus-this-densely-linked]]).

# What the predicate does to the folder today, measured 2026-08-28

**`decided_by:` is carried by 59 pages** — 43 of 45 in `proposals/` and all 16 in `rules/`, and by
none in the other four folders. **They name 43 distinct targets, of which 40 are live decisions and
3 point into `archive/`.** So the mechanism is available today and it reaches 40 of 329 decisions,
which is 12.2 per cent of the folder.

**With `binding: divergence` added, the rule archives 60 of 329 and leaves 269.** The clauses
overlap on exactly one decision.

**Supersession selects nothing.** No live decision carries `superseded_by:` and none carries
`status: deprecated`; the 24 that carry `supersedes:` are the survivors rather than the candidates,
their predecessors having already been moved out unmarked.

# What it obliges

**The librarian sets `processed` only where a live page names the decision in `decided_by:`**, and
the page stays where it is until a sweep moves it
([[processed-means-the-decisions-content-moved-on-and-the-mark-opens-a-state]]).

**A `decided_by:` pointing into `archive/` is kept as it stands** and is a lesson candidate rather
than a defect to repair ([[a-decided-by-target-in-the-archive-is-kept-and-becomes-a-lesson-candidate]]).

**The remaining 269 are the reading agent's subject, and its yield is unmeasured** — the first run
is the measurement, and the range *between 150 and 269 will remain* is the price of not having
measured it.

# The reader that judges the rest now exists, and its vocabulary is here — 2026-08-29

**This page says everything beyond `decided_by:` and `binding: divergence` is decided by the reading
agent, on meaning.** That agent is built: [[the-decisions-agent]], two modes and five verdicts —
`stands`, `carried`, `false`, `undesigned`, `contradicts <slug>`.

**The fourth verdict is named `undesigned` and never `topic`.** `topic` was the session's coinage and
appears nowhere in this corpus; the owner selected the other word before anything cited either.

**`contradicts` is the verdict that justifies a separate reader at all.** Rule 3 of the gate compares
two `current` pages only under the *same* `authority:` slug and is blind the moment two pages disagree
under different ones. **Nothing mechanical will ever find that class.** Its first dispatch returned
seven such rows, including one pair nobody had asked about.

**And an `undesigned` deferral does not leave `wiki/decisions/`.** It stays, and the brief surfaces it
as an open question — the session's own recommendation that such pages move out was reversed the same
day ([[the-frontier-is-a-general-working-concept-and-a-sessions-list-lives-in-the-brief]]).

**Mode 2 adds a job this page's criterion cannot do**: group the decisions by the page each one acts on
and name which of a group is in force. Where two act on one page and neither supersedes the other, that
is a finding, not a choice. The stranding case itself is closed by the `processed` mark instead
([[the-librarian-sets-processed-when-it-writes-decided-by-and-the-archiver-reads-each-field]]).

## The criterion reads causation as carriage, and that is now measured

**`decided_by:` means the page was *caused* by the decision. The archival criterion reads it as "this
page carries that decision's claim."** They are not the same and the gap took a real page out of view.

**The worked case.** `the-lessons-collector-is-a-reading-dispatch-and-the-librarian-writes-what-it-returns`
left `wiki/` because four live proposals name it in `decided_by:` — a hook register, a `PreToolUse` net,
a plant-and-revert helper, a CR-byte census. **Every one of them is a draft it produced; none describes
it.** Its own design was carried by no live page at all until [[the-lessons-agent]] was written.

**The counter-case matters as much.** The session asserted twice that archiving had put constraints out
of `/pre-task`'s reach. Checked rather than repeated: the decisions-agent cadence ruling **is** carried
by its live proposal, so nothing was lost there. **The right question is not whether `/pre-task` reads
`archive/` — the owner is right that it should not — but whether the citing page carries the claim**
([[what-the-pre-task-lost-to-the-decided-by-criterion-is-unmeasured]]).

**A second collision on the same criterion.** `structure-work-agreed-and-not-executed` was taken by the
`decided_by:` predicate although it is a `deferral` whose term had not arrived — three live proposals
name it while several of its rows are undone — and the rule that a deferral leaves on its term and
**never on a citation** says it should have stayed. **The two clauses collide, and the collision is
visible only on a multi-row deferral** ([[a-decisions-archival-condition-is-read-off-its-binding]]).

# Retired 2026-08-30: a further mechanical predicate did come

**Superseded by [[the-sweep-reads-the-handshake-before-it-releases-a-decision]].** The title's clause
*"and no further mechanical predicate is coming"* is the part that went false, and it went false by
seventy-two hours.

**The handshake is that predicate.** A decision declares its target forward in plain text; the target
names the decision back in `decided_by:`; the release criterion is that both agree
([[a-decision-declares-its-target-and-the-handshake-closes-it]]).

**And the criterion this page named was reading the wrong arrow.** `decided_by:` records **causation**;
this page read it as **carriage**. That misreading has already taken a page out of view whose claim
nothing live carried — `the-lessons-collector-is-a-reading-dispatch` left `wiki/` because four live
proposals named it, and every one of them was a draft it had produced rather than a page describing
it.
