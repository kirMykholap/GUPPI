# What travels

<!-- text -->
**A question put to the owner carries its context.** What the thing is, how it works today, what
would be built, the fork if there is one, and a recommendation with its ground. A question missing
any of them is not ready to ask.

**The cost is the asker's.** Several questions that each need four paragraphs make a long message,
and that is the trade: the alternative was questions that could not be answered at all.

**It is not a licence to ask more.** One question per message still stands.
<!-- /text -->

# Why this page exists and why it was written a day late

**The decision was recorded on 2026-08-29 and the rule page was not written**, because a rule page
renders its text into a governed file and choosing that file is a harness change rather than a
librarian's call. The decision page said so in a section of its own
([[every-question-put-to-the-owner-carries-context-and-a-recommendation]]).

**The owner chose the destination in the session of 2026-08-29** — `CLAUDE.md`, under *How to write
it* — which is what the librarian had been waiting for, and it is the whole of what unblocked this
page.

# Where it lands, and what it stands beside

**The region sits inside `## How to write it`, beside
[[write-plainly-name-the-source-and-say-what-is-not-done]].** `governs:` is not unique across rules,
so two rules rendering into one file — and into one section of it — is the normal case rather than a
collision ([[a-region-is-a-paired-comment-named-for-a-rule]]).

**The one-question-per-message clause is repeated here deliberately.** It lives in the neighbouring
region as well, and a reader who meets this rule alone would otherwise read *carry more context* as
*ask more questions*, which is the misreading the decision page names.

# What it does not carry

**The measurement is not in the slice.** He said it after a list of fourteen bare questions, which he
answered with «я не могу догадываться о чем речь»; that stays on the decision page, because what
travels into a harness file is the imperative and never its justification
([[what-travels-from-a-rule-page-is-a-marked-slice]]).

**And the rule has no mechanical instrument.** Nothing counts a question's parts, and no check can:
the four parts are a judgement about prose. It is named here rather than left implicit, under the
standing obligation that every rule in `wiki/rules/` owes an instrument or a page saying why it has
none ([[every-rule-in-wiki-rules-owes-a-mechanical-instrument]]).

# The same session broke it once, in the act of recording it

**«часть F снята» was written to the owner with no statement of what part F was**, and he could not
evaluate it. The decision requiring context was in force at the time and had been taken that same
day. That is the first recorded breach and it is why this rule was worth rendering rather than
leaving on a decision page nobody reads before speaking.

# Retired 2026-08-30, absorbed whole into a wider rule

**Superseded by [[the-reply-is-a-report-answers-and-one-case]].** **owner:** «Поглощаем целиком».

**Its five obligations are strictly contained by the case schema**, which maps them one-to-one onto
four of its five parts and adds a fifth the older rule has not: *what the thing is* and *how it works
today* onto **Context**, *what would be built* onto **How it works after**, the fork onto **The
question**, the recommendation onto **Recommendation**, and then **Ratification**
([[the-reply-format-becomes-a-rule-and-the-context-rule-is-absorbed-whole]]).

**One line was carried into the successor's slice so the absorption lost nothing**: a question that is
**not** a case still carries its context. Without it a clarifying question would have escaped the
obligation this page existed for.

**Two of its paragraphs did not travel and did not need to.** *The cost is the asker's* is ground, and
ground stays off a slice by construction. *One question per message* is a bullet of
[[write-plainly-name-the-source-and-say-what-is-not-done]], which renders into the same section and is
untouched.
