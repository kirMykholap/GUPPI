# The ruling, 2026-08-24

> **owner:** «интересно. Оставим как интересный кейс, можно составить lesson.»

**`wiki/proposals/the-system-naming-which-hooks-block.md` names a cause that is `deprecated` and
lives in `archive/`.** It stays exactly as written.

**Rejected: repointing it at the successor.** The decision that caused the page is the one that
caused it; a retired decision does not stop having happened
([[what-an-archived-page-asserts-is-frozen-and-how-it-is-found-is-live]]).

# The gate already permits it, explicitly

**Rule 5c resolves the wikilink against `wiki/` and `archive/` alike** and accepts an archived
target. That is not an oversight it was decided to tolerate — it is what the field means: a cause is
a historical fact, and history is what `archive/` holds.

# What is open

**Whether a live page arguing from a retired cause is a defect is not settled here.** It is the same
shape as [[a-live-page-can-argue-from-a-deprecated-one]], one level down, and the owner marked it as
worth a lesson rather than a repair.
