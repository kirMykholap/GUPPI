# The ruling, 2026-08-24

> **owner:** «1) оставляем decided_by: пустым. Принимается»

**The field is optional in `wiki/proposals/`, so an absent field is legal**
([[decided-by-is-legal-in-proposals-and-rules-and-required-in-rules]]).

**Rejected: marking them `status: idea` to close the count.** `idea` asserts that nobody ruled on
the page, and about these pages that is false — a ruling exists and had never been compiled into a
`decisions/` page ([[an-idea-is-a-proposal-with-an-immature-status]]).

**A wrong field is worse than an absent one**, and this folder is where that trade is cheapest: the
absence is legal and the falsehood would have been silent.

# One page was moved out of the librarian's verdict, and the owner was told in the same turn

**[[the-suite-gets-a-scanner-exclusion-before-a-narrowed-gate]] was reported as an `idea`.** Its
second step was ordered by the owner on 2026-08-24 and executed that day, so *a half-thought nobody
ruled on* is false about it. It joined the leave-empty group instead.

> **owner:** «согласен»

**A verdict read off the corpus can be correct and already false**, which is what happened here: the
report was right about the record and stale against the day
([[the-brief-is-stamped-with-its-commit]]).

# The absence is temporary and the repair is an ingest rather than an edit

**Each of the six lacked a cause because a ruling was never compiled**, not because none was taken
([[the-missing-decision-pages-are-written-by-the-nearest-ingest]]).

# Two neighbouring rulings about the same field, taken the same day

**A page may name its cause twice** where both sentences are true
([[the-english-migration-page-keeps-its-sources-line-beside-its-decided-by-field]]), and **a cause
that has since been retired into `archive/` is kept as it stands**
([[a-decided-by-target-in-the-archive-is-kept-and-becomes-a-lesson-candidate]]). Both are the same
principle as this one: **the field records what happened, and a tidier field would record less.**
