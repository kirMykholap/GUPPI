# The ruling, 2026-08-24

**A line in a plan's ratification list names where it came from** — «сказано в разговоре, ход N» or
«впервые сформулировано здесь». Selected by the owner.

# His ground

**A plan is not always read closely, and nothing today separates a ruling he gave in conversation
from one the session wrote into the plan and he approved wholesale.** Approving a plan is a real
decision ([[the-retro-records-a-ruling-chosen-from-a-menu-and-ratifies-decisions-not-utterances]]),
but it is a different decision from having said the thing, and the record has been unable to tell
them apart.

**That is the mechanism behind the 53 owner-attributed blocks found across 44 pages**: nothing in
the chain from conversation to plan to page marked where the wording entered
([[a-paraphrase-signed-owner-becomes-a-quote-and-is-reasoned-from-as-his-word]],
[[the-retro-ratifies-every-line-before-it-is-attributed-to-the-owner]]).

# What was rejected, and why the cheaper repair won

**A fourth shape in the retro's ratification table** — more precise by the letter, and it moves a
rule page and arrives a month later ([[a-block-attributed-to-the-owner-is-written-in-his-own-russian]]).

**The plan is where the sourcing costs nothing**, because the session writing the line already knows
which of the two it is at the moment it writes it. Asking later is asking memory, which is the thing
this whole apparatus exists to stop relying on
([[the-floor-under-a-session-is-read-because-the-record-already-existed]]).

# It is checkable now, which it was not a week ago

**`scripts/session-dialogue.py` extracts the dialogue with its turn numbers**, so *turn N* is a
citation a later reader can follow rather than an assertion
([[the-session-dialogue-extract]]).
