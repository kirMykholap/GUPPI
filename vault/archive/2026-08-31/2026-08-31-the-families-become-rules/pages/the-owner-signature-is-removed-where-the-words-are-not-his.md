# The ruling, 2026-08-25

**The owner selected this from a list of options put to him**, so it is a selection and not an
utterance, and the block below is his answer to the questions the option left open
([[the-retro-records-a-ruling-chosen-from-a-menu-and-ratifies-decisions-not-utterances]]).

> **owner:** «1) basis: human остаётся на всех 23 страницах 2) Ссылка на ратификационную страницу
> в каждом переписанном блоке - примиается, делаем. 3) Дубликат остаётся дубликатом. Позже
> разберем детально»

**Where a block signed `owner:` carries the session's paraphrase rather than his words, the
signature comes off and the claim stays.** The block is rewritten in the session's own voice, and
the attribution of the *decision* stays in the prose beside it — he decided it, he did not say it
that way.

**`basis: human` stays on all of the pages.** The page still rests on his decision; what changes is
that it stops presenting a sentence as a quotation.

**Each rewritten block cites the ratification page**, so a reader who wants the provenance of the
claim can reach the list it was ratified in
([[the-ratification-is-one-decision-over-a-shown-list-and-not-a-signature-per-block]]).

**The duplicate stays a duplicate.** One block is carried on two pages, and merging it stays
deferred on his earlier ruling.

# What was rejected, and both alternatives were shown to him

**Rejected: marking a ratified block exempt**, which keeps the false quotation on the page and adds
a field to say it was checked. **Rejected: recovering his Russian from the transcripts** and
restoring it into each block — the words exist in the transcripts of the sessions that wrote them,
and he chose not to go back for them.

# It answers a sprint item carried since S2

**A paraphrase that survives ratification is not demoted to `basis: inference`.** That question was
offered at a retro and neither taken nor refused, and it has sat on the carried list ever since
([[a-paraphrase-signed-owner-becomes-a-quote-and-is-reasoned-from-as-his-word]]). **The answer is
that it stops being a quotation instead** — the basis is what the claim rests on, and it still rests
on him.

# What the repair does to the advisory, measured before it was chosen

**None of the owner-attributed blocks the advisory names is a genuine English quotation from him.**
The only candidate,
`wiki/decisions/a-claude-rules-file-is-generated-whole-and-deleted-by-its-own-marker.md:31`, traces
through its own `sources:` to
`raw/handoffs/2026-08-18-the-filename-half-lands-and-the-escape-hatch-falls.md:136`, where the
sentence sits inside the session's prose rather than as a marked utterance.

**So the repair empties the advisory rather than shrinking it**, which is what makes the alphabet
predicate removable at all ([[the-alphabet-test-behind-the-owner-block-advisory-is-overturned]]).

# Not done

**No block was rewritten on the day this was decided.** The sweep is the next session's, and it runs
before the predicate is cancelled, because the predicate is what produces the list.

# The population, read off the instrument rather than off the brief

**27 owner-attributed blocks across 23 pages** — 25 in `decisions/`, 1 in `lessons/`, 1 in `rules/`.

**The brief said 22 pages.** The figure here is read off `bash scripts/vault-check.sh --lint`, which
prints the count and then one `path:line` per block
([[a-stated-count-drifts-from-the-list-it-heads]], [[the-filename-advisory-prints-one-name-per-line]]).

**And the wider corpus is not that population.** `wiki/` holds 122 owner-attributed blocks in two
spellings, of which 75 carry no guillemets; the 27 are the English ones, leaving about 48 Cyrillic
blocks with no quotation marks that no current instrument can see
([[the-alphabet-test-behind-the-owner-block-advisory-is-overturned]]).
