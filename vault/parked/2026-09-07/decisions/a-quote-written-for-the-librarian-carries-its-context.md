# The ruling, 2026-08-25

> **owner:** «Цитаты моих слов, вырванных из контекста - это плохо... Иначе мы неизбежно будем
> встречать мои слова как "да, окей", а к чему это? - непонятно»

> **owner:** «1) Механизм записи цитаты вместе с контекстом. Да, должен быть контекст а не просто
> циатата. Это одно маленькое дополнение агенту, который пишет FEEDBACK в чат. 2) Пункт «что пошло
> хорошо» на ретро. Агент гвоорил, что такое уже есть.»

**A quotation of his words carries what it was said about.** It is one addition to the instruction the
session follows when it speaks a feedback block, not a new mechanism
([[speak-the-feedback-in-the-turn-and-the-hook-files-it]]).

**Without it the corpus accumulates «да, окей» with nothing to attach it to** — a mark that names a
shape and carries words that answer a question nobody recorded
([[a-mark-that-names-the-shape-of-a-decision-is-not-obliged-to-carry-the-words]]).

# The retro item needs nothing built

**`.claude/templates/retro.md` already carries `## Worth keeping`**, verified in the file, which holds
nine sections. He had proposed adding a *what went well* item so the librarian could build a lesson
out of his approving mentions; the section exists and was filled in this session's own journal.

**And the loss he was reacting to did not happen in the retro.** The journal carried the quotation
twice and it was lost **below**, at a `page:` verdict that named a page which then received the
neighbouring claim of the same paragraph. **A new retro section would not have prevented it**, which
is why the verdict half is queued as an instrument
([[the-page-verdict-is-checked-for-the-pages-existence-and-not-for-the-claim]]).

# Not done

**Nothing was added to any instruction.** The requirement lives on this page and in no file an agent
reads.
