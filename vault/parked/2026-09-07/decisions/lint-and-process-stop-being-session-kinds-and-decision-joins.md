# The ruling, 2026-08-24

**A session writes four kinds: `miss:`, `measure:`, `decision:`, `harness:`.** `lint:` and
`process:` remain words the **librarian** uses in `log.md` and stop being kinds a session may file
under.

| kind | what it reports | who writes it |
|---|---|---|
| `miss:` | something went wrong and where it surfaced | a session |
| `measure:` | a number, with what it does and does not mean | a session |
| `decision:` | what was decided, by whom, and against what | a session |
| `harness:` | a decided change to the files that instruct an agent | a session |
| `lint:` | a defect in the corpus — a stale claim, a contradiction, a copied list | the librarian, in `log.md` |
| `process:` | the ritual or the harness failed, independently of the corpus | the librarian, in `log.md` |

**`decision:` names which of the retro's three shapes it is** — *the owner said it*, *the owner
selected it*, *the session understood it* — and the shape is filed as it stands, never promoted one
step towards him ([[the-retro-records-a-ruling-chosen-from-a-menu-and-ratifies-decisions-not-utterances]]).

# It was decided on a count of every line ever written, not on an intuition

**All 254 feedback lines sessions have written across `raw/handoffs/` were classified.**
**58 of them were decisions with no kind of their own** — 9 filed as `lint:`, 21 as `process:`, 28
as `harness:`. **Twenty-one direct rulings, including the owner's own, were sitting under
`process:`.**

Population by kind, same pass: `process:` 65, `lint:` 53, `harness:` 52, `measure:` 45, `miss:` 39.
The residue after classification is **15 lines of the shape "X is built, Y is fixed"**, which git
already holds. **In `log.md` the same kinds total 704 lines**, so the librarian wrote 339 of them
itself — which is the arithmetic that ended the session's own claim that the librarian was the
author of the `lint:` and `process:` lines ([[journal-vault-brief-each-artefact-has-one-owner]]).

# What was rejected

**Three kinds, folding `harness` into `decision`.** [[harness-is-a-feedback-kind-of-its-own]] is a
live prohibition that had already refused that exact shape — a decided work item folded into a kind
that carries findings — and **a `/pre-task` found it before the plan was approved.** The argument
that saved `harness:` from `process:` in 2026-08-19 reaches `decision:` unchanged.

# What this supersedes, and why it is a supersession rather than a correction

**[[process-is-a-feedback-kind-for-the-ritual-not-the-corpus]] adopted `process:` as a kind a
session files under, and its table says so.** That table is false from this date: the kind exists
and its writer changed. A changed claim is replaced rather than edited
([[a-title-corrected-in-place-is-not-a-supersession]]).

**Nothing in `raw/` is rewritten.** A journal written before 2026-08-24 carries the old vocabulary,
and it transfers into `log.md` unchanged — what a session believed at the time is data, and the
librarian files a line under the kind it was written with
([[an-intent-note-compiled-as-testimony-describes-things-nobody-built]]).

# What it obliges, and the duplication it does not remove

**Five files name the vocabulary and all five were aligned by hand on 2026-08-24**:
`.superpowers/sdd/vault-feedback.md`, `.claude/templates/handoff.md`,
`.claude/skills/handoff/SKILL.md`, `.claude/skills/ingest/SKILL.md`, and — as the librarian's own
words — `log.md`.

**That is the same hand-keeping this store has already flagged and called *designed nowhere***
([[the-drain-and-the-template-disagree-about-the-feedback-vocabulary]],
[[a-copied-list-drifts-and-a-sweep-cannot]]). The durable fix is still a region in each file, and it
is still not built.

# The vocabulary was tested by a fifth kind and did not grow — 2026-08-26

**A session filed a `divergence:` line and the owner ruled it rewritten as `miss:`, with no fifth
kind minted** ([[the-divergence-kind-is-not-minted-and-the-line-is-refiled-as-a-miss]]).

**The axis the four were chosen on is what decided it: what cannot be recovered afterwards.** A
divergence is a rule and an action, both of which are in the tree, so it is recoverable — and the
widening of `miss:` to *anything that burned us*, decided on this page, already reaches it.

**The file accepted the unknown kind with a note rather than refusing it**, which is worth knowing:
the vocabulary is enforced at the ingest and not at the write.
