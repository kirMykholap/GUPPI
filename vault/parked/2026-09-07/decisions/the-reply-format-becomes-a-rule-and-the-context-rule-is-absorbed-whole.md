# Context

**The form the session's replies took on 2026-08-30 was the session's main product, and the owner
said so three times unprompted.** Each reply opened with the frontier as a numbered list carrying a
status per item, then reported what had been done, then answered his questions, then worked exactly
one case through a fixed schema — context, the question with its fork, how it works after, a
recommendation, and a ratification line.

**What existed before it was narrower.** [[every-question-to-the-owner-carries-its-context]] obliged
five things of a question — what the thing is, how it works today, what would be built, the fork, and
a recommendation with its ground — and said nothing about what a whole message is made of. It renders
into `CLAUDE.md` under *How to write it*, beside
[[write-plainly-name-the-source-and-say-what-is-not-done]], which is about how a sentence is written.

**Nothing in the harness enforced the new form.** It held because it was re-stated at the top of
every reply, which is the observation the rule's shape rests on.

# The question

**Does the reply format become a rule, and if so what happens to the rule already standing in that
section of `CLAUDE.md`?**

The fork the session put was amend against supersede. The five obligations of the older rule map
one-to-one onto four of the five sections of the case schema — *what the thing is* and *how it works
today* onto **Context**, *what would be built* onto **How it works after**, the fork onto **The
question**, the recommendation onto **Recommendation** — and the schema adds **Ratification**, which
the older rule has not. Its remaining two paragraphs are ground, which stays off a slice by
construction, and a restatement of *one question per message*, which the neighbouring prose rule
already carries as a bullet of its own.

# How it works after

**A new page lands in `wiki/rules/` under the slug this decision names, with `governs: CLAUDE.md` and
`render: region`, and its region replaces the superseded one under `## How to write it`.** The owner
asked for the block to be *changed* rather than added to: «записать правила в CLAUDE.md блок 'How to
write it' (изменить немного сам блок, не дополнить)».

**[[every-question-to-the-owner-carries-its-context]] is retired** — `status: deprecated`,
`superseded_by:` naming the successor, and a move to `archive/`.

**One line of the retired rule is carried into the new text and must not be lost**: a question that is
**not** a case still carries what the thing is, how it works today, what would be built, the fork and
a recommendation. Without it a clarifying question loses the obligation the older rule was written
for, and the absorption would be a weakening dressed as a merge.

**[[write-plainly-name-the-source-and-say-what-is-not-done]] is untouched.** All six of its bullets
were compared against the new text and none overlaps: the six are about how a sentence is written,
and this rule is about what a message is made of. *One question per message* survives there
unchanged and is not restated by the successor.

**The landing takes two parties and cannot happen in one act.** The librarian writes the page; the
paired `<!-- rule: -->` markers in `CLAUDE.md` are the session's
([[a-decision-with-a-rule-target-cannot-close-inside-a-single-ingest]]).

# Recommendation

**Supersede rather than amend**, because the older rule's obligations are strictly contained by the
new schema and an amendment would leave two regions in one section saying overlapping things. The
session put the containment as a mapping rather than as an assertion, so it could be checked.

# Ratification

**owner:** «Поглощаем целиком».

**The owner said it**, on 2026-08-30, against amending the older rule and leaving both regions
standing. He had also asked for the rule twice on his own initiative, the second time as: «Еще раз
хочу обратить твое внимание, что новый формат общения - очень удобен. Он обязательно должен стать
правилом как можно скорее».

# The rule text

**Written 2026-08-30, after the rule had already landed, and it is a back-fill rather than a
source.** The section became obligatory for a decision carrying `target_kind: rule` on the day this
page was the only one in the folder carrying that value, so the verdict that asks for it found this
page immediately. **The authoritative text is the marked slice on
[[the-reply-is-a-report-answers-and-one-case]]**, which is what the injector renders into
`CLAUDE.md`; this section records the clause the decision obliged, so a later reader sees what was
ratified without opening the rule.

Every reply opens with the frontier, printed as a numbered list with a one-line description and a
status per item — discussed, ratified, deferred, open. Then the reply is three things, in this
order: what the session did since the last message; the answers to the owner's questions; and
exactly one case. A case is worked by the decision schema — Context, The question, How it works
after, Recommendation, Ratification — in that order, and no case is opened until the previous one is
ratified or explicitly deferred.
