# The ruling, 2026-08-26, in his own words

> **owner:** «отклоняю твое предложение. Пока что лучше заплатим больше, но будем уверенны что
> пересказ на руках. Но стоит добавить к вопросу оптимизации - это большая тема.»

**Step 0 of `/retro` goes on reading `.superpowers/sdd/session-dialogue.md` whole**, every time,
whether or not the session's context was compacted. The proposal to make it conditional is refused.

**The cost is accepted rather than denied**: about 26,000 tokens, and it joins the new optimisation
topic as a member ([[optimisation-becomes-a-conversation-topic-of-its-own]]).

# The refusal was vindicated in the same turn

**The session's argument was that its own context already held the conversation**, so a copy would
add nothing. **The reading found a defect the conversation never contained**: the extract's generated
attention list groups by filed block rather than by line, so five of that session's own marks sit
under the wrong heading ([[the-session-dialogue-extract]]).

**The extract is not a transcript.** It carries a derived artefact as well, and nothing in a
session's context holds a list a script generated from it
([[an-argument-from-the-context-i-already-hold-cannot-see-a-derived-artefact]]).

# Rejected

**Rejected: reading the extract only after a compaction.** That is the proposal, and it rests on the
premise the same hour disproved.

# DIVERGENCE: the extract was not read on 2026-08-31, on the ground this page already refused

**The session of 2026-08-31 read none of the 134,036-byte extract beyond its headings.** Step 0 asks for
it whole, every time, and the session did not do it.

**Its ground is the rejected alternative above, restated.** That session's context had never been
compacted and it held every owner turn verbatim, so re-reading was priced at about 35,000 tokens to
recover what was already in hand — and the owner had asked that context be economised the same day
([[optimisation-becomes-a-conversation-topic-of-its-own]]).

**That ground was put to him on 2026-08-26 and refused in his own words**: «отклоняю твое предложение».
**So this is a divergence against a live prohibition and is recorded as one**, not as a correction to
the rule. The prohibition stands exactly as written.

**And the reason it stands is on the page above it.** The reading of 2026-08-26 was vindicated in the
same turn: it found a defect in a *derived artefact* that no amount of holding the transcript could
have surfaced ([[an-argument-from-the-context-i-already-hold-cannot-see-a-derived-artefact]]). **A
session that still holds every turn is exactly the session that believes it has nothing to gain**, and
that belief was measured false once already.

**The journal asks for step 0 to gain a condition. That is the refused proposal.** It is carried into
the brief as a question for the owner and **is not written into the skill**: narrowing a live
prohibition through a harness file is the working-around this store asks about first.
