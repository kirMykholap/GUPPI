# The ruling, 2026-08-29

**owner:** «мне кажется это вцелом неправильный подход. Пока что не трогаем, есть другие вопросы»

**Item 41 is parked, and the objection is unstated.** He said the whole strike-in-place approach may be
wrong; he did not say which part. **The ratify-or-narrow fork is neither ratified nor narrowed**, so
twenty-one wider lines stand in `sprint.md` beside a rule still asking for the narrow form
([[an-answered-item-leaves-the-next-list-by-being-struck-in-place]]).

**And he selected, separately, that the count is *removed* from the governing page's prose rather than
corrected.** Four different counts exist for one pass — the page says twenty-three, `sprint.md` carries
17 lines reading `struck 2026-08-27` and 8 reading `struck 2026-08-26`, commit `a75d4b5` says
twenty-two, and this session's plan said twenty-one. **Rejected: correcting the number**, which would
produce a fifth.

# What it obliges, and what stops the librarian obeying it in full

**The count has not been deleted from that page.** The librarian may not delete text, and that rule was
not lifted; what has been done instead is that the sentence is **marked false in a dated section** on
the page itself. **A session may delete it and the librarian may not**, and this is the note that says
so rather than a quiet substitution ([[a-drifting-list-is-repaired-by-deleting-it-not-extending-it]]).

**Whoever reopens item 41 inherits a parked item whose reason lives in one chat line.** That is the
cost of parking without stating the objection, and it is written down here because nothing else
records it.

# The dates on that page do not reproduce either

**`sprint.md` contains no `struck 2026-08-28`**, while both the governing page and the S3 item 41 line
date the pass to 2026-08-28. Every strike annotation in the file reads `struck 2026-08-27` or
`struck 2026-08-26`. **A grep for the date the pages name finds nothing**, and that is a second defect
in the same prose, untouched by the parking.

# The parking gains a destination, and the term has not arrived — 2026-08-29

**S3 item 41 moves out of the items discussion and into the conversation about what is done with
numbers on a page.** The owner selected it, from the session's recommendation that the strike form is a
numbers question end to end and that any answer given separately becomes a precedent there.

**So the item is no longer parked with nowhere to go.** It is parked *inside* agenda block 4, which is
where the two standing contradiction pairs and the four-disagreeing-counts `CAUTION` block also sit.

**The term has not arrived.** Block 4 was never opened — it is one of two of the owner's four agenda
blocks that did not happen on 2026-08-29 — so the wider strike form still stands unratified and the
narrow form is what this ingest used.
