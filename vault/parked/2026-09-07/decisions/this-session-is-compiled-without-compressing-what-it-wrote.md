# Context

**The day was spent agreeing to shrink the corpus, and compiling its journal grows it by about thirty
pages.** `wiki/` went 597 to 598 during the session itself, and the ingest adds the rest.

**The store has no measurement of what a compressed ingest would cost or save.** The nearest reading
is that the ingest of 2026-08-26 produced ten new pages against twenty-four dated sections appended to
existing ones, **and the share of the ten that could have been a section is unknown**
([[the-output-side-is-finished-today-and-does-not-close-the-corpuss-growth]]).

# The question

**Is this journal compiled as any other, or is the librarian instructed to write less of it?**

# How it works after

**Compiled as any other.** No instruction to compress, no page withheld, and the growth is the
ratified outcome rather than an accident.

**Today is this project's only evidence of the gate failing silently**, of a hook measured in a shape
it never runs in, and of a register deadlock reachable on one page. **Compressing exactly this session
would economise on the one thing the store exists for.**

**The admission rule still applies and is not a compression.** A claim an existing page can carry
becomes a dated section on that page rather than a new one
([[no-new-entity-where-an-existing-one-can-carry-the-claim]]) — that is the ordinary rule, applied at
its ordinary strength.

# Recommendation

**Compile it whole.** The session offered this and the ground with it: the day's one durable output is
the record of how the machine failed, and a store that drops the expensive record on the expensive day
has the ordering backwards.

**Rejected: instructing the librarian to write less.**

# Ratification

**owner selected, 2026-08-30: «принимаю твою рекомендацию».** He took the session's recommendation
against the alternative it had framed, having heard the arithmetic.
