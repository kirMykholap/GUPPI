# The measurement

**Seven of the thirty-nine `decision:` lines filed on 2026-08-30 carry an attribution but never name
the shape in words.** They say *"ratified by the owner approving the plan"* or *"His words:"* with a
quotation attached — without the phrase `/ingest` files on.

**The rest of the set is clean.** By shape: 25 *the owner said it*, 5 *the owner selected it*, 2 *the
session understood it*, and these 7 attributed without naming one. **All 31 quotations attributed to
him are verbatim Russian taken from his own turns** — zero English paraphrases signed as his words.

# Why a real quotation is not enough

**The kind exists precisely so the shape is stated rather than inferred.** A reader looking at
*"His words: «…»"* has to decide whether that was the owner **saying** a thing, **selecting** from
options the session framed, or the session **understanding** something and the owner not contradicting
it. Those three carry different weight and the librarian is forbidden to promote one to the next.

**The failure this guards against is older and worse.** The sweep of 2026-08-21 found **53
owner-attributed blocks across 44 pages**, 28 of them in English, and none of them his words
([[the-owner-signature-is-removed-where-the-words-are-not-his]]).

# The distinction this instance adds

**A verbatim quotation and a named shape are independent.** The 2026-08-21 failure was quotations that
were not his; this is quotations that **are** his with no statement of what he did with them. **A
corpus can pass the first test completely and still fail the second**, which is what happened here —
31 for 31 on verbatim, 7 short on shape.

**And it was found by the wrong instrument at the wrong cost.** The whole yield of the retro's
ratification step that evening was these seven lines
([[divergence-the-retros-ratification-step-was-abandoned-on-the-owners-word]]) — **a finding a grep
would have produced for nothing.**
