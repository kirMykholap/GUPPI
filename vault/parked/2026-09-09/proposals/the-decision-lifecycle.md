# What it would be

**A mechanical answer to the one thing the owner says he wants and cannot get.** He opened
2026-08-30 saying he had stopped understanding what the project does, and named it: «Я хочу получить
инстурмент, который будет подсказывать и доводить решения до реализации - а вместо этого мы
бесконечно что-то решаем.»

**Nothing in `scripts/` produces a list of decisions taken and not built.** 299 decision pages stood
with no way to ask which are open.

**The design is a chain**: a decision declares exactly one target; the target names the decision back
when it lands; the sweep releases the decision on that handshake and on nothing weaker; a refused
proposal takes its whole list with it.

**The key is `decision-lifecycle`**, and every feedback line belonging to this work carries it in the
bracket as `[proposal:decision-lifecycle]` — which is how `/retro` and `/handoff` assemble the body of
work from lines filed hours apart ([[a-case-that-grows-becomes-a-proposal-and-is-announced-when-it-happens]]).

# Decisions

prose carrying no link; an answered one is ticked and carries a resolving link with its verdict. Being
listed here closes nothing: a decision leaves `wiki/decisions/` on its own target being satisfied.

# What is open

**Eight of the twenty are ratified and unbuilt**, and they are the frontier this proposal carries into
the next session.

**The largest blocker is that `wiki/tasks/` does not exist.** Until it does, **no decision with
`target_kind: action` can close by any means** — and that is most of what this project decides
([[no-decision-with-an-action-target-can-close-until-tasks-exist]]).

**The census of `wiki/decisions/` stays owed.** The dated cut honours the prohibition of 2026-08-29
rather than lifting it ([[the-decisions-folder-is-censused-before-its-type-system-is-redesigned]]).
