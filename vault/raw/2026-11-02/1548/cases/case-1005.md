## Case 1005 — The hook finds the files that name what a Case changes

> **context:**
> `case-guppi` must find each file that names a page or a file its Case changes, by every name of it, and a member already in a list when its Case adds a new member. Both steps stand in the Render of `rule-case-build`, and both failed their reading on the build of Case 1001 in session 0254 (readings L17 and L18 of session 1337). For the names, the agent ran one Grep per slug and no Grep of the purpose lines, and it searched two pages over `.guppi/tests` only. For the list, its one search ran over `.guppi/{guppi,tests}`, none over `vault/wiki/` or `.claude/`, though the Case itself missed no text. The person chose to move the names search into the `SubagentStop` hook of `rule-case-check`. The hook already reads the `Read` calls of the agent through `read_outputs` of `.guppi/guppi/casecheck.py`, and already sends notes once through `check_answer` and `judge_and_store`, which store each note under its Case id and do not send it again. So Case 1005.1 adds one note per file that names what the Case changes and that the agent did not read. Case 1005.2 makes the agent answer each note in place of its own search. Case 1005.3 writes the list-member search as one fixed Grep. The rule of one judgment (`rule-one-judgment-one-implementation`) bounds 1005.1. A Grep of `"agents"|agents/|SKILL\.md|__module__` over `.guppi/guppi/` printed `render.py` and `recall.py`, and neither derives a name from a path. A Grep of `code_ref|implementation_ref` over `.guppi/guppi/` printed `generate.py` and `lint.py`. `REWORD_PATH_FIELDS` of `generate.py` lists the three path fields, and the task imports it. `PATH_FIELDS` of `lint.py` lists two fields for another judgment, whether a path exists. `cli.py` was read whole for `COMMANDS`. For the pages a Case updates or deletes, `items_of` of `prespec.py`, read whole, gives each item of a `then` block with its kind and its slug, and the task imports it. For the code spans of a task line, a Grep of `CODE_SPAN\b.*=|import.*CODE_SPAN|CODE_SPAN,` over `.guppi/guppi/` printed `lint.py`, `prespec.py`, `generate.py`, `pointers.py` and `config.py`. `CODE_SPAN` is set in `lint.py`, the other four import it, and the task imports it too. The fixes of `/pre-spec` changed two points of behaviour. First, the path a task line must write in a code span is only that of a file outside `vault/wiki/`. A page task names its page by slug, as `case-schema.md` sets it, and the hook takes pages from the `then` block. Second, the hook counts as a read a `Read` call of the file and a `Grep` call whose `path` is that file, since the Render of `rule-case-build` lets the agent read a text in parts by a Grep with lines of context. The read check of the pairs keeps its own rule, under which the output of a Grep is no read. The names search of this build, over the root with the glob `{vault/wiki,.claude,.guppi/guppi,.guppi/tests}/**`, for the names of `rule-case-build`, `rule-case-check`, both Estimates, `case-guppi`, `casecheck.py`, `tags.py`, `test_casecheck.py`, `settings.json` and `guppi casecheck`, printed 273 files. Of those, the files that bear on the change were read. `estimate-case-build-checks-the-finished-case` stands: its slug search of a page whose text turns false is another step, which stays in the Render. `estimate-case-example-passes-the-check` stands: `.guppi/tests/test_case_schema.py` runs `check_answer` with no transcript, so it gets no names note and still finds no note. `lesson-grep-glob-brace-list-of-paths-matches-nothing` stands as the record of a fact. A Grep of `a note once` over `{vault/wiki,.claude,.guppi/guppi}/**` printed `rule-case-check.md`, `estimate-case-check-notes-a-page-the-cascade-archives.md` and `estimate-case-check-notes-a-sentence-two-cases-change.md`. The two Estimates check their own notes, and the new note leaves them true. The `purpose` field of `rule-case-check` names no note and stands. `/pre-spec` gets no such note: the agent's record of calls is the hook's input alone. The `purpose` and `method` of `estimate-case-build-reads-each-list-of-a-new-member` still hold with the fixed search and stand. A Grep of `parents\[2\]|REPO|ROOT =|project_root|Path\(__file__\)` over `.guppi/tests/` printed 12 files. A Grep of `check_answer|judge_and_store|casecheck\.subagent|casecheck\.run` over `.guppi/tests/` printed `test_prespec.py`, `test_casecheck.py` and `test_case_schema.py`. Of those three, only `test_case_schema.py` is among the 12, and it runs `check_answer` over the live `case-example.md`. The session ledger holds no `error` lesson. The check of this file noted the old Criterion lines that the two replaced Criteria do not carry, and they are dropped on purpose. The old lines of `estimate-case-build-searches-the-names-of-what-it-changes` promise the agent's own search, its names, its purpose-line Grep and its search again at the check of the finished Case. That search now belongs to the hook, and the Criterion of 1005.1 holds the names. The new trigger is the note, and the exclusion of `log.md` and `brief.md` is now a line of 1005.1. The old first line of `estimate-case-build-reads-each-list-of-a-new-member` gave three folders and no form, and the fixed Grep replaces it. The note also found that a `then` line of 1005.3 shares most of its words with one of 1005.2. Both lines stand, since they speak of different texts: in 1005.3 a text that lists the members of a list, and in 1005.2 a file that a note of the hook names.

> Two steps of `case-guppi` failed though its Render held them word for word. A new reading of the same text gives little reason to pass, so each step needs a change that leaves the agent no choice of how to search.
