## Problem

The rule of the levels does not say how to judge an item that is a question or that offers several fixes, so the main agent judged two such items at levels the Criterion does not set.

## Recommend

- **How should the level be judged for an item that asks a question or offers several fixes?**
  - Recommended: (a) judge by the widest change any option of the item could make.
  - Answer: agreed, (a).
