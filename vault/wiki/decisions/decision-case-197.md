## Context

`estimate-brief-written` is the Estimate of `rule-brief`, QA, `passed`; its Criterion is the head text of case 35.3 and two blocks, 124.1 (Deferred: seven items, ranked by `severity`) and 168 (Readings due). Case 190.4 set it `pending` with "the Criterion replaced whole" and gave no text; under the `materialize` of 194.1 its Criterion would become the text of 190.4 — the two writers — and the promise of the librarian's brief itself would leave the vault. The short shape of `/carry` is the promise of `estimate-carry-over-writes` (196.1); the reference to it sits in the Render of `rule-brief` (196.3). This Case touches one Estimate, `estimate-brief-written`; it does not change `rule-brief`.

## Problem

How the promise of the librarian's brief reads as one text, without blocks and without a new contract: a recut of signed text, point 15 of the model.

## Given

The librarian writes `brief.md` by the frame — the Render of `rule-brief` — after `generate`, from the yaml of the run, `handoff.md` and the output of `make lint`.
**＋** A fact no input carries — the status of an Estimate, the `severity` of an Insight — is read from the page, and the page is named in the report.
**－** The brief of `/carry` is the promise of `estimate-carry-over-writes`, not of this one.
