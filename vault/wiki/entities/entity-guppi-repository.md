## Purpose

The repository of GUPPI on GitHub, as a thing of its own. Its rules are those of `rule-github-repository`, which names this page in `applies_to`: the rulesets on `main` and `prod`, the workflow of checks, and the approval of the runs of an outside contributor. A person who uses GUPPI keeps a repository of their own, whose rules may differ, so these rules belong to this repository and not to GUPPI as a whole (case 795.1).
