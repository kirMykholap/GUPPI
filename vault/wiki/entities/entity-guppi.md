## Purpose

GUPPI as the thing this repository builds: a Claude Code harness governed by ratified decisions, and the Obsidian vault it compiles into. Its parts the person named: the sprint as a kind (`entity-sprint`) and the glossary (`entity-glossary`), the things of the vault that are content, not rules, and the repository of GUPPI on GitHub (`entity-guppi-repository`), whose rules are its own. `rule-harness` is its guard: the tools of the harness kept equal to the pages that describe them. In a developer's vault this page stands beside the root of the product and keeps GUPPI's own machinery apart from it.
