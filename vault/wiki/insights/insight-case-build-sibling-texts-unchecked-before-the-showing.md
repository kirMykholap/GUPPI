## Pattern

Two lessons, one cause. In session 1604 one case-guppi wrote the eight sub-cases of Case 781 each against its own page, and ask-guppi at /pre-spec found sub-cases that contradicted each other. In session 2104 five case-guppi built Cases 870, 871 and 872 at the same time, each without the finished files of the others, and ask-guppi found three clashes on rule-case-build. In both, the new texts of siblings were never held side by side before the person saw them.

## Proposal

A step before the showing that reads the finished new texts of every sibling Case and sub-case of one subject together and checks each against the others. A suggestion, not a decision.
