## Pattern

lift_criterion in guppi/materialize.py knows one kind of update of an Estimate, the change of the promise: it lifts the Case's given/when/then into every update of an Estimate and sets pending, a correction of one word or a field alone included, so the Criterion the person signed is replaced by the Case's text and nothing before the librarian's run reads the then of the Case against the mechanism.

## Proposal

Case 296 (session 1315) built the cure for the mechanism: an update that names a part changes that part alone, a replace pair whose old stands inside the Criterion or a written sections.Criterion sets pending, change alone or set.status is the whole replacement; the second lesson carries addressed_by decision-case-296. What remains is a check, not code: pre-spec reads the then of an update of an Estimate against what materialize will assemble for it, and the librarian flags an update whose sections.Criterion replaces a Criterion that stood passed. A suggestion, not a decision.
