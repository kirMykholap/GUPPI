## Pattern

In session 2227 the acknowledging reply kept **Changes:**, **Notes:** and ## Frontier in English, and the lesson put the cause in the text of orchestration.md, which named the parts by English words and gave their language only at the end. Case 997 rewrote that text to say first that each heading and label is translated, ## Frontier named among them. In session 0254 the reply translated the labels and still kept ## Frontier in English, the agent taking it for a fixed term of the harness. Both failures are one pattern: an English name in a code span is copied as a literal.

## Proposal

A Case might weigh whether the text of orchestration.md shows the translated form of each heading in the language of the chat, or whether a check of the reply, such as the Stop hook that reads the LEDGER block, flags an English heading in a reply of another language; a suggestion, not a decision.
