## Pattern

Two lessons, one cause. At session 1831 the main agent dispatched case-guppi for Cases 669 and 670 at once and showed their rounds and the built Case 669 in turn, so a question of 670 stood beside the showing of 669. At session 2249 the person took five backlog items at once; the main agent dispatched five case-guppi side by side and showed each answer in its own reply while the others were out, until the person stopped it. rule-orchestration allowed Cases side by side only in the loop after the findings of /pre-spec and in a batch when a ratified Case changes; it said nothing of items taken up together, so the default of one Case per reply applied.

## Proposal

Case 684.1 makes the Frontier items that one answer of the person takes up a batch: the rounds in one list numbered by Case id, every Case shown in one reply for one ratification. Watch the reading of estimate-orchestration-signs-items-taken-together on the next batch.
