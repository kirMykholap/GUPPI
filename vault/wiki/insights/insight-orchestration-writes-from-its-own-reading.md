## Pattern

Twice in two sessions the main agent wrote what a source of record already held from its own reading of it. In session 2312 it stated that no "modified since read" refusal came, from its impression of the case-guppi summaries, before it opened a transcript. In session 0327 it showed the line of 900.5 with {supersedes:case 885}, retyped from its own reading of the defers, where the summary of case-guppi held {supersedes:case 862}; the Stop check of rule-case-check refused the reply.

## Proposal

A suggestion, not a decision — rule-orchestration could say that a ratification line of a showing is copied from the summary of its case-guppi and a fact of a subagent's tool calls is stated only after a run over its transcript, and a check could compare the lines of a showing with the summary.
