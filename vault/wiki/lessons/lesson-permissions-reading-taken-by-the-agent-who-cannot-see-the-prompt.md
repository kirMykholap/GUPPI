## Trigger

the person reports that the client's permission prompt still appears for the Edit and Write of frontier.md and spec.md in Plan Mode, allow list or not, while estimate-permissions stands passed on the agent's reading "returned without a prompt"

## Root cause

the agent cannot see the client's prompt — a call the person approved returns to it exactly as one that was not asked — so "asked or not asked" is readable by the person only, and the Result of estimate-permissions records what its reader could not observe; in Plan Mode the client asks before every edit outside the plan file, and the allow list does not lift that
