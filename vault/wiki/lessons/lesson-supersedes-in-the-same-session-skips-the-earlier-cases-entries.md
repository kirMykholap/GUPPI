## Trigger

Case 166.2 carried {supersedes:case 164}, ratified an hour earlier in the same session; ask-guppi at pre-task found that every entry of 164 — its replace, its criterion block, its remove_criterion of 124.2 — would get superseded_by and never apply, leaving block 124.2 beside block 166.2 and the rule text silent on the librarian's create

## Root cause

the Case protocol presents supersedes as the removal of the earlier Case's Criterion block, but materialize marks every entry of that Case superseded_by and generate applies none of them, so a Case that supersedes one whose text or removal work is still wanted must carry that work itself; the agent restated 164's behaviour in 166.2's then and forgot its replace and its own supersedes of 124.2 — cured by Case 171
