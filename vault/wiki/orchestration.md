# Orchestration — what the main agent does

`wiki/orchestration.md` — the Render of `rule-orchestration`, loaded by `/hi` with `sprint.md`, `backlog.md`, `brief.md`, `where-to-stop.md`, `ratification.md` and `glossary.md`, so what the main agent does is present from the first turn of a working session. The main agent builds no Case and does not load its form: the form of a Case is `case-schema.md`, read by `case-guppi` alone; the one rule, the form of the ledger lines, atomicity and the harness are `ratification.md` (case 595). The layout of the vault is [[rule-vault-layout]] and the phases of a session [[rule-session]].

**One agent response ≈ one Case.**

## Plan Mode and the Frontier

One reply shows one Case, in the form of `case-schema.md` as `case-guppi` built it, except Cases handled side by side and shown together for one ratification: in the loop after the findings of `/pre-spec` and in a batch, as below (case 598.1). That is the form of Plan Mode. In a working session the rules the harness loads come before the Plan Mode instructions of Claude Code: the main agent launches no Explore or Plan agent — `case-guppi` builds each Case and `ask-guppi` asks the vault at `/pre-spec` —, asks the person by a plain question in the chat, never by a picker tool, except where a rule names one, such as the choice between `/work` and `/carry` after the Spec is written, and calls `ExitPlanMode` only after `/pre-spec` (case 561.14). The Frontier is `raw/<date>/<hi HHMM>/frontier.md`, written at `/hi`, or, in the session that opens a sprint, before the reply that acknowledges the opening Case of `/sprint-open` (case 769.4); close its line in the reply that ends the item — `[x]` for an item ended: `· case N` when ratified, `· work` when it needed no decision; `[-]` for an item put off: `[-] … · sprint · defer` for an item of the sprint left for later — no Case but the short Case of a take-back (case 713), never `· defer case N` (case 356.2) — `[-] … · backlog · kept` for an item of `backlog.md` left as it stands, and `[-] … · defer case N` for a decision of any other source put off (case 451.1). An item of `backlog.md` that a Case defers again with a new wording closes `[-] … · backlog · defer case N`: a `defer` line of Case N names its page and carries `{supersedes:case M}` for each Case M of the page's `deferred_by`, so that `generate` drops the old defer from that list, and `/pre-spec` reports the line otherwise (as set in case 937.2). A reply after the first reply of `/hi` and before the end of Plan Mode that closes a line of `frontier.md` with no ratification and no defer — an `A` line by the person's answer, a `W` line by a reading, a `C` line that needs no decision, an item closed `[-] … · sprint · defer` or `[-] … · backlog · kept`, a line closed `[-] … · defer case N` by a Case of the conserved Spec the session takes (case 620.1) — ends as the acknowledging reply ends, and, when the line is the item of a Case that `case-guppi` built and that has no decision line in the session's ledger, comes in the turn that the answer `**Dropped:**` of that `case-guppi` opens (case 701.6): a heading `## Frontier`, the lines of `frontier.md` as a checklist in its form, and, last, the question which open item is next; when no line under `## Cases` is open, before `/pre-spec` it asks instead whether to go on to `/pre-spec`, with a `LEDGER` block or without one, and the agent calls `/pre-spec` only when the person says to go on, but for the reply that takes a conserved Spec, which goes on to `/pre-spec` with no question, as `rule-skill-hi` sets it, since the person's choice of that Spec is the word to go on (case 697.2); after `/pre-spec` it asks nothing: a reply with a `LEDGER` block ends with the Frontier, for the hook of `rule-prespec-onward` to send the agent on (case 561.6), and a reply with no `LEDGER` block goes on by itself after the Frontier, to the check and the exit or on with the loop after the findings, as `rule-prespec` sets it (case 697.2); a reply that closes several lines shows one Frontier; the first reply of `/hi` keeps the form `rule-skill-hi` sets, and a line closed in Work Mode or at Retro shows none (case 547). Plan Mode does not end while a line under `## Cases` is open — a `W` line closes when its work is done (case 356.1); `/pre-spec` checks it. Never move to the next Case until this one is ratified or deferred — but where Cases are handled side by side they go out together and come back for one ratification: in the loop after the findings of `/pre-spec`, as `rule-prespec` sets it (case 561.2), and in a batch, when the person's one answer takes up two or more items of the Frontier, or when the person's word asks that a Case already ratified change while another Case is built, or when Cases are out at the same time by any other route, as `## Showing and signing a Case` sets it (cases 598.1, 684.1, 730.1).

The session that closes a sprint follows the skill `/sprint-close` of `rule-skill-sprint-close`, and the session that opens the next sprint the skill `/sprint-open` of `rule-skill-sprint-open`, which load only in those sessions (case 743.8).

## Who builds a Case

Every Case, in Plan Mode and in Work Mode, at its first showing and at every showing again — a ratified Case that changed included — is built by the agent `case-guppi` (`rule-case-build`): the agent dispatches it with the Case id — for a new top-level Case the id that `uv run --project .guppi guppi caseid --next --session <stamp>` prints and holds, `<stamp>` the stamp of the `hi:` line of the session, but the opening Case of a sprint, whose id `/sprint-open` sets (case 788.2) —, the Frontier item and its source, and the person's words on it, and each Case of the session, ratified or already shown, that changes a page the item names, sets what happens at the same moment, such as a reply, a hook event or a command, or sets the form of a text that a check reads, or how the check reads it, where the Case of the item may write text of that kind, such as the `method` of an Estimate that `uv run --project .guppi guppi check` reads, by its id, one sentence on what it sets and which pages it changes, and the path of its case file `raw/<date>/<hi HHMM>/cases/case-<N>.md`, the main agent judging which Cases are related and `case-guppi` reading no other Case of the session (case 579.1), and, when the item is one of a batch of items the person took up in one answer, each other Case of that batch by its id and its Frontier item, and by the path of its case file where that file stands (case 684.2) — in the prompt of the call, not in the chat — and creates the plan file with its title line before its first run of `uv run --project .guppi guppi caseid --next --session <stamp>` in the session, since the command prints no id without that file. The main agent runs the command only to get the id of a new top-level Case, once for each Case, and never to check an id or what the store holds, since each run holds a new id; a number that `case-guppi` takes for a Case built again is held by the guard of its case file (as set in case 813). A question or a change of the person on a shown Case goes to the same `case-guppi` by `SendMessage`, and so do the person's answers to a round of questions, verbatim and untranslated. A request for a plain explanation — of a round, of the short text on a Case or of a reply, by `/wait-what` or in the person's words — is not such a question: the main agent answers it itself by the skill `wait-what` (`rule-skill-wait-what`), and asks the `case-guppi` of the Case by `SendMessage` only for a fact of the Case it does not hold; `case-guppi` answers with the fact alone, under a first line `**Fact:**` and with no Case heading (case 477.3). A question of the person that asks to discuss an item, or to learn something of it, while its `case-guppi` builds its Case or after it showed it, the main agent does not answer from its own reading: it sends the question to that `case-guppi` by `SendMessage`, verbatim and untranslated, and `case-guppi` answers in plain words under a first line `**Fact:**`, or with a round when the question opens a fork; the main agent shows that reply translated into Russian and asks again the question that stood open (case 561.12). The agent shows the Case `case-guppi` built at every showing by `SendUserFile` with `display: render` on `raw/<date>/<hi HHMM>/cases/case-<N>.md`, N the id of the top-level Case, the file `case-guppi` writes and the `SubagentStop` hook of `rule-case-check` checks; `case-guppi` answers with a summary of it — the path, a line `**Case:**` with the id and the name of the top-level Case as the heading of its file writes them, then for each Case and sub-case its id, its name, the Estimate that checks it, its ledger lines and, when its `context` says another Case of the session, or the Case of a Frontier item that no Case of the session has built, should change instead, a line `**Other Case:**`, and, when its Case rests on a Case built at the same time whose file it did not read, a line `**Sibling:**` that names that Case — and the main agent writes the reply from the summary without reading the file (case 645.6); in the same reply it writes the heading of the top-level Case, its id and its name in Russian translated from the `**Case:**` line of the summary, never a name of its own or the words of the Frontier item (case 662.2), and under it one list — an item per Case and sub-case of the summary, its id, its name in Russian and a short text in Russian on what done means and how it is checked, taken from the Estimate the Case creates or whose Criterion it replaces, or from the Estimate its `context` names as already checking the change — then one `ratification` section whose fence holds the ledger lines of every Case of the list as the summary holds them, never its `given`, `when`, `then` or `tasks`, then its own text after one horizontal rule; a summary in which a Case gives no such Estimate goes back to its `case-guppi` by `SendMessage` before it is shown (case 561.9); a Case whose item in the summary carries a line `**Other Case:**`, since its `context` says that another Case of the session, which it was bounded around after a clash, should change instead, is shown as it stands; in Plan Mode the main agent asks the person no question on it and sends that other Case to its `case-guppi` by `SendMessage` with the change the line names, in a batch, as `## Showing and signing a Case` sets it, after the second pass of a send of Cases built at the same time where one runs, so that a line the finished file of a sibling answers is dropped first, and the showing of the other Case built again is where the person agrees or not, and where its `case-guppi` answers with a round instead, the round stands in the list of the rounds of the batch, as any round does; in Work Mode it asks the person, in the shape of a question it puts itself, whether that other Case changes, and on the person's yes the change takes the way of Work Mode that `## Showing and signing a Case` sets (cases 629.1, 684.1, 872.3); in Plan Mode a line `**Other Case:**` that names, by its id, an item of `frontier.md` that no Case of the session has built sends no built Case back, in a batch too: the Case that carries it is shown as it stands, with no question to the person, and the main agent keeps the change the line names and puts it into the prompt that dispatches the `case-guppi` of that item when the person takes it up later, beside the Case that wrote the line as a related Case, or, when the person took that item up in the same batch, so that its `case-guppi` is already out, sends the change to that `case-guppi` by `SendMessage`; when the line of that item closes with no Case of its own — put off, kept, closed as work, or its built Case dropped — the main agent, in the turn that closes it, sends the change by `SendMessage` to the `case-guppi` of the Case that wrote the line, with no question to the person, and shows that Case again when it answers, in a batch while another Case of the session is out, the person agreeing or not at that showing; and when the Case that wrote the line is dropped or taken back before the item is taken up, the change goes to nobody (as set in case 916.1); a reply that shows a Case and that the `Stop` check of `rule-case-check` refuses — its file not sent, or a showing that differs from the checked text — is followed, before the person answers, by a reply that sends the file and shows the `ratification` as the summary holds it (case 452.4); a round or a Case shown with a text of the agent's own before it or outside its list is not refused and is not shown again: the check tells the agent of it at the person's next message, and the agent keeps the form from the next showing on (case 699.4); a reply of `case-guppi` with no first line `**Built:**`, `**Fact:**` or `**Dropped:**` is a round of questions by the skill `grill-me` (`rule-case-build`), which the agent shows translated into Russian line by line, slugs, paths and quotations unchanged, each quotation in its blockquote — the `**Context:**` paragraph that opens the first round on an item translated in place before the first question, its label in a batch naming its Case and its Frontier item as `## Showing and signing a Case` sets it (case 699.4), each question in the order and with the number the round gives it, opening with its `❓ **Qn** - **<title>**:` line and closing with its `➡️` line, its title, body and recommended answer translated in place, nothing merged, dropped or added between the questions, the agent's own text after the last question and one horizontal rule — and no Case is shown; a round may come at the first reply on an item, after a question or a change on a shown Case, and in Work Mode when a ratified Case is built again or a new Case replaces a sub-case of one, and the showing of the Case and its ratification confirm the rounds, with no step of their own. An item that needs no decision — Work, a reading again — the agent closes alone, with no dispatch. `case-guppi` asks the vault before a `create` and reads the frame itself; the agent dispatches `ask-guppi` at pre-spec, one run over the whole plan at its first run, and asks that run again only to confirm its own findings, as `rule-prespec` sets it (cases 634.3, 674.2). Outside pre-spec it dispatches `ask-guppi` only in Work Mode, where a task of a ratified Case, or the Work reading of an Estimate whose `method` a ratified Case set, names a test by hand of a hook that judges a call of `ask-guppi` apart from the same call of another agent, and only for that test, with a prompt that says the calls it asks for are a test of that hook (as set in case 954.2). A deferred decision in the short form is the agent's own, written with no dispatch (case 419.1). A fact of what a subagent of the session did — a tool call it made, a tool error it met, a hook that refused its call — the main agent states as its own only after it reads that fact in the transcript of that subagent, never from its summary alone, since a summary does not show its tool errors; what a subagent reports of its own calls the main agent may pass on with no transcript, marked as the report of that subagent, such as by the words of `ask-guppi` (as set in case 923). A question the main agent puts to the person itself — a decision it needs in the report of `/pre-spec`, a question at `/hi`, a question at a stop of Work Mode, the question in Work Mode whether another Case changes after a clash (cases 629.1, 872.3), the question at `/pre-spec` whether each finding of `ask-guppi` not closed is fixed or left, as `rule-prespec` sets it (case 634.2) — takes the question shape of `.claude/skills/grill-me/SKILL.md`: its numbered title line, the `**Now:**` line, the options under `**Options:**`, each with its `**After:**` item, and the recommended answer on the `➡️` line, in Russian, the labels translated or not; the question which Frontier item is next, the question of an open `A` line at `/hi` — the Estimate by slug and its question, which asks for a reading and not a choice —, the question that asks for the sign-off of a shown Case, the question whether to go on to `/pre-spec` when no line under `## Cases` is open (case 697.4), and the choice between `/work` and `/carry` keep their own form (case 561.13).

## Showing and signing a Case

In the chat the heading of the top-level Case stands with its name in Russian, over the list of its Cases (cases 452.4, 561.9). The agent's own text stands after the Case, after one horizontal rule, and the turn ends with a plain question in the chat, never a picker tool (case 355.1). In the chat each reply of `case-guppi` that the agent shows — a round, a `**Fact:**` answer it passes on, or a built Case with its list and its `ratification` — stands between two horizontal rules, one before it and one after it, and the agent's own text follows the second, or, in a batch, the next reply of `case-guppi`, for which that rule is the rule before it; each rule is the line `---` alone, with a blank line after it, and with a blank line before it unless it opens the reply, so that Markdown does not read the line above it as the underline of a heading; a `**Waiting:**` line, the acknowledging reply and a Case in the short form get no such rules (case 751.1). A Case the person changed, or one whose text or scope the agent finds wrong or too narrow after its ratification — in the Spec or at pre-spec — is shown again; in Work Mode a new Case replaces it, as the end of this paragraph sets it. A Case not yet ratified is sent again whole, in its file (case 452.4). When the person puts off or keeps the item of a Case that `case-guppi` built and that has no decision line in the session's ledger, or drops that Case so that the item ends another way, the agent sends the drop to the `case-guppi` of that Case by `SendMessage`, and the turn of the person's words ends with one line under the bold label `**Waiting:**`, translated, and nothing else; `case-guppi` empties the file of the Case and answers with a first line `**Dropped:**` and the path, and the agent goes on with the item only in the turn that answer opens, so that the hook of `rule-prespec-onward` finds the file empty and does not count the Case as still out; the drop adds no closer, and the line of the item closes by one of the closers of `## Plan Mode and the Frontier`, as fits what the person chose; the file of a Case with a decision line is never emptied, since it keeps the `context` and `given` that its Decision page leaves there (case 701.4); when the person drops a change to such a Case that its `case-guppi` made after the Case was last ratified, the agent sends the drop to that `case-guppi` by `SendMessage`, which undoes the change and answers with the summary of the Case in its ratified form, and the agent shows the Case again as after a change — in a batch while another Case of the session is out (case 730.1) — and the person ratifies it again, the acknowledging reply carrying the same decision lines, so that the hook of `rule-prespec-onward` counts the Case as ratified (case 705.2). When, in Plan Mode, the person takes back a ratified Case whole, or one of its sub-cases, the agent dispatches no `case-guppi` and shows in its reply a short Case of its own, in the form of `## The short form — deferred decisions`, whose `LEDGER` block holds one `defer` line for each rule, entity and insight page the Case taken back names, one for each Estimate it updates, and one for the page that each Estimate it updates checks or each Estimate it creates would check, once per page, so that each such page enters `backlog.md` through its `deferred_by` (case 723.1), each line carrying `{supersedes:case N}` for the Case or sub-case taken back, and whose `context` names the Case taken back and each Estimate that Case creates; the person's words are its ratification, the `Stop` hook of `rule-spec-write` removes the Case taken back from the plan, and its file under `cases/` stands; when no part of the Case stays in the plan, the line of its item in `frontier.md` changes in the same reply from `[x] … · case N` to the closer of its source put off — `[-] … · sprint · defer` for an item of the sprint, `[-] … · backlog · kept` for an item of `backlog.md`, and `[-] … · defer case M` of the short Case for any other source — and the reply ends as the reply that carries a defer; in Work Mode no Case is taken back, and the agent tells the person that the ratified Case stands and that a Case of a later session may replace it (case 713). A ratified Case changes through its `case-guppi`, in its prose as in its body; the agent does not edit the plan file, and the hook of `rule-spec-reconcile` refuses its edit, the title line that creates the file excepted (case 445.1). Its new file is sent again whole in Plan Mode, and ratified again, since the hook of `rule-spec-write` writes a Case only from the text the check of `rule-case-check` stored from its file after a reply that carries its lines; until then nothing of the change is acted on (cases 438, 441). In Work Mode a ratified Case of the approved Spec is never built again under its own number, whatever asks for the change — a fork that changes its contract, as the first condition of `where-to-stop.md` sets it, a change the person asks for, or a text the agent finds wrong or too narrow: the agent dispatches `case-guppi` for a new top-level Case under the id that `uv run --project .guppi guppi caseid --next --session <stamp>` prints, with the path of the file of the ratified Case, the sub-case of it that must change and the reason, and shows the new Case as any built Case for the person to ratify; the new Case holds each sub-case it replaces whole, and its decision lines carry `{supersedes:case N.M}` for each such sub-case, or `{supersedes:case N}` for a ratified Case with no sub-cases, so that the hook of `rule-spec-write` writes it into the plan file and `spec.md` and removes the sections it replaces, and `materialize` takes only the new Case; the other sub-cases stand as approved, and `/work` checks the plan before the first task of the new Case, as `rule-work-mode` sets it. A Case ratified in that Work Mode, none of whose tasks has run, changes through its `case-guppi` under its own number, as in Plan Mode. In a `/work` that `/box` calls no Case is built: a fork stops the run, as `rule-spec-box` sets it, and the choice goes to `handoff.md` as a line (case 806.1).

Russian in the chat with the owner for the name and the short text of each Case and sub-case in the list that goes with the file, a round of questions of `case-guppi`, translated, and the agent's own text; the ledger lines of `ratification` stand in the chat as the summary holds them, the words the vault will hold (cases 387.1, 441, 452.5). The main agent writes a short-form Case in English in the chat, and the hook writes it as shown (case 441).

A Case with sub-cases is shown in one reply, its file and one list under the heading of the parent, as `Who builds a Case` sets it; the person ratifies them all at once or names the ids; one reply acknowledges every Case the person ratified in one answer — a Case with its sub-cases, or the Cases of a batch or of the loop after the findings of `/pre-spec` — with one `LEDGER` block holding the decision lines of each, one heading `## Case N — ratified` naming their ids, one **Changes:** paragraph and one Frontier (case 598.2); a sub-case the person questions or changes is shown again alone when no other Case of the session is out, and otherwise in a batch (cases 355.2, 730.1). When, before `/pre-spec`, the person's answer to a round or the person's word on a shown Case asks that a Case already ratified in the session change, the agent sends that Case back to its `case-guppi` by `SendMessage` and shows neither it nor the Case being built alone: the two are a batch, and a Case that a Case of the batch asks to change in the same way joins it. The person ratifies the batch at once or names the ids; a Case the person questions or changes goes back to its `case-guppi` and is shown again, alone when no other Case of the session is out, or in a new batch with each Case it asks to change (case 598.1). When the `Stop` check of `rule-case-check` refuses a reply that shows the Cases of a batch, or of the loop after the findings of `/pre-spec`, for a finding stored with one of them, the agent sends that Case, with each Case of its file, back to its `case-guppi` by `SendMessage` with the finding, that Case leaves the batch, and the agent's answer to the refusal shows the other Cases for one ratification and ends as a showing ends, its own text after the horizontal rule naming the Case sent back as still out, since neither the `**Waiting:**` line of a batch nor the sentence below on Cases out at the same time holds for a Case out of the batch; each later turn that ends while its `case-guppi` has not answered, and while no Case shown waits for the person's word, ends with one line under the bold label `**Waiting:**`, translated, that names it, and nothing else; the Case sent back is shown when its `case-guppi` answers, as a Case the person questions or changes is shown again, and a refusal for a file not sent or for a showing that differs from the stored text sends no Case back (as set in case 890.3). ## The short form — deferred decisions

A deferred decision is its own Case in the short form — `## Case N — name`, then `context`, `problem`, and a `then` block with `defer:` carrying `slug`, `type` and `purpose` — a value that holds a `#` after a space or a tab stands in quotes, since YAML drops the rest of the line at a `#` after a space, PyYAML, the library the scripts use, refuses the whole block at a `#` after a tab, and the `Stop` hook of `rule-spec-write` refuses a block that holds either (cases 521.4, 526); no `recommend`, `given`, `when`, `then` or `ratification` section. It has three moments: the close of Plan Mode, when the person defers the items left open, the take-back of a ratified Case in Plan Mode, as `## Showing and signing a Case` sets it (case 713), and the start of `/sprint-close`, when the person's pick of the close line of the sprint stands as the words that ratify the defer of each other open item under `## Cases`, as `rule-skill-sprint-close` sets it (case 743.8); Retro runs without the person and puts nothing off — what it would put off is a line of `## For the next session` of `handoff.md` (case 531.14). The person's words asking for the defer are its ratification: in the same reply the agent shows it and carries its ledger line — when the item has a Case that `case-guppi` built and that has no decision line in the session's ledger, that reply comes in the turn that the answer `**Dropped:**` of that `case-guppi` opens, after the turn of the person's words ended with one `**Waiting:**` line (case 701.5) —, and the `Stop` hook of `rule-spec-write` writes it under the `## Deferred` heading of the plan file — the agent writes none of it (case 421.2) — then the Frontier with the line closed `[-] … · defer case N`, or, in the reply of a take-back, by the closer of its source that `## Showing and signing a Case` sets (case 723.1), and, last, the question which open item is next, with no text after it — or, when no line under `## Cases` is open, before `/pre-spec` the question whether to go on to `/pre-spec`, with no text after it, and the agent calls `/pre-spec` only when the person says to go on (case 697.3); after `/pre-spec` no question: the reply ends with the Frontier, and the hook of `rule-prespec-onward` sends the agent on with the loop after its findings (cases 561.7, 686.1); it asks nothing about the defer itself (case 461); a wording the person corrects is a new short Case with `{supersedes:case N}`, written the same way (case 405). It stands there with one item per target, so `materialize` finds it like any other Case; a defer line without its Case is marked `incomplete`. Two or more pages share one Case only when one context and one problem hold for all of them. A page-less Slice task goes to `sprint.md`, not into a defer. An item of `backlog.md` left as it stands is no decision: no short Case, no ledger line, but for the short Case of a take-back, whose `defer` lines add the short Case to the `deferred_by` of each page they name (cases 713, 723.1); its Frontier line closes `[-] … · backlog · kept`, and, with no take-back, its source stays as it is — the `deferred_by` of its page, the `open` status of its Insight, or its line in the sprint page's `## Backlog` — and the item stays in the backlog, its count grown by one (cases 421.4, 451.1). The target of a short Case enters `backlog.md` through the `deferred_by` that `generate` writes. A `defer` of an Estimate names a page that stands: a check not yet created is put off through the rule or entity page it would check, as a take-back does, and `/pre-spec` reports a `defer` of an Estimate with no page; the agent fixes that finding with a new short Case with `{supersedes:case N}` whose `defer` names the page the Estimate would check, as for a wording the person corrects (case 783). A `defer` of an Insight names a page that stands too, since only a rule or an entity gets a placeholder page, and `/pre-spec` reports one with no page; the agent fixes that finding the same way, with a new short Case with `{supersedes:case N}` whose `defer` names a page that stands (case 786). A `defer` never names a Lesson or a Decision page, since neither carries `deferred_by` and no list would show the item: the `Stop` hooks of `rule-spec-write` and `rule-ledger-write` refuse such a short Case and its line at the reply that carries them, `/pre-spec` reports it and `guppi generate` fails it, and the agent writes the defer again on the rule or entity page whose change would settle the matter (as set in case 865). How a deferred decision is taken up is `case-schema.md`. A `create`, `update` or `delete` at Retro waits for the next Plan Mode (cases 52.2, 56, 59.5).

## Writing the ledger lines

The form of each line is `ratification.md`. Write the `- decision[...]` lines only in the reply that acknowledges ratification, with each mark `{addresses:<lesson slug>}` that the summary of `case-guppi` holds on a line, and in the reply that answers the ledger hook's hold of a decision line that is not a line of the `ratification` of the Case `case-guppi` stored: that reply holds only a `LEDGER` block with the line written again as the summary of `case-guppi` holds it, or, when the stored line itself is wrong, no block, and the Case goes back to its `case-guppi` to be shown and ratified again (case 688.7); `- estimate[...]` and `- lesson[...]` lines the moment the measurement or the lesson exists, but from `/work` to the end of Retro in the one reply that ends Retro, which carries them all (case 531.8) — a reading of an Estimate that a decision line of this session creates or updates stands in that reply too, and the ledger hook refuses it before Retro opens (case 670.1) — an `estimate[...]` line opening with the date of the reading and the command or the place it was read (case 199). Without `/hi` the hook refuses and says so: tell the person, do not retry. A reply that carries a `LEDGER` block ends in that text. No tool call follows it in the same reply: the text before a tool call waits for the next `Stop` of the session, and the last reply of a session has none. When a `Stop` hook other than the ledger hook refuses a reply that carries a `LEDGER` block — exit 2 — the ledger hook has written the block at that same `Stop`, but for a decision line it holds (case 688.7): the agent's answer to the refusal is a reply of its own and may call tools (case 414.2). A block a tool call did follow is not lost until that `Stop` has passed: check the ledger then, and only a line missing there is a lesson.

## The acknowledging reply

The reply that acknowledges a ratification has four parts, in this order: the `LEDGER` block first; a heading `## Case N — ratified` and a paragraph **Changes:** — what changes, in one or two sentences, and, when the change touches a file rendered from a Rule page, that the file changes at the librarian's render after the session, so the reply claims no new behaviour of that file for the rest of the session (case 690.2); a paragraph **Notes:** — facts that did not enter the Case, omitted when there are none; a heading `## Frontier` and the lines of `frontier.md` as a checklist — the closed lines folded into one, the open `W` lines folded into one line that lists their ids, the other open ones one per line with a short title (case 578.2), the lines put off as `[-] … · sprint · defer`, `[-] … · backlog · kept` or `[-] … · defer case N` — and, last, the question which item is next, or, when no line under `## Cases` is open, before `/pre-spec` the question whether to go on to `/pre-spec`: the agent calls `/pre-spec` only when the person says to go on, and a reply that answers a question of the person instead ends with the same question again (case 697.1); after `/pre-spec`, no question: the reply ends with the Frontier, and the hook of `rule-prespec-onward` sends the agent on (case 561.8); in Work Mode the reply that acknowledges a Case ratified there ends with the Frontier too, but that hook sends nothing on once the session's `spec.md` stands, so the reply ends the turn and the run goes on at the person's next message, as `rule-work-mode` sets it (case 806.1). Headings and labels in the language of the chat (case 167, supersedes 157). The reply that acknowledges the opening Case of `/sprint-open` brings the Frontier, as `rule-skill-hi` sets it: before that reply the agent writes `frontier.md` and takes each reading that `/hi` takes; its `LEDGER` block holds the reading lines after the decision lines; its `## Frontier` shows the new Frontier; and after the lines of the Frontier and before the question which item is next it puts each open `A` line to the person, as `/hi` does (case 769.4). The reply that acknowledges a re-ratification carries the `LEDGER` block with the decision lines of each Case ratified again, as at its first ratification: the ledger hook does not append a line it already holds, and the hook of `rule-spec-write` writes a Case into the Spec only after a reply that carries its lines (case 390). A decision line that gained a mark, such as `{supersedes:case N}`, is new text: the ledger keeps it beside the first line, `generate` changes the page once and takes the marked line for a repeat, and the mark still retires the earlier decision, since the record of a Case takes the marks of all its lines, as `rule-page-generation` sets it (case 673).
