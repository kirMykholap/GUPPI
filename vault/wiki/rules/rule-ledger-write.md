## Purpose

Prevents a ratified line from existing only in a chat reply, or landing in a session no
`/hi` opened. The instruction to write `- decision[...]`, `- estimate[...]` and
`- lesson[...]` lines lives in `ratification.md` and `orchestration.md`, the Renders of `rule-ratification` and `rule-orchestration` that `/hi` loads, so a session without `/hi` produces none — and would produce them in *any* session
if the instruction stood in `CLAUDE.md`, including one with no sprint, no branch context and no brief. Without this hook's check the
ledger would silently gain a session that was never a working session, and the librarian
would materialize pages from it the next day. It also refuses, while no live page `vault/wiki/entities/entity-sprint-<id>.md` stands, every decision line of the session but a `defer`, until the session's ledger or the same reply holds a `create` decision line of an `entity-sprint-` slug, with one stderr line that names the opening of the next sprint, so that the session after the close of a sprint opens the next sprint first, as `rule-sprint-close` sets it. It refuses a fresh `create` decision line of a page `entity-sprint-<id>` when `has_page` of `.guppi/guppi/materialize.py` finds that page live or archived, with one stderr line that names the id and asks for another, and such a refused line opens no sprint. It holds a decision line other than a `defer` that is not a line of the `ratification` of the Case `case-guppi` stored for it, a `{addresses:<slug>}` mark set aside, and tells the agent once a stretch to copy it again from the summary of `case-guppi`; the line never lands while it differs, since `generate` takes the ref and the tags of a page from it. It holds once an `error` lesson line with no `{addressed_by:case N}` written after a decision line of the session, and asks the agent whether a Case of the session closed its cause: the line written again with the mark, or unchanged, is written. It never holds a `knowledge` lesson line for its mark, since such a lesson records a fact and no Case fixes its cause. It writes the decision lines of a reply that follow a lesson of the session no mark links to a Case, and asks nothing about the lesson: the mark `{addresses:<lesson slug>}` comes on a ledger line of the Case that fixed its cause, as `case-guppi` writes it and the person ratifies it, as `rule-case-build` sets it. It refuses aloud a line of a `LEDGER` block that is no record — a non-blank line that does not open with `- decision[`, `- estimate[` or `- lesson[` — so a record with a broken opening is not lost unseen. It refuses a `defer` decision line whose slug names a Lesson or a Decision page, by `defers_a_record` of `.guppi/guppi/materialize.py`, in any text of the transcript, with one stderr line when the line stands in the reply just finished, which asks for a defer of the rule or entity page whose change settles the matter, since neither page carries `deferred_by` and no list would show the item. It refuses a fresh lesson line whose `{tags:...}` holds a tag that no row of `vault/wiki/tags.md` holds and that no tag line of a Case of the session declares, a decision line of that Case standing in the session's ledger or in the same `Stop`, read by `.guppi/guppi/tags.py`, the reading the check of `rule-case-check` shares, so that an unlisted tag reaches no page through a lesson; a `{newtag:<tag>|<meaning>|<aliases>}` block of the line itself, or of a lesson line of the session's ledger or of the same `Stop`, declares the tag too, read by `parse_line` of `.guppi/guppi/materialize.py`, when the tag stands in the `{tags:...}` of its line and its meaning and aliases are not empty, so that a lesson brings its new tag with its meaning and aliases at any phase; its stderr line asks for a tag of `tags.md` or for that block; with no `tags.md` no tag is judged. It refuses a fresh reading or lesson line whose text holds a `[[name]]` outside a code span that names no live or archived page, no hub file of `vault/wiki/` and no slug that a `create` decision line of the session creates, judged by the function of `.guppi/guppi/lint.py` that `guppi check` and pre-spec share, with one stderr line for a line of the reply just finished that asks for the name in a code span or for the slug of a page, since the text of such a line reaches a page as it stands and lint reads the name as a broken link.
