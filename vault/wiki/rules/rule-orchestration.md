# Orchestration — what the main agent does

`wiki/orchestration.md` — the Render of `rule-orchestration`, loaded by `/hi` with `sprint.md`, `backlog.md`, `brief.md`, `where-to-stop.md`, `ratification.md` and `glossary.md`, so what the main agent does is present from the first turn of a working session. The main agent builds no Case and does not load its form: the form of a Case is `case-schema.md`, read by `case-guppi` alone; the one rule, the form of the ledger lines, atomicity and the harness are `ratification.md` (case 595). The layout of the vault is [[rule-vault-layout]] and the phases of a session [[rule-session]].

**One agent response ≈ one Case.**

## Plan Mode and the Frontier

One reply shows one Case, in the form of `case-schema.md` as `case-guppi` built it, except Cases handled side by side and shown together for one ratification: in the loop after the findings of `/pre-spec` and in a batch, as below (case 598.1). That is the form of Plan Mode. In a working session the rules the harness loads come before the Plan Mode instructions of Claude Code: the main agent launches no Explore or Plan agent — `case-guppi` builds each Case and `ask-guppi` asks the vault at `/pre-spec` —, asks the person by a plain question in the chat, never by a picker tool, except where a rule names one, such as the choice between `/work` and `/carry` after the Spec is written, and calls `ExitPlanMode` only after `/pre-spec` (case 561.14). The Frontier is `raw/<date>/<hi HHMM>/frontier.md`, written at `/hi`; close its line in the reply that ends the item — `[x]` for an item ended: `· case N` when ratified, `· work` when it needed no decision; `[-]` for an item put off: `[-] … · sprint · defer` for an item of the sprint left for later — no Case, never `· defer case N` (case 356.2) — `[-] … · backlog · kept` for an item of `backlog.md` left as it stands, and `[-] … · defer case N` for a decision of any other source put off (case 451.1). A reply after the first reply of `/hi` and before the end of Plan Mode that closes a line of `frontier.md` with no ratification and no defer — an `A` line by the person's answer, a `W` line by a reading, a `C` line that needs no decision, an item closed `[-] … · sprint · defer` or `[-] … · backlog · kept`, a line closed `[-] … · defer case N` by a Case of the conserved Spec the session takes (case 620.1) — ends as the acknowledging reply ends: a heading `## Frontier`, the lines of `frontier.md` as a checklist in its form, and, last, the question which open item is next; when no line under `## Cases` is open, before `/pre-spec` it asks instead whether to go on to `/pre-spec`, with a `LEDGER` block or without one, and the agent calls `/pre-spec` only when the person says to go on, but for the reply that takes a conserved Spec, which goes on to `/pre-spec` with no question, as `rule-skill-hi` sets it, since the person's choice of that Spec is the word to go on (case 697.2); after `/pre-spec` it asks nothing: a reply with a `LEDGER` block ends with the Frontier, for the hook of `rule-prespec-onward` to send the agent on (case 561.6), and a reply with no `LEDGER` block goes on by itself after the Frontier, to the check and the exit or on with the loop after the findings, as `rule-prespec` sets it (case 697.2); a reply that closes several lines shows one Frontier; the first reply of `/hi` keeps the form `rule-skill-hi` sets, and a line closed in Work Mode or at Retro shows none (case 547). Plan Mode does not end while a line under `## Cases` is open — a `W` line closes when its work is done (case 356.1); `/pre-spec` checks it. Never move to the next Case until this one is ratified or deferred — but where Cases are handled side by side they go out together and come back for one ratification: in the loop after the findings of `/pre-spec`, as `rule-prespec` sets it (case 561.2), and in a batch, when the person's one answer takes up two or more items of the Frontier, or when the person's word asks that a Case already ratified change while another Case is built, as `## Showing and signing a Case` sets it (cases 598.1, 684.1).

The session that closes a sprint is a session of its own: its Plan Mode holds only the closing Case and the Cases that prepare its readings, so that all of its decisions belong to the sprint it closes, and the closing Case holds the task line `uv run guppi sprint-close --sprint <id> --apply` with the person's choices, which the librarian runs (`rule-sprint-close`, case 628.1). Before the closing Case, the main agent sorts the Estimates that stand `pending` or `failed` into groups by the kind of moment their `method` names; the closing Case names the groups in its `context` and holds one `by hand` task per moment that Work Mode sets up and one `read` line per Estimate, and only an Estimate still unread after Work Mode is carried or archived unread (case 628.2). From the close until the first Case of the next session creates the next sprint page, `guppi sprint` and `make lint` report the missing sprint page, and that is expected (cases 628.3, 632.1).

## Who builds a Case

Every Case, in Plan Mode and in Work Mode, at its first showing and at every showing again — a ratified Case that changed included — is built by the agent `case-guppi` (`rule-case-build`): the agent dispatches it with the Case id, the Frontier item and its source, and the person's words on it, and each Case of the session, ratified or already shown, that changes a page the item names or sets what happens at the same moment, such as a reply, a hook event or a command, by its id, one sentence on what it sets and which pages it changes, and the path of its case file `raw/<date>/<hi HHMM>/cases/case-<N>.md`, the main agent judging which Cases are related and `case-guppi` reading no other Case of the session (case 579.1), and, when the item is one of a batch of items the person took up in one answer, each other Case of that batch by its id and its Frontier item, and by the path of its case file where that file stands (case 684.2) — in the prompt of the call, not in the chat — and creates the plan file with its title line before the first dispatch of the session. A question or a change of the person on a shown Case goes to the same `case-guppi` by `SendMessage`, and so do the person's answers to a round of questions, verbatim and untranslated. A request for a plain explanation — of a round, of the short text on a Case or of a reply, by `/wait-what` or in the person's words — is not such a question: the main agent answers it itself by the skill `wait-what` (`rule-skill-wait-what`), and asks the `case-guppi` of the Case by `SendMessage` only for a fact of the Case it does not hold; `case-guppi` answers with the fact alone, under a first line `**Fact:**` and with no Case heading (case 477.3). A question of the person that asks to discuss an item, or to learn something of it, while its `case-guppi` builds its Case or after it showed it, the main agent does not answer from its own reading: it sends the question to that `case-guppi` by `SendMessage`, verbatim and untranslated, and `case-guppi` answers in plain words under a first line `**Fact:**`, or with a round when the question opens a fork; the main agent shows that reply translated into {{chat_lang}} and asks again the question that stood open (case 561.12). The agent shows the Case `case-guppi` built at every showing by `SendUserFile` with `display: render` on `raw/<date>/<hi HHMM>/cases/case-<N>.md`, N the id of the top-level Case, the file `case-guppi` writes and the `SubagentStop` hook of `rule-case-check` checks; `case-guppi` answers with a summary of it — the path, a line `**Case:**` with the id and the name of the top-level Case as the heading of its file writes them, then for each Case and sub-case its id, its name, the Estimate that checks it, its ledger lines and, when its `context` says another Case of the session should change instead, a line `**Other Case:**` — and the main agent writes the reply from the summary without reading the file (case 645.6); in the same reply it writes the heading of the top-level Case, its id and its name in {{chat_lang}} translated from the `**Case:**` line of the summary, never a name of its own or the words of the Frontier item (case 662.2), and under it one list — an item per Case and sub-case of the summary, its id, its name in {{chat_lang}} and a short text in {{chat_lang}} on what done means and how it is checked, taken from the Estimate the Case creates or whose Criterion it replaces, or from the Estimate its `context` names as already checking the change — then one `ratification` section whose fence holds the ledger lines of every Case of the list as the summary holds them, never its `given`, `when`, `then` or `tasks`, then its own text after one horizontal rule; a summary in which a Case gives no such Estimate goes back to its `case-guppi` by `SendMessage` before it is shown (case 561.9); a Case whose item in the summary carries a line `**Other Case:**`, since its `context` says that another Case of the session, which it was bounded around after a clash, should change instead, is shown as it stands, and the main agent asks the person, in the shape of a question it puts itself, whether that other Case is built again, and on the person's yes sends it to its `case-guppi` by `SendMessage`, in a batch when it is ratified, as `## Showing and signing a Case` sets it (case 629.1); in a batch of items the person took up together, the question stands in the list of the batch before any Case of it is shown (case 684.1); a reply that shows a Case and that the `Stop` check of `rule-case-check` refuses — its file not sent, or a showing that differs from the checked text — is followed, before the person answers, by a reply that sends the file and shows the `ratification` as the summary holds it (case 452.4); a round or a Case shown with a text of the agent's own before it or outside its list is not refused and is not shown again: the check tells the agent of it at the person's next message, and the agent keeps the form from the next showing on (case 699.4); a reply of `case-guppi` with no first line `**Built:**` and no first line `**Fact:**` is a round of questions by the skill `grill-me` (`rule-case-build`), which the agent shows translated into {{chat_lang}} line by line, slugs, paths and quotations unchanged, each quotation in its blockquote — the `**Context:**` paragraph that opens the first round on an item translated in place before the first question, its label in a batch naming its Case and its Frontier item as `## Showing and signing a Case` sets it (case 699.4), each question in the order and with the number the round gives it, opening with its `❓ **Qn** - **<title>**:` line and closing with its `➡️` line, its title, body and recommended answer translated in place, nothing merged, dropped or added between the questions, the agent's own text after the last question and one horizontal rule — and no Case is shown; a round may come at the first reply on an item, after a question or a change on a shown Case, and in Work Mode when a ratified Case is built again, and the showing of the Case and its ratification confirm the rounds, with no step of their own. An item that needs no decision — Work, a reading again — the agent closes alone, with no dispatch. `case-guppi` asks the vault before a `create` and reads the frame itself; the agent dispatches `ask-guppi` only at pre-spec, one run over the whole plan at its first run, and asks that run again only to confirm its own findings, as `rule-prespec` sets it (cases 634.3, 674.2). A deferred decision in the short form is the agent's own, written with no dispatch (case 419.1). A question the main agent puts to the person itself — a decision it needs in the report of `/pre-spec`, a question at `/hi`, a question at a stop of Work Mode, the question whether another Case is built again after a clash (case 629.1), the question at `/pre-spec` whether each finding of `ask-guppi` not closed is fixed or left (`rule-prespec`, case 634.2) — takes the question shape of `.claude/skills/grill-me/SKILL.md`: its numbered title line, the `**Now:**` line, the options under `**Options:**`, each with its `**After:**` item, and the recommended answer on the `➡️` line, in {{chat_lang}}, the labels translated or not; the question which Frontier item is next, the question of an open `A` line at `/hi` — the Estimate by slug and its question, which asks for a reading and not a choice —, the question that asks for the sign-off of a shown Case, the question whether to go on to `/pre-spec` when no line under `## Cases` is open (case 697.4), and the choice between `/work` and `/carry` keep their own form (case 561.13).

## Showing and signing a Case

In the chat the heading of the top-level Case stands with its name in {{chat_lang}}, over the list of its Cases (cases 452.4, 561.9). The agent's own text stands after the Case, after one horizontal rule, and the turn ends with a plain question in the chat, never a picker tool (case 355.1). In the chat one horizontal rule at most, after the Case, its last sub-case or the last question of a round, and only when the agent's own text follows. A Case the person changed, or one whose text or scope the agent finds wrong or too narrow after its ratification — in the Spec, at pre-spec or in Work Mode — is shown again. A Case not yet ratified is sent again whole, in its file (case 452.4). A ratified Case changes through its `case-guppi`, in its prose as in its body; the agent does not edit the plan file, and the hook of `rule-spec-reconcile` refuses its edit, the title line that creates the file excepted (case 445.1). Its new file is sent again whole, in Plan Mode as in Work Mode, and ratified again, since the hook of `rule-spec-write` writes a Case only from the text the check of `rule-case-check` stored from its file after a reply that carries its lines; until then nothing of the change is acted on (cases 438, 441).

{{chat_lang}} in the chat with the owner for the name and the short text of each Case and sub-case in the list that goes with the file, a round of questions of `case-guppi`, translated, and the agent's own text; the ledger lines of `ratification` stand in the chat as the summary holds them, the words the vault will hold (cases 387.1, 441, 452.5). The main agent writes a short-form Case in {{documentation_lang}} in the chat, and the hook writes it as shown (case 441).

A Case with sub-cases is shown in one reply, its file and one list under the heading of the parent, as `Who builds a Case` sets it; the person ratifies them all at once or names the ids; one reply acknowledges every Case the person ratified in one answer — a Case with its sub-cases, or the Cases of a batch or of the loop after the findings of `/pre-spec` — with one `LEDGER` block holding the decision lines of each, one heading `## Case N — ratified` naming their ids, one **Changes:** paragraph and one Frontier (case 598.2); a sub-case the person questions or changes is shown again alone (case 355.2). When, before `/pre-spec`, the person's answer to a round or the person's word on a shown Case asks that a Case already ratified in the session change, the agent sends that Case back to its `case-guppi` by `SendMessage` and shows neither it nor the Case being built alone: the two are a batch, and a Case that a Case of the batch asks to change in the same way joins it. When, before `/pre-spec`, the person's one answer takes up two or more items of the Frontier, the agent dispatches a `case-guppi` for each of them and shows no round and no Case of them alone: they are a batch too. A question whether another Case is built again, which the line `**Other Case:**` of a summary of that batch raises, stands in the list of its rounds, numbered `<Case id>.Q<n>` under the Case that raised it, before any Case of the batch is shown, and on the person's yes that other Case goes back to its `case-guppi` and joins the batch (case 684.1). The batch takes the steps of the loop of `rule-prespec`: while a `case-guppi` of the batch has not replied, each turn ends with one line under the bold label `**Waiting:**`, translated, naming the Cases that have answered and those still out, and nothing else; one list shows every round that came back, each question numbered `<Case id>.Q<n>` and the `**Context:**` label of each first round naming its Case and its Frontier item, translated, such as `**Context — Case 7 (C2):**`, with no heading or other line of the agent's own to mark where a round begins (case 699.5); each answer goes to the `case-guppi` of its Case, all in one message; and every Case of the batch is shown in one reply, each in the showing `## Who builds a Case` sets, for one ratification. The person ratifies the batch at once or names the ids; a Case the person questions or changes goes back to its `case-guppi` and is shown again, alone or in a new batch with each Case it asks to change (case 598.1).

Work Mode ticks the boxes on code, tests, migrations and `by hand` checks in `raw/<date>/<hi HHMM>/spec.md`, the session's folder (case 73), in the reply that finishes the task; `generate` ticks the page lines and the `read` lines of an Estimate that a reading in the ledger, a `delete` or an `update` that sets it `passed` or `failed` by the person's decision closes (case 672), `render`, `sprint` and `glossary` the render, sprint and glossary lines (cases 226, 250, 270, 306), and `guppi sprint-close`, which the librarian runs, its own line (case 628.1); Retro counts the unticked (case 59.3).

## The short form — deferred decisions

A deferred decision is its own Case in the short form — `## Case N — name`, then `context`, `problem`, and a `then` block with `defer:` carrying `slug`, `type` and `purpose` — a value that holds a `#` after a space or a tab stands in quotes, since YAML drops the rest of the line at a `#` after a space, PyYAML, the library the scripts use, refuses the whole block at a `#` after a tab, and the `Stop` hook of `rule-spec-write` refuses a block that holds either (cases 521.4, 526); no `recommend`, `given`, `when`, `then` or `ratification` section. It has one moment: the close of Plan Mode, when the person defers the items left open; Retro runs without the person and puts nothing off — what it would put off is a line of `## For the next session` of `handoff.md` (case 531.14). The person's words asking for the defer are its ratification: in the same reply the agent shows it and carries its ledger line, and the `Stop` hook of `rule-spec-write` writes it under the `## Deferred` heading of the plan file — the agent writes none of it (case 421.2) — then the Frontier with the line closed `[-] … · defer case N` and, last, the question which open item is next, with no text after it — or, when no line under `## Cases` is open, before `/pre-spec` the question whether to go on to `/pre-spec`, with no text after it, and the agent calls `/pre-spec` only when the person says to go on (case 697.3); after `/pre-spec` no question: the reply ends with the Frontier, and the hook of `rule-prespec-onward` sends the agent on with the loop after its findings (cases 561.7, 686.1); it asks nothing about the defer itself (case 461); a wording the person corrects is a new short Case with `{supersedes:case N}`, written the same way (case 405). It stands there with one item per target, so `materialize` finds it like any other Case; a defer line without its Case is marked `incomplete`. Two or more pages share one Case only when one context and one problem hold for all of them. A page-less Slice task goes to `sprint.md`, not into a defer. An item of `backlog.md` left as it stands is no decision: no short Case, no ledger line; its Frontier line closes `[-] … · backlog · kept`, its source stays as it is — the `deferred_by` of its page, the `open` status of its Insight, or its line in the sprint page's `## Backlog` — and the item stays in the backlog, its count grown by one (cases 421.4, 451.1). The target of a short Case enters `backlog.md` through the `deferred_by` that `generate` writes. How a deferred decision is taken up is `case-schema.md`. A `create`, `update` or `delete` at Retro waits for the next Plan Mode (cases 52.2, 56, 59.5).

## Writing the ledger lines

The form of each line is `ratification.md`. Write the `- decision[...]` lines only in the reply that acknowledges ratification, in the reply that answers the ledger hook's reminder of a lesson with no mark, which, when a Case of the reply fixed the cause, holds only a `LEDGER` block with one decision line of that Case or sub-case written again with `{addresses:<lesson slug>}`, and otherwise no block (case 685.1); and in the reply that answers the ledger hook's hold of a decision line that is not a line of the `ratification` of the Case `case-guppi` stored: that reply holds only a `LEDGER` block with the line written again as the summary of `case-guppi` holds it, or, when the stored line itself is wrong, no block, and the Case goes back to its `case-guppi` to be shown and ratified again (case 688.7); `- estimate[...]` and `- lesson[...]` lines the moment the measurement or the lesson exists, but from `/work` to the end of Retro in the one reply that ends Retro, which carries them all (case 531.8) — a reading of an Estimate that a decision line of this session creates or updates stands in that reply too, and the ledger hook refuses it before Retro opens (case 670.1) — an `estimate[...]` line opening with the date of the reading and the command or the place it was read (case 199). Without `/hi` the hook refuses and says so: tell the person, do not retry. A reply that carries a `LEDGER` block ends in that text. No tool call follows it in the same reply: the text before a tool call waits for the next `Stop` of the session, and the last reply of a session has none. When a `Stop` hook other than the ledger hook refuses a reply that carries a `LEDGER` block — exit 2 — the ledger hook has written the block at that same `Stop`, but for a decision line it holds (case 688.7): the agent's answer to the refusal is a reply of its own and may call tools (case 414.2). A block a tool call did follow is not lost until that `Stop` has passed: check the ledger then, and only a line missing there is a lesson.

## The acknowledging reply

The reply that acknowledges a ratification has four parts, in this order: the `LEDGER` block first; a heading `## Case N — ratified` and a paragraph **Changes:** — what changes, in one or two sentences, and, when the change touches a file rendered from a Rule page, that the file changes at the librarian's render after the session, so the reply claims no new behaviour of that file for the rest of the session (case 690.2); a paragraph **Notes:** — facts that did not enter the Case, omitted when there are none; a heading `## Frontier` and the lines of `frontier.md` as a checklist — the closed lines folded into one, the open `W` lines folded into one line that lists their ids, the other open ones one per line with a short title (case 578.2), the lines put off as `[-] … · sprint · defer`, `[-] … · backlog · kept` or `[-] … · defer case N` — and, last, the question which item is next, or, when no line under `## Cases` is open, before `/pre-spec` the question whether to go on to `/pre-spec`: the agent calls `/pre-spec` only when the person says to go on, and a reply that answers a question of the person instead ends with the same question again (case 697.1); after `/pre-spec`, no question: the reply ends with the Frontier, and the hook of `rule-prespec-onward` sends the agent on (case 561.8). Headings and labels in the language of the chat (case 167, supersedes 157). The reply that acknowledges a re-ratification carries the `LEDGER` block with the decision lines of each Case ratified again, as at its first ratification: the ledger hook does not append a line it already holds, and the hook of `rule-spec-write` writes a Case into the Spec only after a reply that carries its lines (case 390). A decision line that gained a mark, such as `{supersedes:case N}`, is new text: the ledger keeps it beside the first line, `generate` changes the page once and takes the marked line for a repeat, and the mark still retires the earlier decision, since the record of a Case takes the marks of all its lines (`rule-page-generation`, case 673).
