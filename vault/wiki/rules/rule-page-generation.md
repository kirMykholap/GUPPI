## Purpose

Prevents a ratified decision from ending the session without a page, or a page from
carrying a field nobody ratified. The script is the only writer to `wiki/` but for what a merge of `main` into a branch writes: the joined text of a page that both a branch and `main` changed, which a Case signs and Work Mode writes, the script then writing the History line and the status that the Case sets, and the lines of both sides of `log.md` and `tags.md`, which git joins by their `merge=union` lines in `.gitattributes`. The header of `tags.md` above its table is not written by the script: a one-off task by hand that a ratified Case names changes it in Work Mode. It lays
the content of `materialize.yaml` into schema fields and invents nothing: what it writes beyond that content is computed from the pages, the Specs and the ledgers of `raw/` by the steps this page names — the cascade, the ticks of the Spec, the links of a `rename`, the words of a `reword`; no section is derived from a ref field, no Estimate from a flag, and the Criterion of an
Estimate is replaced whole by the text its Case signed. A page is always created, even incomplete: red lint is an accurate signal of debt, a decision with no page is the ledger and the vault disagreeing silently. An exception is a `defer` with no page of anything but a rule or an entity: it writes no page and fails as an entry. A check that was never created has no reading to put off, and the short Case puts off the page it would check instead; an Insight is made only by the librarian, and the format of an Insight, a Lesson or a Decision page has no status for a stand-in page. A `defer` of a Lesson or a Decision page writes nothing and fails as an entry too, whether the page stands or not, since neither format has `deferred_by` and no list would show the item. A slug
collision is an error, never a rename — with one exception: a `create` whose slug is a
placeholder page, `status: draft` and no `kind`, fills that page. An `update` whose item carries `rename: <new slug>` moves the page to the new slug, the same category prefix, and rewrites every link and ref field to it in the live pages of `wiki/`, Decision pages included; a taken or foreign slug fails the entry. An `update` whose item carries `reword` — a list of `{old, new}` pairs — replaces each pair as a whole word in every section but History and Result, and in `purpose` and `method`, of every live rule, entity and Estimate of `wiki/`, one History line per page touched; the page is the owner, and a `new` absent from its own text, or a pair with no hit, fails the entry; a slug goes through `rename`, not `reword`. A pair whose `old` ends with `/`, such as `docs/`, is the start of a path: it matches wherever no letter, digit, `_`, `-`, `.` or `/` stands right before it, whatever follows it, and it is replaced in the link fields `implementation_ref`, `governs` and `code_ref` too. An `update` whose item carries `drop`, a list of section names, removes each named section, its heading and its text; a name the page does not carry, or a section that the schema of the page's category requires of the page, fails the entry, and nothing of the item is written to the page. One bad
entry never stops the run: it is marked `failed`, reported, and the rest apply. An entry whose Case, kind and slug match an entry of the same yaml that already applied is marked applied, changes no page and ticks no line of the Spec, so a Case approved again in its session, its lines written again with a mark added, changes each page once. The Decision page of a Case is written before its entries and carries the body of every defer, and `## Recommend`, the `recommend` of the Case; a Case with sub-cases gets a page of its own before theirs, with `## Problem`, `## Recommend` and `children` and never `## Context`, and the page of each sub-case carries `parent` and no `## Problem`; the page of a Case superseded in its own session and gone from the Spec — its record carries `superseded_by` and no `case_text` — has a two-line body naming the superseding Decision; a Case whose record carries `superseded_by`, retired by a later Case before its entries applied, gets its Decision page `superseded` at once, naming in `superseded_by` each Decision its record names, with one History line for each, whether or not another Case retired that later Case in turn; it still lists its own `supersedes`, but that `supersedes` changes no other page and writes no line of History or of `log.md`; a page that a later Case supersedes takes the pages of its `children` with it, at any depth: each that stands `current` becomes `superseded` and names the later Decision in `superseded_by`, an archived or superseded one is left as it is, and no `deferred_by` is cleared through them; a page that a later Case supersedes while it stands `archived` stays `archived` where it lies: it takes the later Decision into its `superseded_by`, with one History line, and the `deferred_by` the later Case clears is cleared as for any page, so `cleanup` does not bring it back; the cascade after all entries computes what is computed — `built`, the archive of a superseded decision, the severity of an Insight, the `rules` of an Entity from the `applies_to` of the live rules — never by hand; a superseded decision that a live page other than a Decision page names in `deferred_by` or `addressed_by` stays `superseded` under `wiki/decisions/` until no such page names it, by the judgment with which `guppi sprint-close` keeps a Decision page, so that no live page names an archived Decision. The script moves no line of the sprint page: a milestone item leaves its milestone only by a Case, as `rule-sprint` sets it. Once an entry is applied, the script ticks its line in the Spec — a page entry the line `- [ ] <kind> `<slug>`` in the section of the entry's Case of the Spec beside the yaml; an `estimate` entry, a `create` with a folded reading, a `delete`, whose Estimate no reading will ever reach, or an `update` that sets an Estimate `passed` or `failed` by the person's decision, which no reading follows, every unticked `- [ ] read `<slug>`` line of every live Spec of `raw/` — one reading is of the page, whose Criterion is one, however many Cases and however many sessions touched it — in the Spec beside the yaml first — skipping the section of a Case whose first decision entry stands after the reading, the `delete` or the `update` in the ledger, a Case ratified after it — and then in the `spec.md` of the other session folders of `raw/`, oldest first, skipping in every Spec the section of a Case whose entries the yaml beside it marks `superseded_by`, its work standing under the superseding Case — so the Spec's page lines and reading lines are closed by the one who did the work, and a line not found is no error. The mechanism — the shape of every entry and every History line — lives in the docstring of `.guppi/guppi/generate.py`.
